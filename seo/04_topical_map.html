How priority was scored

Deterministic, no LLM judgement. targetable_volume = total US volume minus unwinnable head-term volume.

score = 10 · log10(targetable_volume + 10) + format_fit +12 game · +8 programmatic template · +2 glossary · 0 guide/blog/hub + winnability +8 if ≥3 mid-size competitors rank · +4 if exactly 2 + existing_page +10 exists · +5 partial − aio_penalty −9 high · −4 med · 0 low (measured where the SERP was probed, else inferred: guide→high, game→low) − head_penalty −18 · (unwinnable_head_volume / total_volume) Programmatic = the 8 templates · Later/Skip = head share >50% or targetable <400 P1 = top 20 by score + 5 best support guides + the 2 existing hubs · P2 = next 40 · P3 = rest
