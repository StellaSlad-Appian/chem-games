**Purpose:** the cross-state comparison behind the Australian curriculum profiles in
[`../ORGANIC_GAMES_FRAMEWORK.md`](../ORGANIC_GAMES_FRAMEWORK.md). It plays the role that
[`germany-sek2-overview.md`](./germany-sek2-overview.md) plays for the German Länder.

written by desk research from primary sources.

"(unverified)" marks; this overview does not repeat them all, so check the state document before

## What this means for chem-games (summary)

- **Years 7–10 lay no organic or energetics foundation.** The national v9 curriculum has no
  organic chemistry, no energy diagrams, no ΔH and no collision theory. Year 10 has only the
  factors that affect rate. Victoria (exo/endo named at Levels 9–10) and NSW (alkanes C1–C8 and
  combustion in Stage 5) go slightly further. **Every organic and energetics game is a Year 11–12
  (`Senior`) game in Australia.** The one exception is a light Year 10 "rates" level.
- **The year differs by state, so tag by unit, not by year** (as in Germany):
  - Kinetics and energetics are **Year 11** in NSW, Queensland, WA and ACT.
  - They are **Year 12** in Victoria (Unit 3), SA/NT (Stage 2) and Tasmania.
  - Organic chemistry is **mostly Year 12** everywhere. WA, SA/NT (if the teacher selects the
    topic) and Tasmania (in Physical Sciences) start naming in Year 11.
- **Australian thermodynamics is shallow compared with Germany:**
  - **Hess's law** is core only in NSW (until the 2028 HSC) and Tasmania; in Queensland it is a
    suggested investigation.
  - **Formation enthalpies** appear nowhere as a clear dot point.
  - **Entropy and Gibbs energy** are only in NSW, and NSW removes them in its 2025 syllabus
    (first taught 2028).
  - What Australia has instead is **fuels and energy content** (kJ g⁻¹, kJ mL⁻¹; everywhere) and
    **food energy** (Victoria).
- **Australian organic chemistry is wider but has no mechanisms.** Every state covers reaction
  pathways, yield, polymers and biomolecules; most cover spectroscopy. None requires curly
  arrows. Mechanisms (framework G12) are a German-only game.
- **Maxwell–Boltzmann is uneven:**
  - core in Queensland and Tasmania (unnamed in Tasmania)
  - examined but not named in Victoria
  - absent in NSW, WA, SA/NT and ACT
- **Rate laws, reaction order and Arrhenius are absent everywhere**, as in Germany.
- **English naming is uniform in style but not in scope:**
  - Every state uses locant-before-suffix names (*propan-1-ol*, *butan-2-one*), the same
    style as Bavaria's German.
  - Geometric isomers are **cis/trans**, not E/Z.
  - Which families are *named* (not just drawn) differs by state; see §3.
- **The data booklets are part of the game design.** Several printed data sheets are exactly the
  "reference card" a game can show: Queensland's reaction-pathway map and bond-enthalpy table,
  Tasmania's organic flowchart and IR bands, and the NMR tables in Victoria and NSW.

## 3. The Australian safe core (and what is not core)

**Core in every jurisdiction (senior):**
- **Kinetics:** K2 factors, K3 collision theory, K4 energy profiles with Ea and ΔH, K5 catalysts
  (lower-Ea pathway, including enzymes), K6 rate experiments.
- **Energetics:**
  - T1 exo/endo with ΔH negative for exothermic
  - T7 energy-profile diagrams
  - thermochemical equations
  - bond breaking absorbs and bond forming releases energy (qualitative T8)
  - **fuels compared by energy output** (T11)
- **Equilibrium:** G1 dynamic equilibrium in a closed system, G3 Le Chatelier, writing K
  expressions (G2 at least qualitatively).
- **Organic:**
  - homologous families
  - naming alkanes/alkenes up to C8
  - alcohols and carboxylic acids
  - esterification
  - alcohol oxidation
  - addition reactions
  - addition and condensation polymers
  - percentage yield (everywhere except SA/NT)

**Core in most, but not all:**
- quantitative calorimetry (not WA)
- mean rate from data (partial in VIC and NSW)
- cis/trans isomers (VIC, QLD, WA, NSW from 2028)
- reaction-pathway flow charts (all except SA/NT as organic maps)
- IR and mass spectrometry (VIC, NSW, QLD, TAS, ACT)
- quantitative Kc (VIC, NSW, QLD, SA/NT, TAS)

**In one or two jurisdictions only (tag them, never assume them):**

| Skill | Where |
|---|---|
| Hess's law | NSW (until the 2028 HSC), Tasmania |
| Entropy and Gibbs | NSW (until the 2028 HSC) |
| Maxwell–Boltzmann | Queensland, Tasmania (Victoria: examined) |
| NMR | Victoria, NSW |
| Chirality / optical isomers | Victoria, Queensland |
| Markovnikov, elimination | Queensland |
| Food energy | Victoria |
| Tollens' reagent | SA/NT |
| Atom economy (examined) | Victoria |
| Haloalkanes | absent in WA, SA/NT, ACT |

**Not in any Australian course:** curly-arrow mechanisms, rate laws, reaction order, Arrhenius,
E/Z notation, Fehling's solution, Schiff's reagent and 2,4-DNP.

## 5. Conventions and notation across states

| Convention | Practice |
|---|---|
| Alcohol names | *propan-1-ol*, *butan-2-ol* everywhere. Victoria also accepts *1-propanol*. Accept both, show the locant-before-suffix form. |
| Ester names | *ethyl ethanoate*, *propyl butanoate* (alkyl alkanoate). NSW 2017 does not list esters for naming, but they appear in HSC papers. SA/NT limits esters to methyl and ethyl. |
| Geometric isomers | **cis/trans** (VIC, QLD, WA). E/Z is not used anywhere. |
| Oxidising agents | "acidified potassium dichromate" and "acidified permanganate"; Queensland says "manganate(VII)" and "dichromate(VI)". Colour changes: orange → green; purple → colourless. |
| Heat symbol | *q* (VIC, NSW, QLD), **Q** (SA/NT), **E** (Tasmania). All use = mcΔT. |
| Units | kJ mol⁻¹; fuels in kJ g⁻¹ and kJ mL⁻¹. NSW gives *c*(water) in **J kg⁻¹ K⁻¹**, so mass goes in kg. **Kc is written with units** in Victoria and Tasmania. |
| Molar volume | Victoria uses *V*m = 24.8 L mol⁻¹ at SLC (25 °C, 100 kPa). |
| Spelling | Australian English: sulfur, aluminium, Le Chatelier (Queensland and WA print *Le Châtelier*). |

## 6. Consequences for the framework

1. **Curriculum ids.** Extend `CurriculumId` (framework OF1) with `au-ac9`, `au-vic`, `au-nsw`,
   `au-qld`, `au-wa`, `au-sace`, `au-tas` and `au-act`, replacing the single `au-vce`.
   - Anchors use the local unit names: `U3 AoS 2`, `M4`, `U2 T3`, `S2 2.1`, `C6`.
   - NSW needs **two** anchor sets, one for 2017 (until the 2028 HSC) and one for 2025 (from
     2028), because Hess, entropy and Gibbs disappear.
2. **Game catalogue fit.**

| Framework game | Australian fit |
|---|---|
| G1 Chain Namer | Strong everywhere. The answer key differs by state: naming amides (NSW, WA, TAS only), haloalkanes (not WA, SA/NT, ACT), maximum chain C8 vs C10. |
| G2 Isomer Hunt | Strong. Add cis/trans rounds for VIC, QLD, WA. |
| G3 Boiling Point Ladder | Strong everywhere (O5 is universal core). |
| G4 Reagent Bench | Good, with the **Australian test set**: bromine water, acidified dichromate/permanganate, carbonate, pH; Tollens only for SA/NT. Hide Fehling, Schiff and DNPH on Australian profiles. |
| G5 Synthesis Router | Strong (VIC, NSW, QLD, WA, TAS, ACT). Needs per-state edge sets: the VCE findings in [`australia-victoria.md`](./australia-victoria.md) §8 (amide formation, dehydration and Markovnikov out; transesterification in) differ from Queensland (Markovnikov and elimination in) and SA/NT (no substitution). |
| G6 Energy Hill | Strong everywhere (K4, T7 universal). |
| G7 Collision Lab | Strong everywhere. The Maxwell–Boltzmann view must be profile-controlled (on for QLD, TAS, VIC; off elsewhere). |
| G8 Hess Puzzle | **Weak in Australia**: NSW until 2028 and Tasmania only. Treat it as a German-first game. |
| G9 Calorimeter | **Strong in Australia**; widen it. Add a **fuels mode** (kJ g⁻¹, kJ mL⁻¹, complete vs incomplete combustion; universal), a **calibration-factor mode** (VIC, TAS) and a **food-energy mode** (VIC). This may justify moving G9 earlier for Australian students. |
| G10 Spontaneity Quadrant | NSW only, until 2028. German / eA game. |
| G11 Equilibrium Lab | Strong everywhere. Kc calculations off for WA and ACT (qualitative only). |
| G12 Arrow Pusher | Not Australian. |

3. **A spectroscopy game.** IR and mass spectrometry are core in five jurisdictions, and NMR in
   the two largest (VIC, NSW). The "Spectrum Detective" option in
   [`../game-briefs/functional-groups.md`](../game-briefs/functional-groups.md) is better
   supported by Australian curricula than several framework games are. Consider adding it to the
   catalogue as an Australia-led game.
4. **Existing VCE briefs need corrections.** They are listed in [`australia-victoria.md`](./australia-victoria.md) §8:
   - Synthesis Router: the open questions are answered; add transesterification.
   - Stoichiometry: moles are Unit 1, not Unit 2.
   - Reaction Balancer: the curriculum "quote" is not the real wording.
   - Ion Forge: Levels 4–5 are enrichment for VCE.
5. **Bond-enthalpy data.** Victoria removed the tables from its 2026 Data Book. The framework's
   thermochemical dataset (OF5) should not treat any single state's booklet as the source.

## 7. Open points

- Every per-state document lists its own unverified items. The most consequential:

  - the Victorian study design's end date
  - whether Victoria still examines bond-enthalpy calculations after the Data Book change

  whether skeletal formulas are accepted where the syllabus does not name them). A teacher in
  each state should review before the answer keys are finalised.
- NSW 2028: the answer keys and anchors for the new syllabus should be ready for Year 11 in
  Term 1 2028.
