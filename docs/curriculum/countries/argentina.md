# Chemistry curriculum: Argentina (Years 7–12)

**Purpose:** input for the cross-country concept/year map for chem-games.
**Research date:** 24 Sept 2026 (desk research by an agent).
**Review status:** not yet checked by a teacher who teaches in Argentina.
**Key sources:** federal NAP for Ciencias Naturales (CFCyE/CFE 2004–2012) [S1–S3]; Provincia de Buenos Aires (PBA) DGCyE *diseños curriculares* 2006–2011 [S4–S10]; CABA *Nueva Escuela Secundaria* (NES) 2015 [S16–S17]; full list in section 6.

## Summary for game design

- **There is no single national syllabus.** The federal *Núcleos de Aprendizajes Prioritarios* (NAP, "priority learning cores") set a short common floor. Each of the 24 jurisdictions writes its own *diseño curricular*. This file uses **Provincia de Buenos Aires (PBA)** as the typical case and contrasts it with **CABA** (City of Buenos Aires).
- **Chemistry starts inside integrated science and "Fisicoquímica".** In PBA, Year 7 (1º ES) has integrated *Ciencias Naturales* (materials, mixtures, water). Years 8–9 (2º–3º ES) have *Fisicoquímica*, a combined physics and chemistry subject of 2 periods a week. It covers the particle model, gas laws, solutions and % concentration, a simple atom, the periodic table, then Bohr-style atoms, Lewis structures, VSEPR (*TRePEV*), binary nomenclature, balancing by inspection, redox by oxidation number, endo/exothermic reactions and nuclear reactions.
- **The mole comes late, in Year 10 or 11, and only for some students.** PBA's Year 9 design says outright "no mole, no stoichiometric calculations". Amount of substance, molar mass, limiting reagent, yield and purity, the ideal gas equation, molarity and Kc/Le Chatelier are all in *Introducción a la Química*. That subject is **Year 10 for the Ciencias Naturales orientation and Year 11 for every other orientation**.
- **The big track split is the *orientación*.** Only PBA's Bachiller en Ciencias Naturales goes further: Year 11 *Fundamentos de Química* covers colligative properties, Brønsted–Lowry acids and bases, pH, Ka/Kb, buffers, Ksp, electrochemistry, Faraday's laws and kinetics. Year 12 *Química del Carbono* covers orbitals, hybridisation, organic reactions, biomolecules and polymers. In other PBA orientations, the only chemistry after Year 9 is 2 h/week in Year 11.
- **CABA has a gap.** Its NES *ciclo básico* (Years 8–9) has **Biología but no physics or chemistry subject**. Chemistry returns in Year 10 (*Físico-Química*, 4 h) and in *Química* (4 h, Year 12 in the 2015 design). Games aimed at "13–14-year-olds in Argentina" cannot assume any chemistry for CABA students.
- **Context first, not formula drills.** Both the NAP and PBA explicitly reject "formuleo y nomenclatura" (naming drills) as an end in itself [S3 fn. 10, S6]. Topics hang on contexts: petroleum and fuels, food, the Haber process, water, batteries, the environment. Games that wrap chemistry in a real context fit the curriculum better than naming drills do.
- **Notation games must respect local conventions.** These include the decimal comma (ΔEn = 2,1 in PBA's bonding rule [S6]), *número de oxidación* rather than *valencia* [S6], and *uniones químicas* for "chemical bonds". Concentration is written %m/m, %m/V, %V/V, M and molal. Spanish names such as *solvente*, *nafta* and *TRePEV* apply, and so does IUPAC naming, although CABA still presents traditional and "modern" (IUPAC) names side by side [S17].
- **Surprises:**
  - Gas laws (Boyle, Charles/Gay-Lussac, PV = nRT in qualitative form) come very early, in Year 8 (PBA 2º).
  - Nuclear fission and fusion are in Year 9 (NAP and PBA 3º).
  - Biomolecules (Haworth projections, fatty acids ω-3, soaps and micelles) appear in the first real chemistry course of the upper cycle.
- **Stability:** the documents in force date from 2006–2015. PBA has started a redesign, but it did not apply in March 2026. A national bill to replace the 2006 Education Law is pending. The *content* is stable, and the *structure* is starting to move (see section 1a).

## 1. System and year mapping

Argentina has 13 compulsory years: the final year of *nivel inicial*, then primary plus secondary. Under the Ley de Educación Nacional 26.206 (LEN, 2006), jurisdictions choose between **6 + 6** (PBA and most provinces) and **7 + 5** (CABA and some others) [S1 fn. 7, S2]. Children normally start *1º grado* the year they turn 6 (unverified cut-off rule). PBA technical and agrarian secondary schools last **7 years** [S15], so Year 13 exists for them. It is not covered here.

- In PBA a *módulo* is believed to be 60 minutes (unverified). A CABA *hora cátedra* is conventionally 40 minutes (unverified).
- PBA Year 10 for non-Cs. Nat. orientations has no chemistry subject. Year 11 *Introducción a la Química* is common to every orientation and is placed in "4º año of the Ciencias Naturales orientation and 5º año in the rest" [S7].
- **CABA placement of Química:** the NES 2015 structure puts *Química (4 h)* in 5º año (Year 12) [S17], and a 2018 school plan matches it [S19]. A 2026 CABA plan approved under the *Secundaria Aprende* policy lists *Física 3* and *Química 4* in **4º año (Year 11)** [S18]. The CABA resource page files Química under "4º y 5º año" [S27]. Treat CABA chemistry as **Year 11 or 12 depending on the school plan** (unverified which is now the default).

**Reforms in force or being phased in for 2026**

- **National.** The science NAP have not been revised since 2012 (ciclo orientado, Res. CFE 180/12 [S3]). The CFE's 2024–2026 work has focused on literacy: *Compromiso Federal por la Alfabetización*, Res. CFE 471/24, and follow-ups 485/24, 493/25 and 506/25 [S22]. It has not touched science content. I found no 2024–2026 CFE resolution that changes secondary science (the absence is unverified). The *Secundaria Federal 2030* / MOA framework (Res. CFE 330/17) adds cross-cutting "capacidades" and integrated learning, not new chemistry content [S24].
- **National bill, not in force.** The *Ley de Libertad Educativa* draft (Nov–Dec 2025) would repeal LEN 26.206. As reported, it would cap common minimum content at 75% of school time and leave 25% to school autonomy [S25]. I could not confirm any parliamentary approval by Sept 2026 (unverified).
- **PBA.**
  - The new *Régimen Académico* (Res. 1650/24) applies from 2025. It covers passing subject by subject, a pass mark of 7, and four-month intensification periods [S12]. It changes assessment, not content.
  - New secondary *diseños curriculares* are "being prepared" [S13]. Institutional consultation days were held on 3 Dec 2025. DGCyE told the teachers' union that the new design "no será de aplicación en marzo de 2026" ("will not apply in March 2026") [S14]. **The 2006–2011 designs are therefore still in force in school year 2026.** A Nov 2025 DGCyE document still cites the "diseños curriculares provinciales vigentes" (current provincial curriculum designs).
- **CABA.**
  - New primary design (Res. 2271/MEDGC/24) in force since 2025. It affects Year 7 [S20].
  - *Secundaria Aprende* (Res. 2339/MEDGC/24) regroups subjects into fields and interdisciplinary spaces. It ran in 33 schools in 2025 and adds 90 in 2026. It keeps the NES designs as the content reference [S18, S21].
- **Other provinces (example).** Córdoba rolls out a new *ciclo básico* design and a new general-education design for the *ciclo orientado* in all schools from 2026. Its pilot schools regroup Ciencias Naturales as an area [S26].

**National assessments that shape content:** *Aprender* (Secretaría de Educación). The most recent secondary round was in 2024, in the final year, and tested only Lengua and Matemática [S23]. Science is not currently assessed nationally, so assessment puts almost no pressure on chemistry content. Argentina takes part in PISA (unverified for the 2025 cycle). There is no national school-leaving chemistry exam.

**Typical interval.** The two big federal science moments were 2004–2006 and 2011–2012. **Federal chemistry content has not changed for 14 years.** PBA designs usually last 15 years or more (2006/2011 to at least 2026). CABA's NES design has lasted about 11 years so far. LEN art. 85 and PBA Law 13.688 art. 106 require "periodic" review but set **no fixed cycle** [S15]. In practice revisions are **ad hoc and driven by politics and reform**: new national laws (1993, 2006, possibly 2026–27) or new jurisdictional governments (CABA 2014, PBA 2024–25).

**Smaller updates.** Provinces issue frequent guidance and "documentos de desarrollo curricular". These include PBA's 2025 ESI document, national digital-education and robotics NAP (unverified resolution number), and literacy plans. They seldom touch chemistry content. Changes to the *academic regime* (timetabling, assessment, subject grouping) are more frequent: PBA 2025, CABA 2025–26, Córdoba 2026.

**Announced or in progress:**
- PBA's new secondary design is under construction. The earliest plausible start is the 2027 school year (unverified).
- The national *Ley de Libertad Educativa* bill is pending in Congress (status unverified).
- CABA Secundaria Aprende keeps expanding. Some provinces are redesigning, e.g. Córdoba's 2026 rollout.

**Stability rating for game content:** **stable.** The chemistry concepts and their year placement come from 2006–2015 documents that are still in force. Upcoming reforms mainly regroup subjects and change assessment. The structure has **moderate churn** (orientation hours, CABA Química in Year 11 or 12, area-based pilots), so tag content by concept and orientation rather than by exact subject name.

## 2. Year-by-year concepts and skills

Tags: [NAP] federal floor; [PBA] Provincia de Buenos Aires; [CABA]; [Cs. Nat.] Bachiller en Ciencias Naturales orientation; [otras orient.] other orientations.

### Year 7 (PBA 1º año ES / CABA 7º grado primaria)

**Concepts**
- *Matter & particles:* kinetic particle model (*modelo cinético corpuscular*) to explain some features of the states of matter [NAP S2]. Measurable properties: mass, volume, hardness, solubility, thermal and electrical conductivity; scales of values [PBA S4].
- *Mixtures & separation:* homogeneous mixtures (*soluciones*) and heterogeneous mixtures; soluble and insoluble; *fase* (phase) and *componente* (component); separating phases and components; mechanical separation methods; fractional distillation of petroleum (context) [PBA S4; NAP S2].
- *Inorganic/descriptive:* water as a substance compared with tap water as a mixture; drinking-water treatment (*potabilización*); melting and boiling points and density of water [PBA S4]. Families of materials: metals, plastics, synthetic compared with natural, biodegradable [PBA S4].
- *Analytical:* reagent tests for substances linked to nutrition, e.g. limewater for CO₂, iodine for starch [NAP S2].
- *Chemical reactions:* chemical compared with physical change; combustion (complete and incomplete, flame colour) and corrosion; reactants and products; energy released or absorbed [CABA S20].
- *Industrial/environmental:* materials that cause environmental damage; life cycle of materials [NAP S2; CABA S20].

**Skills**
- Practical: measure properties; design separation set-ups; lab safety [PBA S4].
- Quantitative: build and use property scales; measurement as agreed convention [PBA S4].
- Models: first particle drawings [NAP S2].
- Inquiry and communication: classify by chosen criteria, and understand that classification is arbitrary; record in tables and diagrams; write reports; form hypotheses about uses of materials [PBA S4]; ask investigable questions [CABA S20].

### Year 8 (PBA 2º año ES / CABA 1º año NES)

[CABA] has **no chemistry or physics subject** in Year 8 (Biología 4 h only) [S16]. The items below are [PBA] *Fisicoquímica* [S5] and the [NAP] 1º/2º-year block [S1].

**Concepts**
- *Matter & particles:* states of matter and changes of state (fusion, solidification, sublimation, *volatilización*, *licuación*, *vaporización*); kinetic-molecular model; discontinuity of matter [PBA S5; NAP S1]. Atomic-molecular theory: molecules, atoms, ions [NAP S1].
- *Gases:* the variables V, P, T and mass; Kelvin scale; Boyle–Mariotte and Charles/Gay-Lussac laws; the ideal gas equation of state [PBA S5]. Particle view of V, P and T [NAP S1].
- *Solutions & concentration:* solute and solvent; solutions in every combination of states (alloys, air); **%m/m, %m/V, %V/V**; saturated, unsaturated and supersaturated; solubility against temperature; separation by distillation, evaporation and crystallisation; substance compared with solution [PBA S5]. Dissolution explained with particles [NAP S1]. Environmental consequences of solubility [NAP S1].
- *Chemical reactions:* everyday reactions (combustion, redox and corrosion, synthesis, decomposition, acid rain); reaction as rearrangement of atoms that are conserved; first physical/chemical distinction (irreversibility criterion); **balancing by inspection** (*por tanteo*), no calculations; formulas read but **no nomenclature system and no oxide/acid/hydroxide classification** [PBA S5].
- *Atomic structure:* electrons, protons and neutrons; nucleus and electron cloud; atomic number; element as a kind of atom; nucleus-to-atom scale of about 10⁻⁵ [PBA S5].
- *Periodic table:* symbols; groups and periods; metals, non-metals, noble gases [PBA S5; NAP S1 "iniciación en el uso de la tabla periódica"].
- *Redox & electrochemistry:* cells (*pilas*) as chemical to electrical energy, used as an integrating context [PBA S5]. Conductors and insulators explained through the atom's charges [PBA S5].

**Skills**
- Practical: calibrate home-made measuring instruments; determine masses and volumes; introduction to measurement error [PBA S5].
- Quantitative: plot gas data and derive direct and inverse proportionality; calculate % concentration [PBA S5].
- Models: iconic and 3D models of particles in each state; iconic before-and-after diagrams of reactions; scale-model problems [PBA S5].
- Inquiry and communication: distinguish phenomenon (macro) from explanation (particle level); argue whether a change is physical or chemical [PBA S5].

### Year 9 (PBA 3º año ES / CABA 2º año NES)

[CABA] has **no chemistry subject** in Year 9 (Biología only) [S16]. The items below are [PBA] *Fisicoquímica* [S6] and the [NAP] 2º/3º-year block [S1].

**Concepts**
- *Atomic structure:* school model mixing Bohr (stationary levels) with the modern maximum of 2n² electrons per level; electron distribution by level; Z and A; isotopes; the "force between nucleons" [PBA S6]. Simplified current atom (nucleus plus cloud) [NAP S1].
- *Periodic table:* the table as a tool for systematic study of elements; reading atomic structure from it [NAP S1; PBA S6].
- *Bonding & structure:* ionic and covalent bonding classified by **electronegativity difference (ΔEn > 2,1 means ionic)**, metals excluded; octet rule; Lewis diagrams; **VSEPR (*TRePEV*)** geometry of simple binary molecules (H₂O, CH₄, NH₃, SO₂/SO₃); prototype ionic lattices; physical molecular models [PBA S6].
- *Formulas & nomenclature:* **oxidation number (not *valencia*)**; formulas and names of binary compounds (oxides, hydrides, *hidrácidos*, binary salts) using **one IUPAC-accepted nomenclature**; no systematic formation equations [PBA S6].
- *Chemical reactions & equations:* bonds broken and formed; conservation of atoms and mass; reading and writing balanced equations (still by inspection); combustion; law of conservation of mass and symbolic equations [PBA S6; NAP S1].
- *Redox:* ion-formation half-equations; oxidation and reduction as loss and gain of electrons; charge conservation; identifying redox by oxidation number [PBA S6].
- *Acids, bases & salts:* acidic, basic and neutral aqueous solutions, **experimental only**, with natural indicators (red cabbage, tea, beetroot), litmus, pH paper and phenolphthalein; everyday acids and bases (vinegar, citrus, ammonia cleaners, slaked lime); acid rain [PBA S6; NAP S1].
- *Energetics:* endothermic and exothermic reactions linked to bond energy; calculations from **heat of combustion per unit mass** (fuel needed for a task); orders of magnitude compared with nuclear [PBA S6].
- *Kinetics:* introduction to reaction rate; effect of temperature, concentration, surface area and catalysts, identified experimentally [PBA S6; NAP S1].
- *Nuclear chemistry:* fission and fusion; quantities conserved; natural radioactivity; reactors; applications [PBA S6; NAP S1 "reacción nuclear"].
- *Industrial/environmental:* chemical methods used to make materials; reactions used to prevent or repair environmental damage [NAP S1].
- **Explicit exclusion:** "no se introduce aún la noción de mol" ("the notion of the mole is not yet introduced"), so no stoichiometry [PBA S6].

**Skills**
- Practical: indicator tests; rate experiments [PBA S6].
- Quantitative: work out p, n and e from Z and A; energy from heats of combustion [PBA S6].
- Models: Lewis structures; ball-and-stick geometry; critique the solar-system analogy for the atom [PBA S6].
- Inquiry and communication: write and name formulas; read equations; argue about fuels and pollution [PBA S6].

### Year 10 (PBA 4º año ES / CABA 3º año NES)

**Concepts**
- [PBA, Cs. Nat.] *Introducción a la Química* [S7]. The same course is taught in Year 11 for the other orientations, so the rows are the same.
  - *Organic chemistry:* petroleum as a resource; refining and fractions; cracking and reforming; octane rating (*octanaje*); alternative fuels; functional groups; molecular, structural (*desarrollada*) and condensed formulas; chain isomerism; IUPAC names for hydrocarbons and groups as needed.
  - *Bonding & structure:* bond and molecular polarity; temporary and permanent dipoles; intermolecular forces; effect of geometry and molar mass on boiling point and solubility.
  - *Stoichiometry & the mole:* **molar mass, amount of substance (the mole)**; stoichiometric relations; limiting reagent; yield; reagent purity.
  - *Gases:* **ideal gas equation (PV = nRT)** in calculations.
  - *Energetics:* molar heats of combustion; exo- and endothermic reactions.
  - *Biochemistry:* carbohydrates (open-chain and **Haworth** forms, glycosidic bond, condensation polymers); lipids (triesters of glycerol, saturated and unsaturated fatty acids, ω-series, cis/trans, hydrogenation); soaps and detergents, micelles and bilayers; amino acids, proteins, denaturation, enzymes. [Cs. Nat.] omits the metabolism core, which is taught in Year 12.
  - *Solutions:* **molarity**.
  - *Equilibrium:* reversibility; **Kc**; reaction quotient Q to predict direction; **Le Chatelier** (qualitative: concentration, P, V, T, catalyst); homogeneous gas-phase systems only.
  - *Industrial:* Bosch process (H₂), Haber process (NH₃), Ostwald process; metallurgy (ores, *mena* and *ganga*, redox extraction); aluminium and nitric acid; industrial pollution.
- [PBA, otras orient.] no chemistry subject in Year 10.
- [CABA, all orientations] *Físico-Química* (4 h) [S17]:
  - *Energetics:* heat and temperature; thermometry; thermal equilibrium; latent heat; cooling curves.
  - *Solutions:* solute and solvent; dilute, concentrated and saturated; colligative properties (fresh and salt water).
  - *Matter & particles:* particle model of gases and of phase change.
  - *Formulas:* classification of materials; history of atomic models; periodic table (groups and periods; metals, non-metals, noble gases); element and molecule; air as a gas mixture; the symbolic language of chemistry.
  - *Acids, bases & salts:* oxides, acids and bases in environmental processes, distinguished with lab reagents.
  - *Chemical reactions:* reactants and products; reactions in the environment (corrosion, photosynthesis, carbon cycle).
  - *Bonding & structure:* water (structure, intermolecular forces, resource).
  - *Industrial/environmental:* greenhouse effect.
- [NAP ciclo orientado] applies from Year 10 onwards: levels of description (macro, micro, submicro); bonding, molecular-geometry and intermolecular-force models; composite, polymer, "smart" and nano materials; acid–base as **hydron (*hidrón*) transfer**, redox as electron transfer, collision theory; qualitative and approximate quantitative treatment of matter and energy in reactions; chemical equilibrium; socio-scientific issues (pesticides, waste, water, open-pit mining) [S3]. Jurisdictions spread these over 1–3 years.

**Skills**
- Quantitative: stoichiometry with gases, purity, limiting reagent and yield; Kc calculations [PBA S7]. Fourier's-law modelling and graph construction [CABA S17].
- Models: choose between formula types and explain their limits [PBA S7].
- Inquiry and communication: argue about energy alternatives and industrial pollution; communicate to different audiences [PBA S7, S3].

### Year 11 (PBA 5º año ES / CABA 4º año NES)

**Concepts**
- [PBA, Cs. Nat.] *Fundamentos de Química* (3 h) [S8]:
  - *Solutions & concentration:* seawater composition; concentration units; molarity; **molality**; Argentine *Código Alimentario* definition of drinking water; solution properties.
  - *Solutions (colligative properties):* Arrhenius dissociation of electrolytes; **colligative properties** (boiling-point elevation, freezing-point depression, osmotic pressure).
  - *Equilibrium:* precipitation reactions; **Ksp** and Q (carbonates, sulfates, Cr/Fe/Al in waterways); Henry's law and mole fraction; dissolved O₂ and CO₂; BOD.
  - *Acids, bases & salts:* autoprotolysis of water; **pH concept and calculations**; Arrhenius and **Brønsted–Lowry**; Ka and Kb, and conjugate strength; buffers and the **Henderson** equation; pH regulation in oceans and blood.
  - *Redox & electrochemistry:* half-reactions; **ion-electron balancing**; electrochemical cells; batteries (lead–acid, dry, alkaline) and their disposal; electrolysis; **Faraday's laws** and redox stoichiometry; chlorine production; corrosion; biological redox.
  - *Industrial:* sulfuric acid (contact process, V₂O₅); heats of solution and dilution; preparing solutions by dilution and mixing.
  - *Kinetics:* rate against temperature, surface area and concentration; collision model and activated-complex model; catalysts and enzymes; running the Haber process in practice.
- [PBA, otras orient.] *Introducción a la Química* (2 h): the Year 10 content above [S7, S10]. Schools pick core 1 or 2 of axes 2 and 3. [Educación Física orientation] extends metabolism and drops the industrial axis [S7].
- [CABA] *Física* (3 h) in the 2015 design. **Química (4 h) sits here instead of Year 12 in some 2026 Secundaria Aprende plans** [S18] (unverified how widespread).

**Skills**
- Quantitative: pH, Ka/Kb, Ksp, Faraday and dilution calculations [PBA S8].
- Practical: prepare solutions; build electrochemical cells; run rate experiments [PBA S8].
- Inquiry and communication: evaluate the environmental impact of batteries and effluents; interpret legal water standards [PBA S8].

### Year 12 (PBA 6º año ES / CABA 5º año NES)

**Concepts**
- [PBA, Cs. Nat.] *Química del Carbono* (3 h) [S9]:
  - *Atomic structure:* Rutherford and Bohr compared with the current model; levels, sublevels and **orbitals**; **electron configurations**; configuration and position in the periodic table; periodic properties.
  - *Bonding & structure:* **hybridisation** of carbon; benzene and resonance.
  - *Organic chemistry:* oxygen and nitrogen functional groups (alcohols, aldehydes, ketones, carboxylic acids, amines, amides) with IUPAC names; hydrogen bonding and boiling point and solubility trends; reactive sites; **addition, elimination and substitution** (classifying, not predicting products); esterification; condensation; organic redox; combustion.
  - *Biochemistry:* chirality; stereoisomerism; relative and absolute configuration; **Fischer projections**; aldoses and ketoses; hemiacetals; reducing sugars; polysaccharides; proteins, haemoglobin, enzyme models and cofactors; metabolism (anabolism and catabolism, glycolysis as a pathway, fermentation, fatty-acid catabolism).
  - *Polymers & materials:* common polymers and their monomers; classification; thermoplastics, thermosets and elastomers; glass transition temperature; cross-linking; fibres; free-radical addition of ethene; HDPE and LDPE; **homolytic and heterolytic cleavage**, reaction intermediates.
- [PBA, otras orient.] no chemistry.

## 3. Terminology and notation conventions

- **Nomenclature.**
  - PBA: "only one of the nomenclatures accepted by IUPAC", with *número de oxidación* explicitly **instead of *valencia*** [S6]. Organic names follow IUPAC [S7, S9].
  - CABA: "nomenclatura tradicional y moderna" presented for information, and the teacher then picks one [S17]. Expect students and textbooks to use a mix of traditional names (*ácido sulfúrico*, *óxido férrico*, *-oso/-ico*), Stock names (*óxido de hierro(III)*) and systematic names (unverified which dominates by province).
- **Decimal comma** in official documents (ΔEn = 2,1) [S6]. The thousands separator is a point (unverified in school use).
- **Units.** SI plus °C, K, L, atm and mmHg (unverified). Concentration is written **%m/m, %m/V, %V/V**, **M** (molaridad) and **m** (molalidad) [S5, S8, S17]. *Cantidad de sustancia* (amount of substance) and *masa molar* (molar mass) [S7].
- **Local terms that differ from English usage:**
  - *unión química* or *enlace*: chemical bond.
  - *TRePEV*: VSEPR.
  - *sistema material*: material system.
  - *fase / componente*: phase / component.
  - *sustancia simple / compuesta*: element / compound substance (unverified phrasing).
  - *solvente* (not *disolvente*).
  - *estados de agregación*: states of matter.
  - *licuación* (gas → liquid) and *volatilización* (solid → gas) as named changes of state.
  - *hidrón*: H⁺, as in the NAP.
  - *número másico / de masa*: mass number.
  - *hemirreacción*: half-reaction.
  - *método ion-electrón*: ion-electron method.
  - *formuleo*: formula-writing drill (used pejoratively in the curricula).
  - *nafta*: petrol or gasoline.
  - *reactivo limitante*: limiting reagent.
  - *rendimiento*: yield.
  - *pureza*: purity.
  - *Fisicoquímica / Físico-Química*: school subject combining physics and chemistry, not "physical chemistry".
- **Equations and ions.** Balancing is by inspection (*tanteo*) until Year 9, then ion-electron for redox in Year 11 [S5, S6, S8]. Ionic half-equations are introduced with ionic bonding in Year 9 [S6]. States written in brackets, e.g. (s), (l), (g), (ac) for aqueous (unverified).
- **Structure representations:** Lewis diagrams (Year 9); *fórmula desarrollada / condensada / molecular* (Year 10); Haworth (Year 10); Fischer (Year 12) [S6, S7, S9].
