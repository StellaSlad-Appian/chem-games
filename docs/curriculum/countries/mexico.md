# Chemistry curriculum: Mexico (Years 7–12)

**Purpose:** input for the cross-country concept/year map for chem-games.
**Research date:** 24 Sept 2026 (desk research by an agent).
**Review status:** not yet checked by a teacher who teaches in Mexico.
**Key sources:** SEP *Programa sintético Fase 6* (Plan de Estudio 2022, NEM) [S1–S4]; SEP/COSFAC *MCCEMS Modelo Educativo 2025, Ciencias Naturales, Experimentales y Tecnología* [S7] and DOF Acuerdo 21/08/25 [S8]; DGB curriculum maps 2025/2026 and MCCEMS 2023 programmes [S9–S15]; CCH-UNAM *Química I–IV* 2024 [S17]. Full list in section 6.

## Summary for game design

- **Chemistry as a named subject starts in Year 9 (3º de secundaria, age ~14).** Under Plan de Estudio 2022 / Nueva Escuela Mexicana (NEM), *Química* is the science discipline of 3º secundaria, with 6 lesson periods a week. Years 7 and 8 have Biología and Física instead. Year 8 Física already covers the particle model, atomic models and states of matter [S1][S4].
- **Year 9 is broad for a lower-secondary course:** mixtures and % concentration, ppm, the periodic table with periodic properties (electronegativity, ionisation energy, atomic radius), Bohr diagrams, **Lewis dot diagrams**, ionic vs molecular compounds, equations and conservation of mass, endo/exothermic reactions, **Arrhenius acids/bases and neutralisation**, and **redox with oxidation numbers and oxidising/reducing agents** [S1]. The mole is *not* in the secundaria programme.
- **In upper secondary (bachillerato), chemistry sits inside one integrated science subject, *Ciencias Naturales, Experimentales y Tecnología* (CNEyT I–VI, one course per semester, 4 h/week).** The chemistry-heavy courses are CNEyT I (semester 1, Year 10: matter, the atom, the periodic table, bonding, concentration) and **CNEyT IV "El poder de la química"** (semester 4, Year 11: reaction types, **mol and molar mass**, balancing by inspection, equilibrium constant, Arrhenius/Brønsted-Lowry/Lewis, pH, redox, combustion, organic functional groups and nomenclature, biomolecules, metabolism) [S7][S9].
- **Reform in mid-rollout:** a new national upper-secondary framework (MCCEMS "Modelo Educativo 2025", Acuerdo 21/08/25, part of the *Bachillerato Nacional*) applies to students who entered from Aug 2025. In 2026/27, **Years 10–11 follow the 2025 model and Year 12 still follows the 2023 model** (earlier cohorts finish on their old plan) [S8][S11].
- **Year 12 has almost no chemistry in the common core.** CNEyT V covers physics and CNEyT VI biology. Chemistry in Year 12 comes from electives, e.g. the DGB optional *Organización del flujo de materia y energía en los organismos I/II* (organic chemistry, stoichiometry, polymers, biomolecules), or from university-run schools (CCH Química III/IV optional; ENP Química IV compulsory for the science areas) [S13][S15][S17][S18].
- **Big split between subsystems.** The SEP/DGB *bachillerato general* is the typical track. The UNAM schools (CCH, ENP) use their own separate chemistry courses: CCH has Química I–II in semesters 1–2, the ENP has Química III in 5º año [S17][S18].
- **Notation games must respect:** Spanish names; **Stock nomenclature** for oxides and hydroxides but **traditional names for oxoacids** (CCH explicitly teaches both); IUPAC names for hydrocarbons; "balanceo por tanteo" (balancing by inspection) as the main balancing method; the word *disolución* (not *solución*) for solutions in official texts; "semimetales" for metalloids; *nutrimentos* for nutrients [S1][S7][S17].
- **The approach is context-led and project-based.** NEM programmes are "synthetic": schools write their own *programa analítico*, and textbooks are organised by campo formativo and projects, not by chemistry topic. Games should map to topic *contents* (contenidos) and *PDA* (processes of learning development), not to a textbook chapter order [S1][S5].

**Reforms in force or being phased in for 2026/27**
- **Secundaria:** Plan de Estudio 2022 (Acuerdo 14/08/22) and *Programas sintéticos* Fases 2–6 (Acuerdo 08/08/23, DOF 15 Aug 2023) are fully in force [S2][S3]. The last cohort on *Aprendizajes Clave 2017* left secundaria in June 2026 at the latest, so all three grades are on NEM in 2026/27. The 2026/27 free textbooks are the NEM *Saberes y pensamiento científico* series. The 3º book is *Colección Nanahuatzin* (S3SAA), which combines Mathematics and Chemistry [S5]. New literacy/numeracy books were added in 2026 (*Trazos y palabras*, *Trazos y números*). These do not change chemistry content (news source, unverified in official text).
- **Contrast with Aprendizajes Clave 2017** (*Ciencias y Tecnología. Química*, 3º; Acuerdo 07/06/17, then 12/10/17): the older programme was also Year 9 and organised by *ejes* ("Materia, energía e interacciones", "Sistemas", "Diversidad, continuidad y cambio"). It stressed submicroscopic models of atoms, molecules and ions, energy exchange in reactions, and **factors affecting reaction rate**, and it listed biomolecules and pollutant separation [S6]. The 2022 programme drops explicit kinetics and adds Lewis diagrams, periodic properties, Arrhenius acid–base and redox with oxidation numbers [S1]. Some commercial planning materials still use the 2017 labels.
- **Bachillerato:** MCCEMS **Modelo Educativo 2025** (Acuerdo 21/08/25, DOF 15 Aug 2025) replaced the MCCEMS 2023 acuerdos (09/08/23 and 09/05/24). It started in 2025/26 for school-based and dual options at SEP-run schools, and must reach all other options **by 2026/27 at the latest** [S8]. Transitorio Sexto: students already enrolled finish on their old plan. So in 2026/27 the **2026–2029 and 2025–2028 cohorts (Years 10–11) use the 2025 model** and the **2024–2027 cohort (Year 12) uses the 2023 progressions** [S8][S11]. The DGB issued a revised 2026 structure for the bachillerato general (office CSAC/856/2026). It keeps CNEyT I–VI at 4 h but adds compulsory electives from semester 4 and an elective track (*trayecto electivo*) [S9].
- **Bachillerato Nacional:** on 6 Aug 2025 the SEP signed the agreement creating the *Sistema de Bachillerato Nacional* from 2025/26. It unifies the ~30 subsystems into two forms (general and technological), with one common certificate plus a technical certificate [S16]. The MCC documents now carry the heading "Sistema Nacional de Bachillerato de la Nueva Escuela Mexicana" [S7]. UNAM (CCH, ENP) and many autonomous state universities keep their own plans. How far they align with the MCCEMS is unclear (unverified).

**National exams that shape content**
- **EXANI-II** (CENEVAL, university admission) has an optional *Química* module, reportedly 24 items covering general chemistry, stoichiometry and organic nomenclature and functional groups (commercial prep sites only; ceneval.edu.mx did not resolve, so this is **unverified**).
- **Entry to bachillerato in the Mexico City metro area:** the COMIPEMS exam was replaced in 2025 by the **ECOEMS** exam under the "Mi derecho, mi lugar" allocation scheme (2026 sitting: 128 items across 11 subjects). It draws on secundaria content, including Química (news/prep sites only, **unverified**).
- The national PLANEA large-scale assessments no longer shape secundaria chemistry (unverified).

**Typical interval and trigger.** Basic-education plans have changed about every 5–6 years (2006, 2011, 2017, 2022). Each change coincides with a six-year presidential term (*sexenio*). The revisions are ad hoc and political, not a fixed legal review cycle. The Ley General de Educación only requires the SEP to carry out "systematic and continuous" reviews and keep plans updated (Arts. 23 and 27), with no set period [S19]. Upper secondary has been much more volatile: RIEMS 2008, then an update around 2017, then **three successive frameworks in four years** (MCCEMS 2022, 2023/24, 2025), plus a 2026 structure revision. Acuerdo 21/08/25 now builds in evaluations after 2025/26 and again after three cohorts, at the end of 2028/29 [S8].

**Smaller updates.** Textbooks (*libros de texto gratuitos*, CONALITEG) are reissued every school year and sometimes reworked mid-plan: the NEM secundaria books came out in 2023–2024, and new supplementary books arrived in 2026 [S5]. DGB programmes are revised often. The 2023-model CNEyT programmes were reissued in Sept 2025, *Taller de Ciencias* in Jan 2026, and *Laboratorio de Investigación* in Feb 2026 [S11][S14]. UNAM's CCH updated its chemistry programmes in 2016 and 2024 [S17].

**Announced or in progress now.** The Modelo Educativo 2025 reaches all EMS options by 2026/27. The first evaluation is due after 2025/26, and a fuller one at the end of 2028/29 [S8]. The Year 12 content in this report changes in **2027/28**, when the first 2025-model cohort reaches semesters 5–6 (CNEyT V physics, CNEyT VI biology) [S7]. No secundaria revision has been officially announced, but the current federal term runs to 2030, so a new basic-education plan around 2029–2031 would follow the pattern (speculative, unverified).

**Stability rating for game content:** secundaria (Years 7–9): **stable to moderate churn**. The NEM contents have been fixed since 2023, and the core topics (mixtures, periodic table, bonding, reactions, acids/bases, redox) survive every revision. Bachillerato (Years 10–12): **high churn**. Frameworks changed in 2022, 2023, 2025 and 2026, and chemistry topics move between semesters with each model.

## 2. Year-by-year concepts and skills

### Year 7 (1º de secundaria)
**Concepts** (no chemistry subject; chemistry-adjacent content inside *Biología*) [S1]
- Biochemistry: nutrients (*nutrimentos*) and the effects of their lack or excess; excess salt, sugar and saturated fat linked to disease.
- Industrial, environmental & applied: consumption practices that alter the **carbon and nitrogen biogeochemical cycles**; global warming.
- Matter & particles: none explicit (mathematics and biology only).

**Skills** [S1]
- Inquiry/communication: forming hypotheses, interpreting data on correlations, using the microscope, debating (genetic manipulation), communicating findings through different media.
- Models/representations: food webs and trophic pyramids as models of the transfer of matter and energy; cell models.

### Year 8 (2º de secundaria)
**Concepts** (inside *Física*) [S1]
- Matter & particles: structure, properties and characteristics of matter; theories of the structure of matter from **atomic and particle models** and the phenomena that led to them; recent advances and how theories are built historically.
- Matter & particles: **three states of matter** explained with the particle model; physical properties such as **melting point, boiling point and density**; temperature and thermal equilibrium in the particle model.
- Energetics: heat as a form of energy; heat engines and exhaust gases; renewable/non-renewable sources, including nuclear (as an energy source only).
- Industrial, environmental & applied: greenhouse gases and climate change; heat, radiation and temperature distinguished.

**Skills** [S1]
- Quantitative: **SI base and derived units and their symbols**; measuring instruments; conversions with multiples and sub-multiples.
- Practical: simple experiments (fluids, Pascal/Archimedes, electricity safety protocols).
- Models/representations: proposing hypotheses with particle and atomic models.
- Inquiry: researching the contributions of women and men to physics; community mitigation proposals.

### Year 9 (3º de secundaria)
**Concepts** (*Química. Tercer grado*, Programa sintético Fase 6) [S1]
- Matter & particles: **extensive vs intensive properties** used to identify substances and materials; **mixtures, compounds and elements** told apart by experiment and represented with the **corpuscular (particle) model** in solids, liquids and gases.
- Mixtures & separation: components of a mixture (**soluto–disolvente**, **fase dispersa–fase dispersante**); **homogeneous vs heterogeneous**; separation by **evaporation, decantation, filtration, extraction, sublimation, chromatography and crystallisation**, based on physical properties.
- Solutions & concentration: concentration as **% by mass and % by volume** in everyday products (hygiene, food, cleaning); **ppm** for pollutants in air, water and soil.
- Periodic table: presence and abundance of elements in living things, the Earth and the Universe; **metals, non-metals, semimetals**; reading the table by atomic number, **groups and periods**; **periodic properties: electronegativity, ionisation energy, atomic radius** of representative elements.
- Atomic structure: **Bohr models** (electrons in orbits) of the first elements; protons, neutrons, electrons; **valence electrons shown as Lewis diagrams**, related to group number.
- Bonding & structure: **ionic vs molecular compounds** by their properties (experimental); formation and structure predicted from periodic properties; uses in the human body.
- Biochemistry: food as a source of chemical energy: **carbohydrates, proteins, lipids**; vitamins, minerals, water; energy content of food.
- Chemical reactions & equations: recognising reactions by their signs and changes in properties; representing reactions with **3D models and chemical equations**; **law of conservation of matter**; what changes and what stays the same in a reaction.
- Energetics: **endothermic and exothermic** reactions and their uses.
- Acids, bases & salts: properties of acids and bases, **indicators**, the **acidity/basicity scale** (pH scale, qualitative); **Arrhenius model**; predicting products of simple **neutralisation** reactions; neutralisation for treating waste.
- Redox & electrochemistry: redox in the environment; **electron transfer judged by the change in oxidation number**; **oxidising and reducing agents**.
- Industrial, environmental & applied: pollution and degradation linked to chemical products and processes; responsible consumption; sustainability; historical milestones and the contributions of different peoples and cultures (medicine, building, crafts, textiles, food).

**Skills** [S1]
- Practical/lab: designing and carrying out neutralisations; separating mixtures; experiments to tell ionic from molecular compounds; using measuring instruments.
- Quantitative: % m/m and % v/v concentrations; ppm (interpreting, not heavy calculation).
- Models/representations: particle models of mixtures, compounds and elements; Bohr diagrams; Lewis diagrams; 3D molecular models; chemical equations.
- Inquiry/communication: hypotheses → evidence → conclusions and predictions; community projects (*proyectos comunitarios*); debates on the benefits and costs of redox processes; oral and written sources.

### Year 10 (1º de bachillerato, semesters 1–2)
**Concepts: DGB bachillerato general, MCCEMS Modelo Educativo 2025** [S7][S9]
- *CNEyT I "Invitación a la ciencia. Naturaleza de la materia" (sem 1, 4 h)* [DGB]:
  - Matter & particles: matter and body (*cuerpo*); **mass vs weight**; **density** (calculation); **classification of matter**: substance, pure substance, element, compound, mixture; physical and chemical properties; **kinetic theory of matter**; states of aggregation and changes of state (**solid, liquid, gas and plasma**); kinetic, potential and internal energy of particles.
  - Mixtures & separation: types of mixture; separation methods.
  - Solutions & concentration: **concentration calculations: mass–mass, mass–volume, volume–volume and ppm**.
  - Atomic structure: atomic theory **Dalton → Thomson → Rutherford → Bohr → Schrödinger**; electric charge; **atomic number and mass number**; **isotopes**; **electron configuration and valence**.
  - Periodic table: periodic classification of the elements.
  - Bonding & structure: **chemical bonding**; **electronegativity** and intramolecular forces; formation of **ions and molecules** as atoms tend towards energetic stability.
  - Polymers & materials: technological applications of matter (e.g. nanotechnology in the 2023 model).
- *CNEyT II "El poder de la energía" (sem 2, 4 h)* [DGB], chemistry-relevant parts:
  - Energetics & thermodynamics: conservation of energy; **heat vs temperature**; thermometric scales (absolute and relative); thermal equilibrium; conduction, convection, radiation; **specific heat capacity**; **calorie–joule equivalence**; zeroth and **first law**; **entropy and enthalpy** (concept); second and third laws.
  - Gases: **ideal gas dynamics and equation**; open and closed systems, state variables.
- *Taller de Ciencias (sem 2, 4 h, compulsory extended core)* [DGB]: inquiry project on energy transfer in natural systems.
- [CCH] *Química I* (sem 1): water (states, particle model, solvent capacity, mixtures and concentration, electrolysis and synthesis, Dalton and Bohr models, polarity, hydrogen bonds); *Química II* (sem 2): soil (salts, dissociation, pH and nutrients, **oxidation states**, **Stock nomenclature** of salts, **stoichiometric calculations**) and food and medicines (carbon compounds, isomerism, functional groups, hydrolysis) [S17]. The oxygen unit covers the air as a mixture, combustion, **metal vs non-metal oxides**, periodic trends, acid rain and ocean acidification, and Lewis formulas of oxides, hydroxides and acids [S17].

**Skills** [S7][S14][S17]
- Practical/lab: school experiments with the classroom as a laboratory; virtual labs and simulators where there is no lab; separation and concentration practicals [CCH].
- Quantitative: measurement, magnitudes and units; volume and density calculations; concentration calculations (% and ppm); energy calculations; the ideal gas equation.
- Models/representations: historical atomic models; particle model of the states of matter; representing bonding.
- Inquiry/communication: the scientific method alongside empirical and traditional knowledge; history of science in Mexico; the 5E model; science communication (*divulgación*) as the closing product (Taller de Ciencias).

### Year 11 (2º de bachillerato, semesters 3–4)
**Concepts: DGB, MCCEMS Modelo Educativo 2025** [S7][S9]
- *CNEyT III "Nuestro hogar. El sistema terrestre" (sem 3, 4 h)* [DGB]:
  - Inorganic/descriptive: chemical composition of the atmosphere and hydrosphere; **Oparin–Haldane reducing primitive atmosphere** vs today's; oxygen cycle; **formation of basic and acidic oxides**.
  - Chemical reactions & equations: **concept and structure of a chemical reaction**; **the chemical equation as a representation**; symbols used in formulas and equations.
  - Biochemistry: photosynthesis (light phase, Calvin cycle; overall equation and products).
  - Industrial, environmental & applied: carbon, nitrogen and phosphorus cycles; greenhouse effect and climate change; air and water pollution; technologies that reduce environmental damage.
- *CNEyT IV "El poder de la química" (sem 4, 4 h)* [DGB], the main chemistry course:
  - Chemical reactions & equations: reactants and products; rearrangement of atoms; energy released or absorbed; chemical energy; **classification: exothermic, endothermic, synthesis, decomposition, displacement, neutralisation**; reactions in nature and technology.
  - Stoichiometry & the mole: **law of conservation of mass**; **mol and molar mass**; **balancing equations by inspection (*método de tanteo*)**; algebraic and redox balancing methods *mentioned only*.
  - Equilibrium: **reversible vs irreversible reactions**; **equilibrium constant and equilibrium expression**; reversible reactions in nature.
  - Acids, bases & salts: **Arrhenius, Brønsted–Lowry and Lewis theories**; classification by **conductivity** (strong/weak); **pH** and ranking everyday substances by pH.
  - Redox & electrochemistry: **oxidation–reduction reactions**; importance for living things and industry.
  - Energetics: **combustion reactions**.
  - Organic chemistry: why organic compounds matter; **structure of carbon and its bond types**; **classification of organic compounds, functional groups and their nomenclature**.
  - Polymers & materials: **monomers and polymers** (definition).
  - Biochemistry: carbohydrates (mono-, di-, polysaccharides), lipids (oils, fats, phospholipids, steroids), proteins (essential/non-essential amino acids), nucleic acids; chemical aspects of **glycolysis, Krebs cycle, electron transport chain, fermentation**.
- Contrast: the MCCEMS 2023 version of CNEyT IV (*"Reacciones químicas: conservación de la materia en la formación de nuevas sustancias"*, taken by the 2024–2027 cohort in 2025/26) also had **bond energy**, **collision theory and reaction rates**, **dynamic equilibrium and Le Chatelier-type response to disturbance**, the **mole as the bridge from atomic to macroscopic scale**, and **nuclear processes (fusion, fission, radioactive decay; nucleon number conserved)** [S12].
- [ENP] *Química III* (5º año, compulsory, 2017 programme): context units on the chemical elements in mobile devices, controlling urban air emissions, and drinking-water supply [S18] (detail unverified; site unreachable).

**Skills** [S7][S12]
- Quantitative: molar mass; mole calculations; balancing by inspection; writing equilibrium expressions; interpreting pH values.
- Models/representations: chemical equations and symbols; structural formulas of organic compounds; particle models of reaction (2023 model).
- Practical/lab: identifying reactions and acids/bases (conductivity, indicators); combustion.
- Inquiry/communication: explaining phenomena in nature, daily life and industry; linking chemistry to the bioethics of metabolism and technology.

### Year 12 (3º de bachillerato, semesters 5–6)
**Concepts: DGB, for the 2024–2027 cohort still on the MCCEMS 2023 plan in 2026/27** [S11][S13][S15]
- Core *CNEyT V "La energía en los procesos de la vida diaria"* (sem 5, 4 h) [DGB]: mainly physics (forces, momentum, fields, electromagnetic radiation modelled as waves or photons, electric energy). Chemistry appears only as energy transfer in chemical reactions (**cellular respiration breaks and forms bonds**) and the Earth's energy/matter cycles.
- Core *CNEyT VI "Organismos: estructuras y procesos. Herencia y evolución biológica"* (sem 6) [DGB]: biology; no chemistry progressions identified.
- Elective *Organización del flujo de materia y energía en los organismos I* (sem 5, 3 h, one of many electives) [DGB-optional]:
  - Organic chemistry: carbon **tetravalence and catenation**; **hybridisation** and bond angles, molecular geometry; **single, double, triple bonds**; **hydrocarbons** (open-chain, branched, **cyclic**); **functional groups**; organic reaction types (**addition, elimination, substitution, condensation, rearrangement, combustion**).
  - Stoichiometry: **stoichiometric mass calculations** for syntheses of organic molecules.
  - Polymers & materials: polymers, their degradation and environmental impact.
- Elective *… II* (sem 6) [DGB-optional]: water as solvent; carbohydrates, lipids, glucose and **glycolysis**; energy coupling in polymer (protein) synthesis; amino-acid sequence and nucleic acids; technological uses of biomolecules.
- From **2027/28** Year 12 moves to the 2025 model: CNEyT V "Del átomo al universo. Fuerza y energía" (physics, including optional outreach topics on nuclear and particle physics) and CNEyT VI "¿Qué es la vida?" (biology, including **Oparin–Haldane chemosynthesis, the Miller–Urey experiment**, organic molecules of the cell, DNA/RNA nucleotides) [S7].
- [CCH] *Química III* (sem 5, optional): Mexico's chemical industry; **from minerals to metals** (chemical processes of extraction); **controlling industrial processes** for strategic products. *Química IV* (sem 6, optional): **petroleum** as a source of carbon compounds; **polymers** [S17].
- [ENP] *Química IV* (6º año, areas I and II, compulsory by area choice): medicines, food and water as contexts [S18] (unverified detail).

**Skills** [S15][S17]
- Models/representations: 3D models and simulators of carbon compounds; condensed and structural formulas.
- Quantitative: stoichiometric calculations.
- Practical: laboratory work on carbon compounds [DGB-optional]; industrial-process case studies [CCH].
- Inquiry/communication: asking questions, analysing data, evaluating and communicating information; ethics of consumption.

## 3. Terminology and notation conventions

- **Language:** Spanish throughout; official texts use inclusive forms ("las y los estudiantes", "el estudiantado").
- **Nomenclature:** mixed. CCH teaches **Stock nomenclature** (with oxidation states) for oxides, hydroxides and salts, **traditional nomenclature for oxoacids** (e.g. *ácido sulfúrico*), and **IUPAC nomenclature for hydrocarbons** up to C8 [S17]. The DGB/MCCEMS programmes say only "nomenclatura" of organic compounds and functional groups [S7]. Games should accept Stock and traditional inorganic names (e.g. *óxido de hierro(III)* and *óxido férrico*) (typical textbook practice, unverified across all textbooks).
- **Key local words:** *disolución* (solution), *soluto/disolvente*, *fase dispersa/dispersante*, *estados de agregación* (states of matter), *sustancia pura*, *mezcla homogénea/heterogénea*, *propiedades extensivas/intensivas*, *modelo corpuscular* (particle model), *diagrama/estructura de Lewis*, *número de oxidación* (secundaria) / *estado de oxidación* (CCH), *agente oxidante/reductor*, *balanceo por tanteo* (balancing by inspection), *masa molar*, *el mol*, *potencial de Hidrógeno (pH)*, *semimetales* (metalloids), *nutrimentos* (nutrients), *óxidos básicos/ácidos*, *oxiácidos*, *hidróxidos*, *puente de hidrógeno* (hydrogen bond), *fuerzas intramoleculares/intermoleculares*.
- **Units:** SI via the Mexican standard NOM-008-SE-2021 (which replaced NOM-008-SCFI-2002). Physics in secundaria explicitly teaches SI units and symbols [S1][S23]. The calorie is still taught alongside the joule (CNEyT II, calorie–joule equivalence) [S7]. Concentrations appear as % m/m, % v/v, m/v and ppm [S1][S7].
- **Decimal separator:** the national standard allows **either a comma or a point** on the line [S23]. Mexican everyday and school practice overwhelmingly uses the **decimal point** (e.g. 6.022 × 10²³) and commas for thousands (common practice, **unverified** in a sampled official textbook). Games should display a decimal point for Mexico.
- **Equations:** standard arrow notation; state symbols in Spanish-language texts are usually (s), (l), (g), (ac) for *acuoso* (unverified for Mexican official texts).
- **Periodic table:** groups and periods; secundaria stresses metals, non-metals and semimetals and three periodic properties (electronegativity, ionisation energy, atomic radius) [S1]. Group-numbering convention (1–18 vs IA–VIIIA) is not stated in the programmes. Older Mexican texts often use A/B numbering (unverified).
