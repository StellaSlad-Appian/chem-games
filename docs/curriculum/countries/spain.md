# Chemistry curriculum: Spain (Years 7–12)

**Purpose:** input for the cross-country concept/year map for chem-games.
**Research date:** 24 Sept 2026 (desk research by an agent).
**Review status:** not yet checked by a teacher who teaches in Spain.
**Key sources:** Real Decreto 217/2022 (ESO) and 243/2022 (Bachillerato) in the BOE; Comunidad de Madrid Decretos 65/2022 and 64/2022 (worked example); Real Decreto 534/2024 (PAU) with the 2025-26 Madrid and CRUE model exams; RSEQ nomenclature guidance. Full list in §6.

## Summary for game design

- **Chemistry is taught inside "Física y Química" (FyQ), a combined physics and chemistry subject, until the last year.** A chemistry-only subject ("Química") exists only in Year 12 (2º Bachillerato), and only students on the science track who choose it take it. [S1][S2]
- **The national minimums (the *enseñanzas mínimas*) give Years 7–9 (1º–3º ESO) as one band with one list of *saberes básicos* (content statements).** Each autonomous community (*comunidad autónoma*, the 17 regions that set their own school curricula) decides which of those years get FyQ. Under the national rule each student takes "Biología y Geología and/or Física y Química" in each of 1º–3º. Madrid and Andalucía teach FyQ in **2º and 3º ESO (Years 8–9)** and not in Year 7. Catalonia treats 1º–3º as one band. [S1][S3][S6][S7]
- **Year 10 (4º ESO) FyQ is optional**: students pick 3 subjects from a list of 10. Madrid bundles it with Biología y Geología as the "Opción Científica". This is where the mole, electron configurations, bonding types, ternary nomenclature, stoichiometry and the first organic nomenclature arrive. [S1][S3]
- **The big topics land late and together.** Thermodynamics (ΔH, Hess, ΔS, ΔG), kinetics (rate laws, orders), equilibrium (Kc, Kp, Ks, Le Châtelier), acid–base (pH, Ka/Kb, hydrolysis, titrations), redox (ion-electron method, E°, Faraday), isomerism, organic reaction types and polymers all fall in Year 12 Química. Madrid pulls enthalpy and Hess's law forward into Year 11. [S2][S4]
- **Nomenclature is a big, examined skill in every year.** The sequence is IUPAC 2005 compositional names with multiplying prefixes (Years 8–9), then Stock numbers and accepted traditional oxoacid names (Year 10), then full inorganic nomenclature (Year 11), then organic mono- and polyfunctional nomenclature (Years 10–12). The RSEQ (Real Sociedad Española de Química, the Spanish chemical society) advises against old *anhídrido* and *-oso/-ico* names for binaries. Games must use the Spanish word order ("cloruro de sodio", the anion first), "óxido de hierro(III)", "amoniaco", "fosfano" (not "fosfina") and "propan-1-ol". [S16]
- **Notation:** decimal comma (0,082 atm·L·mol⁻¹·K⁻¹), a middle dot for multiplied units, atm and mmHg still used alongside Pa, and "diagrama de Moeller" for the Aufbau diagonal rule. **RPECV** is the Spanish acronym for VSEPR. [S2][S12][S14]
- **Exam:** the PAU (*Prueba de Acceso a la Universidad*, formerly EBAU/EvAU) has run to Real Decreto 534/2024 since June 2025. Each paper is one 90-min exam and at least 70 % of the marks go to open or semi-constructed answers. The university districts agreed that at least 20–25 % of each paper must be compulsory "competency" questions set in real contexts. Química is **not** a compulsory PAU paper. Science students take it to raise their admission score. [S8][S11]
- **Context-rich, competence-based framing** runs through all LOMLOE documents. Each subject has 6 *competencias específicas* (subject competences), *criterios de evaluación* (assessment criteria) and *saberes básicos* (content), with a sustainability and society angle. Games that tie chemistry to fertilisers, CO₂, batteries or fuels match what the PAU rewards. [S1][S2][S12][S14]
- **Surprises:** (1) Year 7 usually has no chemistry at all. (2) Physics and chemistry share one teacher and one timetable slot until Year 12, and in 4º ESO about half of FyQ is mechanics. (3) Regional variation is large. Content, the course a topic falls in and even the exam format differ: Madrid's PAU has 4 questions of 2,5 points, while the CRUE (Spanish university rectors' conference) model has 5 of 2 points. (4) In bilingual schools FyQ may be taught in English. Madrid does not exclude it from foreign-language teaching. [S5]

**Reforms in force or being phased in for 2026/27**
- LOMLOE (Ley Orgánica 3/2020) curricula are fully in force. RD 217/2022 applied to 1º/3º ESO from 2022-23 and 2º/4º from 2023-24. RD 243/2022 applied to 1º Bach from 2022-23 and 2º Bach from 2023-24 [S1][S2]. 2026/27 is the fourth or fifth year of these curricula, so nothing is being phased in at curriculum level.
- The BOE consolidated text of RD 243/2022 was last updated on 02/07/2026 by **RD 535/2026**. That change only affects how the Bachiller grade is calculated for recognised foreign qualifications and does not touch chemistry content. RD 217/2022 has had no content changes since a 2022 correction [S2][S10].
- PAU: RD 534/2024 has applied since the 2024-25 exams. The 2025-26 exams kept the 2025 structure [S13]. The CRUE harmonised model for Química applied to PAU 2026. **PAU 2027 guidance was still "pendiente publicar" (not yet published)** on the Universidad de Murcia site [S14][S15].

**National exams that shape content**
- **PAU**, run by each community's university district. The compulsory papers are Lengua, Historia de España or Filosofía, Lengua Extranjera and the compulsory modality subject; for Ciencias y Tecnología that is **Matemáticas II or MACS II, not Química** [S8 art. 12]. Química is taken as an optional extra paper, up to 3 extra subjects, to raise the admission grade (maximum 14) [S8][S11].
- Format under RD 534/2024: 90 min; one single paper per subject (the old choice between two complete papers is gone); competence-based design; open or semi-constructed answers worth at least 70 %; context "close to students' lives"; calculators and data tables allowed as set by each commission [S8 art. 13].
- The Química paper is organised by the national blocks: **A** structure and bonding, **B** reactions (thermodynamics, kinetics, equilibrium, acid–base, redox), **C** organic. The CRUE model sets weights of about **20 % A / 60 % B / 20 % C**. Its compulsory question is worth 2 points, followed by 4 A/B choice questions [S14]. Madrid's 2025-26 model has 4 questions of 2,5 points: 1 compulsory, 3 with an A/B choice [S12].
- There is no national exam at the end of ESO. The sample-based *evaluación de diagnóstico* (a low-stakes survey test) in 2º ESO does not steer chemistry content (unverified).

**Typical interval.** A new organic law and new national minimums have come roughly every **7–8 years**: 1991/92, 2006/07, 2014, 2022, plus a within-law revision in 2000. Changes are **ad hoc and political**. Each one follows a change of government or a reform programme; the LOMLOE decrees were also tied to EU Recovery Plan commitments. There is no scheduled legal review cycle. The core chemistry has stayed stable across reforms (the 1º Bach FyQ / 2º Bach Química split has held since 1992). What changes is the framing (objectives → standards → competences) and which ESO years carry FyQ.

**Smaller updates.**
- Regional decrees are amended every year or two. Madrid Decreto 59/2024 (June 2024) changed Geografía e Historia and foreign-language teaching rules but not FyQ [S5]. Catalonia's Decret 480/2024 (Dec 2024) clarified one 4º ESO FyQ assessment criterion and changed hours from 2025-26 (reported on the official modification page; the text was not read in full) [S7]. Castilla-La Mancha's Decreto 31/2024 amended its ESO decree (details unverified).
- PAU guidance is reissued **every year** by each district's subject commission, usually September–November, with a new model exam. For example, the Madrid Química commission met on 20 Oct 2025, and Madrid kept its 2025 structure for 2026 [S13].

**Announced or in progress now (Sept 2026).**
- No national revision of the FyQ or Química minimums had been published in the BOE as of 24 Sept 2026 [S2].
- PAU 2027 subject guidance is due in autumn 2026 (still pending at Murcia) [S15]. Watch whether districts adopt the CRUE harmonised Química model and its 20/60/20 block weighting [S14].
- A change of national government after the next general election (due by 2027) could bring a new organic law, as happened in 2002, 2006, 2013 and 2020 **(unverified; nothing has been tabled)**.

**Stability rating for game content: moderate churn.** The chemistry concepts and their order have hardly moved in 30 years. But which ESO year carries FyQ, how many hours it gets and how the PAU paper is built change with regional decrees and yearly exam guidance, and a new organic law has arrived every 7–8 years.

## 2. Year-by-year concepts and skills

Unless stated otherwise, "national" means the RD 217/2022 or RD 243/2022 *saberes básicos*. Madrid additions come from Decretos 65/2022 and 64/2022, which split the national 1º–3º band into separate 2º and 3º ESO lists.

### Year 7 (1º ESO)

**Not applicable in the typical community.** Madrid and Andalucía teach Biología y Geología in 1º ESO and no FyQ [S3][S6]. The national 1º–3º band (see Year 8) may be taught in 1º where a community schedules FyQ there, or in Catalonia, where FyQ runs across 1º–3º as one band [S7]; the per-course split there is unverified. For mapping, treat any Year 7 chemistry as the **intro** end of the Year 8 list.

### Year 8 (2º ESO)

National band 1º–3º ESO [S1]; Madrid 2º ESO list [S3].

**Concepts**
- *Matter & particles:* properties of matter; introductory kinetic-molecular theory (*teoría cinético-molecular*); states of matter (*estados de agregación*) and changes of state explained with particles. [S1][S3]
- *Mixtures & separation:* pure substances vs mixtures; mixtures of special interest (aqueous solutions, alloys, colloids); separation methods. [S3]
- *Atomic structure:* early atomic models, **Thomson and Rutherford**; atomic number. [S3]
- *Periodic table:* introduction; atoms vs molecules; simple and compound substances in everyday use. [S3]
- *Chemical reactions:* physical vs chemical changes; macroscopic introduction to reactions; chemistry, environment and society. [S1][S3]
- *Energetics (physics side):* forms of energy, conservation of energy, effects of heat on matter. [S3]

**Skills**
- *Practical/lab:* basic lab equipment; measuring instruments; lab safety rules; **hazard labels on chemicals** (*etiquetado*); waste disposal and recycling; simple guided investigations. [S3]
- *Quantitative/math:* SI units and symbols; direct and indirect measurement; simple unit changes. [S1][S3]
- *Representations/models:* particle diagrams (from KMT); graphing results. [S3]
- *Inquiry/communication:* the scientific method and its stages; asking questions and forming hypotheses; the history of science and the role of women and men scientists. [S1][S3]

### Year 9 (3º ESO)

National band 1º–3º ESO [S1]; Madrid 3º ESO list [S3].

**Concepts**
- *Gases:* **gas laws** (Boyle, Charles, Gay-Lussac; which laws are covered is unverified) and the KMT explanation. [S3]
- *Solutions & concentration:* solutions and **concentration** (in g/L and % are typical; unverified). [S3]
- *Atomic structure:* protons, neutrons, electrons; **isotopes** (existence, formation, properties); **ions**. [S1][S3]
- *Periodic table:* arrangement of the elements and periodic properties (qualitative). [S1][S3]
- *Bonding & structure:* introduction to chemical bonding. [S3]
- *Stoichiometry & the mole:* atomic and molecular mass; **approach to the mole concept**. [S1][S3]
- *Formulas & nomenclature:* formulating and naming **simple substances, monatomic ions and binary compounds** under IUPAC rules. [S1][S3]
- *Chemical reactions & equations:* microscopic interpretation of reactions; **balancing simple equations**; **law of conservation of mass (Lavoisier)** and **law of definite proportions (Proust)** as evidence for the atomic-molecular model; simple stoichiometric calculations. [S1][S3]
- *Kinetics:* **qualitative effect of temperature and concentration** on reactions. [S1][S3]
- *Industrial, environmental & applied:* elements and compounds with industrial, technological and biomedical uses. [S3]
- (Physics in the same course: kinematics, forces, Hooke's law, Newton's laws, electricity and Ohm's law.) [S3]

**Skills**
- *Practical/lab:* correct use of materials and tools; lab safety; interpreting hazard labels; waste. [S3]
- *Quantitative/math:* derived quantities; conversion factors; **scientific notation; significant figures**. [S3]
- *Representations/models:* tables, graphs and mathematical expressions for data; particle and atomic models. [S3]
- *Inquiry/communication:* guided projects; **introduction to writing a lab report** (*informe científico*); reading popular-science texts critically. [S3]

### Year 10 (4º ESO) [optativa 4º ESO]

National 4º ESO [S1]; Madrid 4º ESO list [S3].

**Concepts**
- *Gases:* **general gas law** (combined); problems with gases. [S1][S3]
- *Solutions & concentration:* problems with solutions; **molar concentration**. [S1][S3]
- *Atomic structure:* history of classical and quantum atomic models, up to **Bohr–Sommerfeld**; subatomic particles. [S1][S3]
- *Periodic table:* **electron configuration** linked to position in the table and to physico-chemical properties. [S1][S3]
- *Bonding & structure:* **ionic, covalent and metallic bonding**; properties of the resulting substances. [S3]
- *Stoichiometry & the mole:* the **mole**, **Avogadro's constant**, number of moles in different systems; **percentage yield** (*rendimiento*). [S1][S3]
- *Formulas & nomenclature:* **binary and ternary** inorganic compounds and ions (IUPAC); **introduction to organic nomenclature**: monofunctional compounds, main functional groups, **alkanes, alkenes, alkynes**. [S1][S3]
- *Organic chemistry:* the variety of carbon compounds; organic compounds of industrial and biological interest. [S1][S3]
- *Chemical reactions & equations:* balancing; qualitative and quantitative predictions; types of reaction. [S1][S3]
- *Acids, bases & salts:* **neutralisation**, described qualitatively. [S1]
- *Redox & electrochemistry:* "simple electrochemical processes", qualitative. [S1]
- *Energetics:* **combustion**; introduction to energy in chemical reactions. [S1][S3]
- *Kinetics:* **collision theory**; reaction mechanism (qualitative); rate factors; approach to reaction rate. [S1][S3]
- (Physics, about half the course: motion and graphs (uniform, uniformly accelerated and circular motion), vector forces, gravitation, pressure in fluids (Archimedes, Pascal), energy, work, heat, waves.) [S1][S3]

**Skills**
- *Practical/lab:* designing experiments; independent investigation projects; correct use of instruments; safety. [S1][S3]
- *Quantitative/math:* **measurement error**; analysing experimental data; dimensional equations; handling different unit systems. [S1][S3]
- *Representations/models:* choosing between formats (tables, graphs, equations); atomic models as models. [S1]
- *Inquiry/communication:* conclusions that "go beyond the experimental conditions"; rigorous lab reports; digital tools; evaluating sources. [S1][S3]

### Year 11 (1º Bachillerato) [Bachillerato Ciencias y Tecnología]

National FyQ 1º Bach, chemistry blocks A–C [S2]; Madrid additions [S4]. Blocks D–F of the same subject are physics: kinematics, dynamics, energy.

**Concepts**
- *Periodic table:* history of the table (Döbereiner triads, Newlands octaves, Mendeleev and Meyer in Madrid); the table as a predictive tool; **periodic properties: atomic radius, ionisation energy, electron affinity**. [S2][S4]
- *Atomic structure:* electronic structure from **atomic spectra** (interaction with electromagnetic radiation); configuration and group similarities. [S2][S4]
- *Bonding & structure:* stability of atoms and ions → predicting bonds; **Lewis structures**; **molecular polarity**; **intermolecular forces**; molecular vs covalent-network substances; ionic crystals; metallic bonding; structure → properties. [S2][S4]
- *Formulas & nomenclature:* full IUPAC **inorganic** nomenclature of simple substances, ions and compounds, including peroxides and oxoacids and their salts (RSEQ reserves peroxides, P and B oxoacids, acid salts and hydrated salts for Bachillerato). [S2][S16]
- *Stoichiometry & the mole:* **fundamental laws** (Lavoisier, Proust, **Dalton's multiple proportions**); **percentage composition**; molar mass and formula mass; **limiting and excess reagent, purity (*riqueza*), yield**; stoichiometry in industrial processes. [S2][S4]
- *Gases:* **ideal gas law, molar volume, standard/normal conditions, Dalton's law of partial pressures**. [S2][S4]
- *Solutions & concentration:* mass concentration, **molarity, mole fraction**; solution properties (whether colligative properties are covered is unverified). [S2][S4]
- *Chemical reactions & equations:* classification of reactions (synthesis, substitution, double substitution, decomposition, combustion); chemistry and society (environment, drug development). [S2][S4]
- *Energetics & thermodynamics:* **Madrid only**: thermodynamic systems, state variables, **enthalpy**, thermochemical equations and **enthalpy diagrams**, measuring ΔH experimentally, enthalpies of combustion, formation and bond, **Hess's law**. National: thermodynamic variables and heat transfer, taught from the physics side. [S2][S4]
- *Organic chemistry:* properties by **functional group** and **homologous series**; the carbon atom and single, double and triple bonds; IUPAC naming of **mono- and polyfunctional** compounds (hydrocarbons, oxygenated and nitrogenated compounds). [S2][S4]
- *Industrial, environmental & applied:* the chemical industry in society; combustion and sustainability. [S2][S4]

**Skills**
- *Practical/lab:* lab and field work with knowledge of equipment and safety rules (criterion 3.4); observing reaction types and checking their stoichiometry. [S2][S4]
- *Quantitative/math:* rigorous use of different unit systems and their equivalences (criterion 3.1); multi-step stoichiometry. [S2]
- *Representations/models:* **naming and formulating inorganic and organic compounds** (criterion 3.2); moving between representations (criterion 3.3); Lewis diagrams. [S2]
- *Inquiry/communication:* forming and testing hypotheses with several methods (criteria 2.1–2.3); collaborative projects; informed debate on environmental, social and ethical issues (criterion 5.3). [S2]

### Year 12 (2º Bachillerato), Química [Bachillerato Ciencias y Tecnología]

National Química [S2]; Madrid additions [S4]; PAU weighting [S12][S14].

**Concepts**
- *Atomic structure:* **atomic spectra** (emission and absorption; the hydrogen spectrum); **Planck**; the **Bohr model** (postulates, orbit energies, successes and limits); **Heisenberg uncertainty**, wave–particle duality; **orbitals** as probability; **quantum numbers**, **Pauli**, **Hund**, **Aufbau** using the **Moeller diagram**. [S2][S4]
- *Periodic table:* position from configuration; periodic trends in **atomic and ionic radius, ionisation energy, electron affinity, electronegativity**. [S2][S4]
- *Bonding & structure:* bond types and energetics; **Lewis, RPECV (VSEPR), valence-bond theory with hybridisation**; molecular geometry and polarity; **Born–Haber cycle and lattice energy**; metallic bonding with the **electron-sea model and band theory**; **intermolecular forces (hydrogen bonds, dispersion, dipole–dipole)**. [S2][S4]
- *Energetics & thermodynamics:* **first law**; thermochemical equations; **ΔH**; **Hess's law** via ΔHf° or bond energies; **second law, entropy**; **Gibbs energy and spontaneity versus T**. [S2]
- *Kinetics:* **collision theory**; **transition-state theory** (Madrid); **activation energy**; conditions affecting rate; **differential rate law and reaction orders from data**; **Arrhenius equation**; catalysts in industry (Madrid). [S2][S4]
- *Equilibrium:* dynamic equilibrium; **law of mass action**; **Kc, Kp and their relation**; heterogeneous equilibria; **solubility and Ks**; **Le Châtelier and the reaction quotient Q**; industrial relevance. [S2][S4]
- *Acids, bases & salts:* **Arrhenius and Brønsted–Lowry**; strong and weak acids and bases; degree of dissociation; **Kw, pH, Ka, Kb**; conjugate pairs; **salt hydrolysis**; **buffers** (Madrid); neutralisation and **acid–base titrations**; industrial and environmental acids and bases. [S2][S4]
- *Redox & electrochemistry:* oxidation number; oxidant and reductant; **ion-electron method** for balancing; redox stoichiometry and **redox titrations**; electrodes and **standard reduction potential E°**; spontaneity; **galvanic cells**; **electrolysis** of molten and aqueous salts (Madrid); **Faraday's laws**; batteries, electrolytic cells, **fuel cells**, **corrosion** prevention. [S2][S4]
- *Organic chemistry:* **nomenclature of aliphatic and aromatic hydrocarbons, halogen derivatives, alcohols, ethers, aldehydes, ketones, carboxylic acids, esters, amides and amines** (Madrid list); molecular and structural formulas; **structural isomerism** (position, chain, function); **cis–trans and spatial isomers** with 3D models; properties of functional groups; **reaction types: substitution, addition, elimination, condensation, redox**. [S2][S4]
- *Polymers & materials:* **polymerisation** from monomers; structure and properties; classification; applications and **environmental risks**. [S2]
- *Stoichiometry & the mole* (carried over): purity, yield and gas volumes inside PAU problems. [S12]

**Skills**
- *Practical/lab:* titrations (acid–base, redox); electrolysis; lab safety. [S2][S4]
- *Quantitative/math:* equilibrium calculations (degree of dissociation, partial pressures, mole fractions); pH with logarithms; Hess cycles; ΔG = ΔH − TΔS; rate-law orders from tables; Faraday calculations. [S2][S12][S14]
- *Representations/models:* electron configurations and quantum numbers; Lewis structures and geometries; enthalpy and energy-profile diagrams; skeletal and condensed organic formulas; 3D models. [S2][S14]
- *Inquiry/communication:* justifying true/false statements (a common PAU format); reading graphs and data tables, e.g. an ionisation-energy trend; context-based **competency questions** (fertilisers, CO₂ in the atmosphere, fuels). [S12][S14]

## 3. Terminology and notation conventions

- **Nomenclature standard:** IUPAC 2005 inorganic recommendations (*Libro Rojo*, Spanish translation 2007 by Ciriano & Román, Prensas Universitarias de Zaragoza) [S17]. The RSEQ working group published teaching guidance on 17 May 2016 [S16]. It is guidance, not law, but widely followed (unverified how widely). Its main points:
  - ESO: **compositional names with multiplying prefixes** for binaries and hydroxides ("trióxido de dihierro", "dihidróxido de calcio").
  - 4º ESO onwards: **Stock numbers** ("cloruro de hierro(III)", with no space before the bracket) and **charge numbers** ("hierro(2+)", "estaño(2+)").
  - **Accepted common names** for oxoacids and their salts ("ácido sulfúrico", "nitrato", "carbonato"); the *hipo-…-oso / -oso / -ico / per-…-ico* series only for halogen oxoacids, from 4º as enrichment.
  - **Discouraged:** "anhídrido" for non-metal oxides; *-oso/-ico* names for binaries and hydroxides; the old systematic names like "tetraoxosulfato(VI) de hidrógeno".
  - Hydrides: "agua", "amoniaco" (also "azano"), "metano", "silano", **"fosfano", "arsano", "estibano"** (not *fosfina, arsina, estibina*).
  - "Ácido clorhídrico" is used only for HCl dissolved in water; the gas is "cloruro de hidrógeno".
- **Word order:** Spanish names put the anion or electronegative part first: "cloruro de sodio" (NaCl), "óxido de disodio", "sulfato de cobre(II)". Formulas are written in the usual order.
- **Organic names** follow current IUPAC practice with locants next to the suffix: "but-2-eno", "propan-1-ol", "cis-pent-2-eno", as used in the 2025-26 Madrid model paper [S12]. Older textbooks still show "2-buteno". Names are lower case, with Spanish suffixes such as *-ol, -al, -ona, ácido …-oico, -oato de …, -amina, -amida*.
- **Numbers and units:** **decimal comma** ("0,082", "2,5 puntos"); a middle dot between multiplied units and negative exponents ("kJ·mol⁻¹", "atm·L·mol⁻¹·K⁻¹"); SI units plus atm and mmHg (1 atm = 760 mmHg) in gas problems; L for litre; °C and K. Concentration: "molaridad" (mol·L⁻¹, M), "fracción molar", "concentración en masa" (g·L⁻¹), "tanto por ciento en masa". [S4][S12][S14]
- **Symbols:** ΔH°f (often printed "∆Hºf"), Kc, Kp, **Ks** (solubility product), Ka, Kb, Kw, E°, F = 96485 C·mol⁻¹, "EI" (*energía de ionización*), "RPECV" (VSEPR). [S12][S14]
- **Equations and ions:** state symbols (s), (l), (g), (aq/ac) are required in PAU thermochemistry ("indicando el estado de las especies") [S12]. Redox equations are balanced by the "método del ion-electrón", with half-reactions first, then the ionic and molecular equations [S12]. Ion charges are written as superscripts: Sn²⁺, NO₃⁻.
- **False friends and local terms:**
  - *valoración / volumetría* = titration
  - *riqueza* = purity (% by mass)
  - *rendimiento* = yield
  - *reactivo limitante* = limiting reagent
  - *pila* = galvanic cell
  - *cuba electrolítica* = electrolytic cell
  - *disolución* = solution (and *solución* is avoided)
  - *estados de agregación* = states of matter
  - *sistemas materiales* = material systems
  - *leyes ponderales* = the mass laws (Lavoisier, Proust, Dalton)
  - *condiciones normales* = 0 °C and 1 atm, kept separate from *estándar*
  - *diagrama de Moeller* = the Aufbau diagonal rule
  - *número de oxidación / estado de oxidación* = oxidation number/state
  - *enlace covalente dativo / de coordinación* = dative bond
  - *saberes básicos* = content statements
  - *competencias específicas* = subject competences
- **Element names:** "cinc" is used alongside "zinc", and "yodo", "wolframio" (for tungsten) appear in textbooks and exams (spelling habits unverified). In co-official-language communities materials and exams may be in Catalan, Galician, Basque or Valencian [S8 art. 13.8].
