# Chemistry curriculum: Ukraine (Years 7–12)

**Purpose:** input for the cross-country concept/year map for chem-games.
**Research date:** 24 Sept 2026 (desk research by an agent).
**Review status:** not yet checked by a teacher who teaches in Ukraine.
**Key sources:** NUS model programmes "Хімія. 7–9 класи" (Hryhorovych 2023, Lashevska 2023) [S3][S4]; 10–11 programmes, standard and profile levels (2017) [S6][S7]; State Standard for profile secondary education (2024, amended Aug 2026) [S8]; Typical Educational Programme for 10–12 [S9]; MON letter for 2026/27 [S10]; ZNO/NMT chemistry programme and 2026 demo [S15–S17]. Full list at the end.

## Summary for game design

- **Chemistry starts as its own subject in Year 7 (7 клас), at 1 hour a week.** In Years 5–6 it is folded into the integrated course "Пізнаємо природу" / "Природничі науки". Hours rise to 2 h/week in Year 8 and 2.5 h/week in Year 9 [S3][S4].
- **2026/27 is the first year the whole of basic school (Years 5–9) is on НУШ (NUS, New Ukrainian School) programmes.** Years 7, 8 and 9 all use competence-based *модельні навчальні програми* (model programmes). Several of these are approved, and a school picks one. The two main ones are Hryhorovych and Lashevska. They put the same content in **different years** (see Year 7–9 notes), so treat the year placement of Year 7–9 topics as ±1 year [S3][S4][S10].
- **Years 10–11 in 2026/27 still follow the old 2017 programmes.** The *рівень стандарту* (standard level, 1.5 h then 2 h/week) teaches **organic chemistry in Year 10** and **general and inorganic chemistry in Year 11**. That order is unusual. The *профільний рівень* (profile level) gets 4 h then 6 h/week [S6][S7].
- **Reform: the 3-year профільна старша школа (profile upper school, Years 10–12) starts nationally on 1 Sept 2027.** Year 12 first exists nationally in 2029/30 and in pilot lyceums in 2028/29. In 2026/27, 150 pilot lyceums run the new Year 10 [S8][S10][S12][S13]. Under the reform, chemistry as a separate subject exists **only in profiles with in-depth chemistry and biology**: 3 h/week from the second half of Year 10 to Year 12. Everyone else takes an integrated natural-science course in Years 10–11 and has **no science at all in Year 12** [S9].
- **The mole is taught in Year 8** (amount of substance, molar mass, molar volume, Avogadro's law). Reaction-equation mass calculations start in Year 8. Mass fraction of a mixture component already appears in Year 7 in the Hryhorovych programme [S3][S4].
- **Organic chemistry appears twice.** Year 9 (NUS) gives a descriptive tour: methane and the alkanes, ethene and ethyne, alcohols, acetic acid, fats, soap, carbohydrates, proteins and polyethylene. Year 10 gives the systematic course: the theory of structure, isomerism, IUPAC names and functional groups [S3][S4][S6].
- **Nomenclature is a big trap for games.** Element names differ from simple-substance names: **Оксиген** is the element, **кисень** the substance; **Ферум** vs **залізо**. Element names are capitalised in running text. Salts are named "натрій хлорид" (cation name + anion name, no "of"). Acids take adjective names: хлоридна, сульфатна, нітратна кислота. IUPAC organic names are used: етанова кислота, гліцерол, бензен. Standards: ДСТУ 2439-94 and IUPAC [S16].
- **Notation:** decimal comma (2,5). Amount of substance is *n*. н. у. (normal conditions) means 0 °C and 101,3 kPa. Charges and ions are written "йон" (not "іон") in new texts. Oxidation-state notation differs between sources: купрум(ІІ) in exams and the old programmes, купрум(+2) in the Lashevska NUS programme [S4][S16][S17].
- **Wartime context shows up in content.** Lashevska's Year 7 unit covers chemical safety "у зоні … воєнних дій" (in war zones). Year 8 includes radiation protection and a home first-aid kit for nuclear threat. Recommendations stress catching up on lost learning (*надолуження освітніх втрат*) and virtual labs when real experiments are impossible [S4][S10].
- **The national exam is НМТ (National Multi-subject Test).** Chemistry is one of the elective fourth subjects: 24 tasks in 2026. It still tests the **2018 ZNO chemistry programme**, which is built on the 2011 standard: general, inorganic and organic chemistry plus calculations [S15][S16][S17].

**Reforms in force or being phased in for 2026/27**

- **НУШ in basic school (Years 5–9).** It rests on the Державний стандарт базової середньої освіти (State Standard for Basic Secondary Education; Cabinet resolution № 898, 30.09.2020) and the Типова освітня програма (Typical Educational Programme) for 5–9 (MON order № 235, 19.02.2021). That programme was newly amended by **order № 1430 of 17.08.2026**, which gives schools more freedom over hours and catch-up classes. A school must keep the same model programme for the whole 7–9 cycle [S2][S10][S21]. Rollout: the NUS Year-5 cohort reached Year 7 in 2024/25, Year 8 in 2025/26 and Year 9 in 2026/27. Schools in the NUS pilot were one year ahead: "8-мі класи, які є учасниками … Пілоту НУШ" already in 2024/25 [S2 search summary]. The 2026/27 MON letter treats Year 9 as NUS and gives Year-9 NUS chemistry guidance [S10].
- **Chemistry model programmes for 7–9** (all "Рекомендовано МОН"):
  - Hryhorovych, order № 1575 of 27.12.2023 [S3]
  - Lashevska, order № 1001 of 16.08.2023 [S4]
  - Papish, order № 484 of 18.03.2026. This one is new, and its content was not reviewed [S11].
  - Integrated alternative "Природничі науки. 7–9 класи" (Mandrenko et al.) [S5]
- **The old programme "Хімія 7–9" (order № 804, 07.06.2017) is no longer used for Years 7–9 in 2026/27.** It still matters, though: 2026/27 Year 10 and Year 11 students learned their basic-school chemistry from it [S20].
- **Years 10–11 (non-pilot schools)** work from the Typical Educational Programme for level III (order № 408, 20.04.2018, as restated by № 1493, 28.11.2019). Chemistry is taught from the 2017 programmes (order № 1407, 23.10.2017) at standard or profile level, or through the integrated course "Природничі науки" [S6][S7][S10][S22]. Assessment still uses the 12-point criteria of 2011 [S10].
- **Profile upper-school reform ("Профільна")**:
  - State Standard for Profile Secondary Education: Cabinet resolution № 851 of 25.07.2024, amended by № 1000 of 06.08.2026. It applies from **1 Sept 2027** "для здобувачів освіти, які навчаються за програмами дванадцятирічної повної загальної середньої освіти" (for students on 12-year complete secondary programmes).
  - Year 10 becomes a *профільно-адаптаційний* (profile-adaptation) year; Years 11–12 are the *профільний* (profile) cycle.
  - There are two directions: *академічне* (academic) and *професійне* (vocational).
  - Each subject area has a *основний* (basic) and a *поглиблений* (in-depth) level [S8].
  - Pilot: the experimental project (Cabinet resolution № 1058, 26.08.2025) runs from 1 Sept 2025 to 31 Aug 2027. 30 lyceums pre-piloted in 2025/26. **150 lyceums** (order № 437, 11.03.2026) run it from 1 Sept 2026 on the 10–12 Typical Programme; the MON letter cites order № 1674 of 19.12.2025 [S10][S13]. Trials of model programmes and materials in 2026/27 cover **Year 10** [S10].
  - First Year-12 graduates: pilot cohort in 2029 [S14]; nationally in spring/summer 2030 [S12].
- **Wartime.** No formally reduced chemistry programme (*адаптована програма*) for 2026/27 was found. Instead, model programmes let teachers compress or drop items "відповідно до інструктивно-методичних рекомендацій" (in line with the ministry's guidance). The 2026/27 guidance stresses catch-up (*надолуження освітніх втрат*) and virtual or digital experiments [S3][S10].

**National exams that shape content**

- **НМТ** (Національний мультипредметний тест, National Multi-subject Test) replaced ЗНО (the external independent assessment) as the university-entry test in 2022.
  - 2022–2023: 3 subjects, no chemistry.
  - Since 2024 the format is "3+1": Ukrainian, mathematics and history of Ukraine, plus one elective. **Chemistry is an elective in 2026** and is planned for 2027 [S15][S18].
  - Chemistry block in 2026: 24 tasks (18 single-choice, 2 matching, 4 short numeric answers), 0–32 points. It may use the periodic table (short and long forms), the solubility table and the electrochemical activity series [S15][S17].
  - Content follows the **ZNO chemistry programme approved in 2018 (order № 696, applied from 2020)**, which is built on the 2011 standard. Sections: Загальна хімія (general), Неорганічна хімія (inorganic), Органічна хімія (organic), Обчислення в хімії (calculations) [S16].
- **ДПА (state final assessment) at the end of Year 9** is mentioned in the model programmes as the check against the Standard [S4]. Its 2026/27 format was not researched.

**Typical interval and mechanism**

- **State standards change about every 7–10 years:** 2004 → 2011 → 2020 (basic) / 2024 (profile). Each change is driven by **reform and politics** (the 2010 reversal, the post-2014 NUS reform), not by a fixed legal review cycle. No statutory review cycle for chemistry programmes was found (unverified).
- **Chemistry programmes change about every 5–6 years:** 2012 → 2017 → 2023 → 2026 (profile). Since NUS, MON can **add new model programmes at any time** through "гриф" (MON-recommended status) orders, for example March 2026. So the set of valid programmes changes almost yearly, even when the Standard does not.
- **Smaller updates happen every year:** the Typical Educational Programme is amended (2024, 2026); every August MON issues instructive and methodical recommendations; the NMT format is tweaked yearly (subjects in 2022–24, task counts in 2026). Wartime drives flexibility: catch-up hours, virtual labs.
- **The exam content programme is very stable.** The ZNO chemistry programme has not changed since 2018. It is now out of step with NUS: it was built on the 2011 standard, while the first NUS Year-9 cohort finishes basic school in 2027.

**Announced or in progress**

- 1 Sept 2027: national start of the profile Standard in Year 10. 2028/29: Year 11. 2029/30: first Year 12. First national 12-year graduates in 2030; pilot graduates in 2029 [S9][S12][S14].
- NMT 2027: planned to keep "3+1" with chemistry as an elective. Two-day testing and retakes are under discussion (May 2026) [S18]. How admission will work for the 2029 pilot-only graduating cohort is still open [S14].
- No revised NMT or ZNO chemistry programme matching the NUS content has been announced (not found; unverified).

**Stability rating for game content:** *high churn (structural), moderate churn (core content)*. Year placement of topics is moving right now: NUS Years 7–9 differ between model programmes, and Years 10–12 switch model in 2027–2030. The core concepts, though, are stable: mole, bonding, ions, pH, organic families. So games should tag content to concepts with an approximate year range, not to exact years.

### Year 7 (7 клас)

**Concepts**
- *Chemistry as a science, safety:* chemistry as a natural science; lab safety rules; lab equipment and basic operations; measurement, observation, experiment; the scientific method and engineering design [NUS-L]; chemical safety at home, in industry and in emergency or war zones [NUS-L] [S3][S4].
- *Matter and particles:* atoms and molecules as micro-world objects; chemical element; symbols; primary ideas of atomic structure (nucleus + electrons) [NUS-H]. Atomic structure moves to Year 8 in [NUS-L] [S3][S4].
- *Periodic table:* first look at the periodic system; metallic vs non-metallic elements [NUS-H] [S3].
- *Substances:* simple substances (metals and non-metals) vs compounds (*складні речовини*); chemical formulas as "sentences of chemical language" [NUS]; relative atomic and molecular mass [NUS-L]. In [NUS-H] relative masses come in Year 8 [S3][S4].
- *Physical properties:* density, hardness, melting and boiling points, solubility, conductivity; pure substances vs mixtures [NUS] [S3][S4].
- *Mixtures and separation:* homogeneous vs heterogeneous mixtures; solutions, aerosols, suspensions, emulsions, gels (disperse systems); separation by filtration, settling, separating funnel, evaporation, distillation, paper chromatography; mass fraction of a component in a mixture [NUS-H]; waste-management hierarchy [NUS-L] [S3][S4].
- *Chemical change:* physical vs chemical phenomena; signs of reaction; reaction scheme vs chemical equation; law of conservation of mass [NUS-H]. In [NUS-L] reactions and equations are Year 8 [S3][S4].
- *Classification:* classifying substances (simple vs complex, organic vs inorganic) [NUS-L] [S4].

**Skills**
- *Practical:* pouring, mixing and heating in test tubes and porcelain dishes; massing solids and liquids; measuring volumes; collecting gas in a home-made gasometer; the candle-flame structure investigation; separating mixtures; making an emulsion (mayonnaise) [S3][S4].
- *Quantitative:* mass fraction of mixture components [NUS-H]; relative formula mass [NUS-L] [S3][S4].
- *Representations:* 2D and 3D atom models; writing formulas from molecular models and the reverse [NUS-H] [S3].
- *Inquiry and communication:* frame a problem, write a hypothesis, plan, record and present (lapbooks, mind maps, comics about safety rules); product labels (*маркування*) [S3][S4].

### Year 8 (8 клас)

**Concepts**
- *Formulas and nomenclature:* principles for writing formulas and names of binary compounds; oxides and sulfides; systematic names (e.g. of H₂O, CO₂, SO₂) [S3][S4].
- *Stoichiometry and the mole:* Ar and Mr; mass fraction of an element in a compound; deducing formulas of binary compounds from composition; **amount of substance (кількість речовини), the mole, Avogadro's number and constant**, molar mass; mole ratios in reactions [NUS] [S3][S4].
- *Gases:* **Avogadro's law, molar volume**, Gay-Lussac's law of combining volumes; relative density of gases [NUS-L] [S3][S4].
- *Reactions:* combustion and slow oxidation; combination (синтез) and decomposition, e.g. electrolysis of water and decomposition of H₂O₂; energy effect of a reaction; **catalysts**, activation energy [NUS-L]; effect of temperature and light on rate [S3][S4].
- *Air and environment:* composition of air; oxygen (production, catalysts, combustion, the oxygen cycle); ozone and the ozone layer; hydrogen as a fuel; CO₂ and CO, the greenhouse effect, the carbon cycle; oxides reacting with water [NUS-H] [S3].
- *Atomic structure:* protons, neutrons, electrons; isotopes (use and health risk); electron shells and energy levels for the first 20 elements; ions (cations and anions) from gaining or losing electrons; electronegativity, atomic radius [NUS-L]; **oxidation states** [NUS-H] [S3][S4].
- *Periodic table:* **periodic law**; groups; alkali metals, halogens, noble gases [NUS-L]; the link between position and atomic structure [S3][S4].
- *Bonding and structure:* covalent non-polar and polar bonds; electron-dot and structural formulas; valence; bond length [NUS-H]; hydrogen bonds between water molecules [NUS-L]; ionic bonds and ionic crystals; atomic, molecular and ionic lattices (diamond, graphite, buckminsterfullerene, quartz, iodine, ice, NaCl); crystalline vs amorphous [S3][S4].
- *Metals* [NUS-L]: metallic bond; physical properties from structure; electron transfer when metals react with non-metals; extracting metals from oxide and sulfide ores (simplified); alloys; metal waste [S4].
- *Nuclear, applied* [NUS-L]: radioactivity, radionuclides, radiation protection (a wartime context) [S4].

**Skills**
- *Practical:* making and collecting O₂ and H₂; testing the effect of a catalyst; comparing how fast metals react with acids; detecting ozone; examining crystals under a microscope [S3][S4].
- *Quantitative:* calculations with n, M, m and V (molar volume); mass of a product or reactant from an equation; **relative yield** (practical vs theoretical) [NUS-H]; mass fraction of an element; formula from composition [S3].
- *Representations:* electron shells (first 3 periods); electron-dot and structural formulas; 3D digital molecular models; crystal-lattice models; graphic forms of the periodic table [S3][S4].
- *Inquiry and communication:* modelling the greenhouse effect and the ozone layer; a thought experiment comparing numbers of molecules; projects on Skłodowska-Curie and Puluj [S3][S4].

### Year 9 (9 клас) — first all-NUS Year 9 in 2026/27

**Concepts**
- *Solutions:* water as a polar solvent; hydrogen bonding; true solutions; solubility and its temperature dependence; **mass fraction of solute**; crystal hydrates; heat of dissolution and crystallisation; extraction; dissolved gases; protecting water resources [S3][S4].
- *Electrolytes:* electrolytes vs non-electrolytes; **electrolytic dissociation**; strong vs weak electrolytes; degree of dissociation [NUS-L]; acids, bases and salts in terms of dissociation; the conductivity of NaCl solution explained [S3][S4].
- *Acids, bases, salts:* acidic and alkaline solutions; indicators (natural and synthetic); **pH (qualitative, no calculation)**; alkalis as caustic ionic substances; slightly soluble and insoluble hydroxides (Mg, Al, Zn, Cu(+2)) and their thermal decomposition; **amphoterism** (Zn(OH)₂); acids: classification, trivial and systematic names, hydrochloric acid in the body, acid rain; neutralisation; normal, acid and basic salts; writing salt formulas from ion charges [NUS-L] [S3][S4].
- *Ionic reactions:* ion-exchange reactions and conditions for them going to completion; detecting ions; water hardness and softening; ion exchange in water treatment [S3][S4].
- *Redox and electrochemistry* (introductory) [NUS-L]: electron transfer in metal + acid and metal + salt reactions; **activity series of metals**; corrosion protection [S4].
- *Organic chemistry, descriptive* [NUS]: organic vs inorganic; methane (structural formula, tetrahedral shape); alkanes C1–C10 (names, general formula, butane/isobutane isomerism); ethene and ethyne; addition and elimination; complete vs incomplete combustion; halogen derivatives; **polyethylene and polymerisation**; alcohols (methanol, ethanol, glycerol, xylitol and sorbitol, cetyl alcohol); ethanol ⇄ ethene (hydration and dehydration); harm from alcohol; methanoic and ethanoic acids compared with HCl; higher fatty acids; soap and detergents; fats (triglycerides, hydrolysis, hydrogenated fats); glucose, sucrose, starch, cellulose, fermentation; amino acids, proteins (denaturation), peptides [S3][S4].
- *Generalisation:* classes of inorganic compounds and the **genetic links** between them; classifying organic compounds and reactions; links between organic and inorganic [NUS-H]; greenhouse gases [NUS-L] [S3][S4].
- *Careers in chemistry* (Lashevska's Topic 4) [S4].

**Skills**
- *Practical:* making solutions of a given mass fraction; growing crystals; conductivity tests; home-made indicators; pH of foods, gastric juice and mineral water; detecting C, H and Cl in organic compounds; glucose fermentation; starch paste; experimental problems on the genetic links [S3].
- *Quantitative:* mass fraction of solute; solutions in equations; freezing and boiling point shifts (qualitative) [S3].
- *Representations:* dissociation equations; full and net ionic equations; models of mono- and polyatomic ions; structural and 3D organic molecule models; homologous-series tables [S3][S4].
- *Inquiry and communication:* predicting uses from properties (booklets); stain removal with different solvents; the Year 9 ДПА [S3][S4].

### Year 10 (10 клас)

**Concepts (old 2017 programmes, most schools)**
- *Organic review, then theory:* theory of the structure of organic compounds (Butlerov tradition); **isomerism**; C–C single, double and triple bonds; classification of organic compounds [стандарт][профільний] [S6][S7].
- *Hydrocarbons:* alkanes (general formula, structural isomerism, **systematic nomenclature**, chlorination, cracking and isomerisation); alkenes and alkynes (addition of H₂, halogens, HX and water; partial oxidation; polymerisation); **benzene** (structure, properties); preparation and uses; natural gas, oil and coal and their refining [профільний in more depth]; heterocycles (pyridine) [профільний] [S6][S7].
- *Oxygen-containing compounds:* functional (*характеристична*) groups; saturated monohydric alcohols (isomerism of propanols and butanols, H-bonding); glycerol; phenol; aldehydes; carboxylic acids; **esterification**; esters and their hydrolysis; fats; carbohydrates (glucose open-chain form and reactions; sucrose, starch, cellulose hydrolysis) [S6].
- *Nitrogen compounds:* amines as organic bases (methanamine, aniline); amino acids (amphoteric); the peptide group; proteins (biuret and xanthoproteic reactions); nitro compounds [профільний] [S6][S7].
- *Polymers:* polymerisation vs polycondensation; plastics, rubbers, fibres; recycling [S6].
- **[pilot]** In 150 pilot lyceums: an integrated natural-science course (4 h/week, first semester). Chem-bio profiles then take "Хімія. Поглиблений рівень" at 3 h/week from the second semester. Programme content could not be retrieved (see Open items) [S9][S11].

**Skills**
- *Practical:* detecting organic acids in foods; oxidising glucose with fresh Cu(OH)₂; protein colour reactions; experimental problems [S6].
- *Quantitative:* molecular formula from mass fractions; from the general formula plus density or relative density; from the mass, volume or amount of reactants or products; calculations with impure reagents [S6].
- *Representations:* molecular, structural and "semi-structural" (condensed) formulas; 3D models of isomers; IUPAC names of alkanes, alkenes, alkynes, alcohols, aldehydes, acids and esters [S6].
- *Inquiry and communication:* projects such as "isomers in nature" and pesticide use and safety [S6].

### Year 11 (11 клас)

**Concepts (old 2017 programmes)**
- *Atomic structure and the periodic law:* electron and orbital-diagram configurations of s-, p- and d-elements (to Fe) in periods 1–4; the "minimum energy" principle; excited states and valence states; possible oxidation states of period 2–3 non-metals [стандарт]. The profile level adds nuclides, wave–particle duality of the electron and quantum numbers [профільний] [S6][S7].
- *Bonding:* ionic, covalent, metallic and hydrogen bonds; donor–acceptor mechanism (NH₄⁺); crystalline vs amorphous solids [S6].
- *Reactions:* irreversible vs reversible; **chemical equilibrium and Le Chatelier's principle**; **salt hydrolysis** and the pH of salt solutions; **galvanic cell** [стандарт]. The profile level adds enthalpy and thermochemical equations, average rate and the factors affecting it, the law of mass action, the equilibrium constant, radical and electrophilic mechanisms, catalysis and inhibitors, redox equations by electron balance with pH effects, electrolysis, molar concentration [профільний] [S6][S7].
- *Inorganic, descriptive:* non-metals (allotropy of O, S, C, P; adsorption; redox behaviour); hydrogen compounds (HCl, H₂S, NH₃); non-metal oxides in the atmosphere; acids (HNO₃ and concentrated H₂SO₄ with metals); acid rain; metals (general properties; Al and Fe); NaOH and Ca(OH)₂; normal and acid salts; water hardness; silicate materials; mineral fertilisers and soil acidity; biological role of elements [стандарт]. The profile level covers the element groups (H; VIIA; VIA; VA; IVA; IA–IIIA metals; Fe) and corrosion [профільний] [S6][S7].
- *Disperse systems* [профільний]: colloids, the Tyndall effect, coagulation [S7].
- *Chemistry and society:* new materials; food, energy and resource problems [S6].

**Skills**
- *Practical:* pH of salt solutions with indicators; activated charcoal adsorption; **qualitative tests for ions** (Fe²⁺, Fe³⁺, Ba²⁺, NH₄⁺, silicate, phosphate); genetic links between inorganic substances; making a solution of given molar concentration [профільний] [S6][S7].
- *Quantitative:* relative yield; excess-reagent problems; thermochemical calculations, rate and law of mass action [профільний] [S6][S7].
- *Representations:* electron configurations and orbital box diagrams; equilibrium arrows (⇄); hydrolysis equations [S6].
- *Inquiry and communication:* projects such as a potato or lemon battery, battery recycling and liquid crystals [S6].
- *Exam:* the Year 11 cohort sits the NMT; chemistry is an elective [S15].

## 3. Terminology and notation conventions

- **Standard.** Element and simple-substance names follow ДСТУ 2439-94 ("Елементи хімічні, речовини прості. Терміни та визначення"); organic names follow the latest IUPAC recommendations. The NMT uses the same rules [S16].
- **Element vs substance.** Many elements have Latin-root element names that differ from the name of the simple substance:
  - Гідроген / водень (H)
  - Оксиген / кисень (O)
  - Карбон / вуглець (C)
  - Нітроген / азот (N)
  - Сульфур / сірка (S)
  - Ферум / залізо (Fe)
  - Купрум / мідь (Cu)
  - Аргентум / срібло (Ag)
  - Аурум / золото (Au)
  - Плюмбум / свинець (Pb)
  - Станум / олово (Sn)
  - Силіцій (Si)
  - Меркурій (Hg)

  In running text element names are **capitalised** ("Гідроген як найпоширеніший елемент", "Нітроген є окисником"), and substances are lower case ("водень як паливо") [S3][S6][S17]. Games must not translate "oxygen" by one word in both senses.
- **Inorganic compound names:** cation name + anion name with no preposition: натрій хлорид, гідроген хлорид, кальцій карбонат. Variable valence: купрум(ІІ) гідроксид and ферум(ІІІ) хлорид (Roman numerals) in the NMT and 2017 programmes; купрум(+2) хлорид (oxidation state) in the Lashevska NUS programme [S4][S6][S17].
- **Acids** have adjective names: хлоридна (HCl), сульфатна (H₂SO₄), сульфітна, нітратна (HNO₃), нітритна, ортофосфатна (H₃PO₄), карбонатна, силікатна, сульфідна. Trivial names such as соляна кислота appear as "тривіальні назви" [S4][S6][S17].
- **Other preferred forms:** амоніак (NH₃, not аміак); **йон/йонний** ("ion") in NUS texts, while the older 2017 texts mix іон and йон; гліцерол (not гліцерин); етанова кислота (оцтова is trivial); метанова кислота (formic acid); бензен (benzene); етен/етин (ethylene/acetylene); естер (ester); анілін; аміноетанова кислота (glycine) [S3][S4][S6][S17].
- **Units and symbols:**
  - SI units.
  - **Decimal comma** (2,5; 22,4 л/моль).
  - Amount of substance *n* (the older ν is allowed), unit моль; molar mass *M* (г/моль); molar volume *V*ₘ = 22,4 л/моль at **н. у.** (0 °C, 101,3 kPa).
  - Mass fraction *w* (масова частка, often in %); volume fraction φ.
  - Relative atomic and molecular masses *A*ᵣ, *M*ᵣ.
  - Heat effect ΔH [S16].
  - Molar concentration appears only at profile level [S7].
- **Equations:** "→" for reactions and "⇄" for reversible ones (the NMT uses ⇄ for dissociation of CH₃COOH). Students write full and net ("скорочене") ionic equations and dissociation equations. Redox equations are balanced by electron balance (*електронний баланс*) [S7][S17].
- **Reference tables that students are expected to read:**
  - Таблиця розчинності (solubility table), with р / м / н = soluble / slightly soluble / insoluble at 20–25 °C
  - Ряд активності металів (activity series)
  - Periodic table in **short form** (with A/B subgroup columns) **and long form**, both used in exams [S15][S16]
- **Words that differ from English usage:**
  - *складна речовина* means "compound" (literally "complex substance")
  - *проста речовина* means "element as a substance"
  - *валентність* (valence) is still taught alongside oxidation state
  - *генетичні зв'язки* means the "genetic links" between classes of compounds (conversion chains)
  - *характеристична група* means "functional group"
  - *кристалічні ґратки* means "crystal lattice" (with the letter ґ)
  - *н. у.* means STP at 0 °C
  - "Дослідження" means "investigation"
