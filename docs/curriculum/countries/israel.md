## Summary for game design

- **Chemistry has no subject of its own before Year 10.** In Years 7–9 it is the "חומרים" (materials/matter, labelled chemistry) strand of the integrated subject **מדע וטכנולוגיה** (Science and Technology). S&T gets about 14 weekly-hour units spread over the three middle-school years. Chemistry is roughly 30–45 teaching hours per year of that.
- **The spiral in middle school:** Y7 is macroscopic properties, states of matter, the particle model and conservation of mass (physical change only). Y8 adds atoms, the periodic table, elements/compounds/mixtures, separation and chemical reactions as word and formula equations, **without balancing**. Y9 covers covalent and ionic bonding, bond energy, carbon compounds, food molecules, polymers, isotopes and radioactivity (extension), and acids, bases and pH (extension).
- **High school splits students sharply.** A **5-unit chemistry major (כימיה 5 יח"ל, the "מגמה")** runs through Years 10–12, with a minimum of 15 weekly-hour units over three years. Everyone else takes **one year (3 h/week, 90 h, usually Year 10) of "מבוא למדעים"** (Intro to Sciences), which can be "מבוא לכימיה" (Intro to Chemistry). Other options are Science & Technology for All (מדע וטכנולוגיה לכל) or another science. No **3-unit** chemistry exam is currently listed (see §5).
- **The 5-unit course is heavy in Year 11.** Exams are 70% external and 30% school-based. The 70% is a 55% written or computer-based exam plus a 15% **oral exam on the inquiry lab (מעבדת החקר)**. Mole calculations, redox, acids/bases, food chemistry (fatty acids, triglycerides) and energetics/rates typically land in Year 11. Equilibrium, entropy, sugars and 1–2 **electives (מבניות בחירה)** land in Year 12.
- **Food chemistry and biochemistry are unusually prominent.** Examples are cis/trans fatty acids, triglycerides, glucose anomers (Haworth/Fischer) and the glycosidic bond. The same is true of **"levels of understanding" (רמות הבנה: macroscopic, microscopic and symbolic)**, which exam answers are explicitly marked against.
- **Some electives go well beyond typical secondary level:** MO theory, HOMO/LUMO, chromophores, band theory and LEDs; SN1/SN2/E1/E2 mechanisms; Gibbs energy; DNA and the genetic code. The **bromine (Dead Sea industry) elective was taught for the last time in 2025/26.**
- **Watch for RTL/LTR mixing.** Hebrew (and Arabic in Arab-sector schools) runs right to left, but formulas, equations, units and state symbols are Latin script and run left to right, embedded in the RTL sentence. Compound names follow a **traditional Hebrew adjectival pattern**, e.g. נתרן כלורי ("sodium chloride-ic") for NaCl and פחמן דו-חמצני for CO₂. They are not literal IUPAC translations. Games must keep the formula LTR and never mirror it.
- **Content changes every year.** Since 2020 (COVID, then the war from Oct 2023), the superintendent has issued a **yearly exam focus/update (מיקוד / עדכון תכנים)** that removes and returns subtopics. Summer 2026 also brought a national cut of about 20% in exam material. Treat fine-grained "is X examined this year" details as volatile. The core concept list is stable (see §1a).

Notes:
- School starts in grade א (1) at about age 6, so Year N = grade N. The school year runs from 1 Sept to June. 2026/27 is **תשפ"ז**.
- "ש"ש" (שעות שבועיות) = weekly hours. The 5-unit total of 15 ש"ש means 15 weekly-hour units summed over three years [S3].
- **Arab-sector schools** (ממלכתי ערבי, Bedouin, Druze) follow the same state curriculum and בגרות in Arabic. The ministry publishes Arabic versions of the chemistry appendices (formula glossary, levels of understanding, reaction sheet, exam insights) and Arabic bonding slides [S1]. **Reforms in force or being phased in for 2026/27**
- **70-30 adapted curriculum (התוכנית המותאמת)** in 5-unit chemistry, in force since 2014/15 (תשע"ה) [S1]. It came out of the national "למידה משמעותית" (meaningful learning) programme, which sets 70% external and 30% school-based assessment [S15, S20].
- **Yearly exam focus/update.** The latest is the 2025/26 "עדכון תכנים לבחינה", dated 12 Aug 2025 and valid "for students examined in תשפ"ו only" [S2]. It returned some topics dropped in earlier years, continued a policy of reducing the **amount of written text required** in exam answers, and stopped requiring microscopic verbal descriptions. **No 2026/27 update was found** (open item).
- **Bromine elective withdrawn.** "ברום ותרכובותיו" was taught for the last time in 2025/26 [S2]. Six electives remain unless a replacement is announced **(unverified)**.
- **Climate change** has been compulsory since 2022/23: 20 h inside Year-10 Intro to Sciences / Intro to Chemistry, and 10 h in Year-8 S&T [S3, S7, S15].
- **"ישראל ריאלית"** (STEM-focus programme). 2025/26 was its first focus year. Middle-school S&T content specifications received "slight changes" for 2025/26 [S10].
- **Summer 2026 national exam accommodations** after war disruption: about 20% less examinable material (through more question choice and focus lists), +15% time for all, and oral language exams turned internal. Announced March 2026 [S17] **(partially verified: news headline/summary only)**. Whether this carries into 2026/27 is unknown.

**National exams that shape content**
- **בגרות (matriculation) in 5-unit chemistry**, main questionnaire code 037580 [S8]:
  - **55%**: theory exam, either written (037381) or computer-based and media-rich (037387, "מתוקשבת עתירת מדיה"). Section 1: eight multiple-choice questions (20 pts) plus one compulsory **unseen scientific article** question (20 pts, chemical literacy). Section 2: answer 3 of 5 open questions (20 pts each) [S8]. Students get a formula sheet. Since 2016/17, up to 5 points per exam may be higher-order or values questions (עמ"ר) [S1].
  - **15%**: external **oral exam** on the inquiry lab (037388) or on a "mini-research level 3" project (037376), at the end of Y12 [S3, S8].
  - **30%**: school-based assessment (037283), covering the 30% content plus either Part 1 of the inquiry lab or a second elective [S1, S3].
- **Intro to Chemistry** (037183): 100% internal [S8]. External and repeat candidates have their own syllabi (037182 = 1 unit) [S1].
- There is **no national exam in Years 7–9**. Middle-school S&T is school-assessed. National RAMA/"Meitzav" tests exist **(not researched)**.

- **Typical interval between major revisions:** about 15–20 years for frameworks (middle-school S&T 1996 → 2016; high-school chemistry reform early 2000s → 70-30 in 2014/15), with smaller modular edits in between. **No statutory review cycle was found.** Revisions are made by subject committees (ועדת מקצוע, with academics such as the Weizmann and Technion groups) and are triggered by ministerial reforms (Tomorrow 98, meaningful learning, "ישראל ריאלית") or by crises (COVID, war).
- **Smaller updates:** **every year.** They come as the superintendent's annual circular (חוזר מפמ"ר) and an exam focus/update document that strikes out or restores specific subtopics and changes exam emphasis. Formula sheets and "exam insights" (תובנות) documents are also reissued yearly [S1, S2, S3, S4].
- **Announced or in progress now:** the bromine elective ends; the "ישראל ריאלית" STEM push continues; war-related exam relief may continue. Nothing chemistry-specific for 2026/27 could be confirmed.
- **Stability rating: moderate churn.** The core concept sequence (particles → atoms and periodic table → bonding → mole → redox, acids and bases → energetics, rates and equilibrium) has been stable for over 10 years. Exam-level detail, electives and "not examined this year" strike-outs change every year.

## 2. Year-by-year concepts and skills

### Year 7 (כיתה ז, age 12, middle school, S&T "חומרים")
**Concepts**
- Matter & particles: body (גוף) vs substance (חומר); mass and volume and their measurement; density as a concept only, with no calculations [extension]; common properties of solids, liquids and gases; air as a mixture (composition, atmosphere); identifying O₂, CO₂ and H₂ (demonstrations); properties of water and its freezing-point anomaly [extension]; metals: expansion and conduction [extension] [S5]
- Matter & particles (particle model, 13 h): matter is discontinuous; particles in constant motion with empty space between; particle description of the three states (spacing, arrangement, motion, attractive forces); changes of state explained with particles [S5]
- Chemical reactions: **physical change** only (shape, heating/cooling → temperature, volume and pressure change); changes of state (melting/freezing, evaporation/boiling/condensation, sublimation/deposition); melting and boiling points identify substances; factors affecting evaporation rate; **conservation of mass in physical change** [S5]
- Industrial, environmental & applied: benefits and environmental cost of materials (plastics, fuel, rock); renewable vs non-renewable resources [extension]; choosing materials in a design process (תהליך התיכון) [S5]

**Skills**
- Practical/lab: measuring mass (balance) and volume (graduated tools) of solids, liquids and gases; compressing air in a syringe; safe lab work [S5]
- Quantitative/math: units of mass (mg, g, kg, t) and volume; no density calculations [S5]
- Representations/models: particle diagrams for the states; pie chart of air composition [S5]
- Inquiry/communication: plan an experiment (e.g. ranking liquids by density, factors in evaporation); make claims with justification (טיעון); engineering design process [S5]

### Year 8 (כיתה ח, age 13, middle school, S&T "חומרים")
**Concepts**
- Atomic structure: element as identical atoms; nucleus (p, n) and electron "cloud"; charge and relative mass of subatomic particles; atomic number; neutral atom p = e; electrical forces; free electrons in metals [S5]
- Periodic table (7 h): rows by atomic number; columns as families (alkali metals, halogens, noble gases); metals vs non-metals; symbols for 16 common elements (H, He, C, N, O, Cl, Br, I, S, P, Na, Mg, Ca, Zn, Fe, Cu); carbon allotropes (graphite, diamond, fullerene) [S5]
- Bonding & structure (intro): particle types (atoms, molecules, ions); chemical bond as atoms "stuck together"; molecule; ionic bond as attraction of opposite ions in a lattice (NaCl) [S5]
- Formulas & nomenclature: a formula shows constituents and ratio (H₂O, CO₂, glucose, HCl, NaCl, MgO, CuBr₂, Na₂O, Fe₂O₃); ion symbols [S5]
- Mixtures & separation (6 h): pure substances vs mixtures; homogeneous (solution, alloy, air) vs heterogeneous mixtures; composite materials; dissolving (solvent, solute, solution); concentrated vs dilute (qualitative only); rate of dissolving (temperature, stirring); separation by distillation and chromatography; desalination [S5]
- Chemical reactions (7 h): the chemical process (reactants → products); synthesis, decomposition (electrolysis of CuCl₂/ZnBr₂/water), combustion; recognising a chemical change; word and formula equations **without balancing**; endothermic examples [extension]; **conservation of mass in chemical change** [S5]
- Acids, bases & salts [extension]: sour/bitter; litmus; pH scale 0–14; neutralisation as mutual cancelling [S5]
- Industrial, environmental & applied: combustion for energy; environmental cost of fuels (5 h) [S5]; climate change 10 h (with geography) [S3]

**Skills**
- Practical/lab: forming oxides (rust, burning Mg on sand), electrolysis of CuCl₂, heating sugar, paper chromatography of pen inks, testing solvents (water/oil/acetone), mass before and after burning steel wool [S5]
- Quantitative/math: mass-balance "equation" (mass of reactants = mass of products) [S5]
- Representations/models: classify particle diagrams (atoms of an element, molecules of an element or compound, ionic lattice); macro vs micro sorting; atom-structure models [S5]
- Inquiry/communication: design an experiment on dissolving rate or to test conservation of mass; information from formulas and diagrams [S5]

### Year 9 (כיתה ט, age 14, middle school, S&T "חומרים")
**Concepts**
- Bonding & structure (10 h): electrical attraction and repulsion as the basis of bonding; covalent (non-metal/non-metal) and ionic (metal/non-metal) bonds; outer electrons, same group = same bonding capacity (valency); octet in small molecules (CH₄, NH₃, H₂O, HF) [S5]
- Energetics & thermodynamics: bond forming releases energy and bond breaking needs energy; exo/endothermic processes explained by bond energies (qualitative) [S5]
- Chemical reactions: balancing simple equations (H₂ + O₂ → H₂O) as a consequence of conservation of mass [extension] [S5]
- Nuclear chemistry [extension]: isotopes, mass number (D, C-14); α, β and γ decay changes the element; mass defect and E = mc² (qualitative) [S5]
- Organic chemistry (12 h): uniqueness of carbon (4 bonds, 3-D [extension], double and triple bonds [extension]); allotropes and nanotubes; organic compounds in living things; crude oil and fractional distillation [extension] [S5]
- Biochemistry: carbohydrates (glucose ring, sucrose, starch), proteins (amino acids), fats, cholesterol; cellular respiration as a chemical process; calorimeter and food energy (kcal vs kJ) [S5]
- Polymers & materials: food molecules as polymers; synthetic polymers from petrochemicals; compulsory preparation of one synthetic polymer (slime/"silly putty" or nylon) [S5]
- Acids, bases & salts [extension]: acid = excess H₃O⁺ in water, base = excess OH⁻; indicators (litmus, pH paper, red cabbage); non-metal oxide + water → acid (SO₃ → H₂SO₄), metal oxide → base; acid rain; neutralisation H₃O⁺ + OH⁻ → 2H₂O; HCl(g) is not an acid until dissolved [S5]
- Industrial, environmental & applied: industry making carbon compounds (food, drugs, plastics, fuels, dyes, detergents, fertilisers); benefits and environmental costs (6 h) [S5]

**Skills**
- Practical/lab: exo/endothermic experiments (H₂O₂ decomposition, citric acid + baking soda, steel wool + copper salt); food tests (Benedict/Fehling, iodine, biuret, grease spot); solubility of sugars and fats; heating egg white [S5]
- Quantitative/math: simple balancing; food energy [S5]
- Representations/models: electron-dot style bonding for small molecules; ball-and-stick models of carbon allotropes (plasticine and toothpicks) [S5]
- Inquiry/communication: plan and run tests for starch and fat; measure rain acidity; simulated acid rain on plants; read nutrition labels; web search on number of organic compounds [S5]

### Year 10 (כיתה י, age 15, upper secondary)
Two parallel tracks. Grade placement inside the 5-unit course follows the ministry's suggested sequences [S14]; schools may reorder.

**Concepts — 5-unit major [5 יח"ל], typical Year 10 content**
- Matter & particles: states of matter at the **three levels** (macroscopic, microscopic, symbolic); melting and boiling points; pure substance, element, compound; homogeneous and heterogeneous mixture; conservation of matter [S2]
- Formulas & nomenclature / Chemical reactions: element symbols; writing and balancing equations; complete vs incomplete combustion [S2]
- Atomic structure: p, n, e; atomic and mass number; isotopes; radioactivity (α, β, γ: composition, charge, penetration; effect on Z and A in a given equation; no writing of nuclear equations) [S2]
- Periodic table: groups and periods; metals and non-metals; named families (alkali, alkaline-earth, halogens, noble gases); electron arrangement in energy levels up to Z = 20; valence electrons; "orbital" (term); Coulomb's law; atomic radius and first ionisation energy (qualitative factors: nuclear charge, number of shells); monatomic ions and their position in the table [S2]
- Bonding & structure: covalent bond (non-polar/polar; single, double, triple); electronegativity (values given); partial charges; bond energy and bond length (factors); molecular formula, Lewis ("נוסחת ייצוג אלקטרונים"), condensed and full structural formulas; identifying isomers; shapes (tetrahedral, trigonal pyramidal, bent, trigonal planar, linear; known, not derived); polarity of molecules with one central atom; hydrocarbons; identifying functional groups (C=C, hydroxyl, carboxyl, amine, ether, ketone, aldehyde, ester, amide) with no reactions; van der Waals interactions and hydrogen bonds; boiling point and solubility comparisons; atomic (network) solids (diamond, graphite, Si, SiO₂); ionic lattice, empirical formula, hydrated ions, soluble vs insoluble salts, net ionic precipitation equations; metallic "sea of electrons", malleability, alloys [S2]
- Stoichiometry & the mole (may start in Y10 or Y11): mole, Avogadro's number, molar mass [S2, S14]

**Concepts — Intro to Chemistry (מבוא לכימיה) [non-majors, 90 h]**
- Required: states of matter at three levels; materials and "the chemists' language"; atomic structure incl. radioactivity; periodic table; structure and bonding part A (molecular substances); energetics and dynamics (exo/endothermic, reaction rate) [S7]
- Two of three electives: structure and bonding part B (atomic, ionic and metallic lattices and properties); carbon compounds "on taste and smell" (hydrocarbons, oil distillation, alcohols, aldehydes, esters as flavours and fragrances, polymers and recycling, ethanol's effects on the body and drink-driving, ethanol as fuel); acids and bases (definitions, water as acid/base, indicators, neutralisation, pH, body acidity, acid rain, ocean acidification) [S7]
- Industrial, environmental & applied: climate change, 20 h compulsory (fuel cells, energy storage, carbon capture) [S7, S15]
- [מוט"ל track] S&T for All module "חומרים בחיי היום יום" (materials in everyday life); contents not retrieved [S9]

**Skills**
- Practical/lab [5 יח"ל]: two **level-I inquiry experiments** (guided) [S14]; safety rules (the ministry publishes lab-safety guidelines) [S1]
- Quantitative/math [5 יח"ל]: mass ↔ moles ↔ molar mass [S2]
- Representations/models: switching between macro, micro and symbolic descriptions; Lewis structures of molecules, atoms and monatomic ions; structural formulas; graphs and tables [S2, S6]
- Inquiry/communication: observation vs interpretation; results, explanation, conclusions; literacy tasks built on everyday contexts (popcorn, pickles, cycling) [Intro] [S2, S7]

### Year 11 (כיתה יא, age 16, 5-unit major only)
**Concepts [5 יח"ל]** (the heaviest year; in the "exam at end of Y11" sequence all of the 70% core is finished here [S14])
- Stoichiometry & the mole: balanced equations, mole ratio, reaction calculations **without limiting reagent** (except partial neutralisation) [S2]
- Solutions & concentration: molar concentration; moles = c × V [S2]
- Gases: PV = nRT with R = 0.082 L·atm·mol⁻¹·K⁻¹ (P in atm, V in L or mL, T in K); °C ↔ K; qualitative effects of T, n and V on P and V; Avogadro's hypothesis; molar volume; molecular formula of a gas [S2]
- Redox & electrochemistry: oxidant and reductant; oxidation and reduction; relative activity of metals and the electrochemical series (not memorised); metal + metal-ion reactions; corrosion factors (O₂, humidity, temperature) and protection (coating, environment, cathodic protection); **oxidation numbers**, incl. carbon in organic compounds from structural formulas and max/min values; balancing simple redox equations; moles of electrons transferred; antioxidants as reducing agents; free radicals [S2]
- Acids, bases & salts: Brønsted–Lowry acid and base; indicators (no colours memorised); acid–water and base–water reactions from the official **reaction sheet** (appendix 4); carboxylic acids RCOOH and primary amines RNH₂; water as acid and base; pH scale (range only, **no calculation**); full and partial neutralisation [S2]
- Biochemistry (food chemistry, "כימיה של מזון"): food groups; calorific value calculation; water- vs fat-soluble vitamins; saturated and unsaturated fatty acids; cis/trans isomers (**draw them**); melting-point factors (chain length, unsaturation, cis/trans); essential fatty acids; triglycerides (esterification, hydrolysis, hydrogenation) [S2]
- Energetics & thermodynamics: internal, potential and kinetic energy; temperature vs energy; system and surroundings; open, closed and isolated systems; ΔH, exo/endothermic, energy diagrams; ΔH° tied to the written equation; kJ and J; enthalpies of fusion, vaporisation and sublimation; **Hess's law**; ΔH from **bond enthalpies** (all-gas reactions only) [S2]
- Kinetics: reaction rate; collision model; activation energy; activated complex; factors (concentration, temperature, surface area, nature of reactants); catalyst (no types); read rate data and graphs [S2]

**Skills**
- Practical/lab [5 יח"ל]: inquiry lab **level II partial and level II full** experiments [S14]; Part 2 of the inquiry lab is examined externally in the oral exam [S1, S2]
- Quantitative/math: mole, concentration and gas-law calculations with the official formula sheet; Hess's law and bond-enthalpy sums; calorific value [S2]
- Representations/models: condensed fatty-acid notation (e.g. C18:2 ω6 cis,cis; numbering from the methyl end, per the ministry glossary) [S16] **(ω symbol reconstructed from a damaged text extract; check)**; energy-profile graphs [S2]
- Inquiry/communication: unseen-article reading questions (chemical literacy) in the exam [S8]; reduced demand for long verbal explanations in structure and bonding [S2, S4]

### Year 12 (כיתה יב, age 17, 5-unit major only)
**Concepts [5 יח"ל]** (the 30% school-assessed part, plus any 70% topics deferred from Y11)
- Biochemistry (sugars) [30%]: glucose in Haworth and Fischer projections (structure given; D-isomer); carbon numbering; anomers (draw them) and mutarotation (no mechanism); glucose isomers (6-ring only); disaccharides and the glycosidic bond; hydrolysis; polysaccharides (cellulose as structure, starch and glycogen as storage; linkage position) [S2]
- Equilibrium [30%]: reversible, dynamic equilibrium in homogeneous systems; K_c calculation; reaction quotient Q; effect of concentration, temperature and catalyst (Le Chatelier or Q vs K); K vs temperature for exo/endothermic reactions [S2]
- Energetics & thermodynamics [30%]: entropy as spreading of energy and particles; ΔS sign from phase and gas-mole changes; ΔS°system from tables; ΔS°surroundings = −ΔH°/T; second law via ΔS°universe (no inversion temperature) [S2]
- **Electives (מבניות בחירה):** one elective with inquiry-lab Part 1, or two electives without it [S1, S2]:
  - Polymers: addition (1,2 only) and condensation polymerisation; polyesters, polyamides; LDPE/HDPE; copolymers; Tg, Tm, crystallinity; thermoplastics, thermosets, elastomers, cross-links; fibres; recycling [S2]
  - Physical chemistry: spectra, photon energy, Bohr (qualitative), s/p orbitals, Pauli, Hund; colour; **MO theory** (bond order, σ/π); HOMO/LUMO, chromophores; band theory, doping, PN junction, LED [S2]
  - Advanced organic: functional families; **optical isomerism** (chirality, enantiomers, racemates); SN1/SN2/E1/E2 and addition mechanisms; nucleophiles, leaving groups, solvents; reaction order; curly arrows; **no systematic naming required** [S2]
  - Biochemistry: amino acids (zwitterion, pKa, pI); peptides; protein structure levels, denaturation; DNA/RNA structure, transcription, translation, genetic code, mutation [S2]
  - Environmental chemistry: water analysis (spectrophotometry and Beer–Lambert, titration, dilution, blank); purification (activated carbon, ion exchange, distillation, redox); greenhouse effect [S2]
  - Energetics & Dynamics stage 2: first law ΔU = q + w; state functions; third law; ΔG = ΔH − TΔS; ΔG and K (qualitative) [S2]
  - Bromine and its compounds (Dead Sea industry, flow diagrams, yield and conversion, biocides, flame retardants): **last taught 2025/26, so not available in 2026/27** [S2]

**Skills**
- Practical/lab [5 יח"ל]: the **inquiry lab (מעבדת החקר)**. Minimum experiments by level: I, II partial, II full, and optionally a III mini-research project with a "rolling" second question (שאלה מתגלגלת). Stages: scientific background → asking questions → research question linking two variables → hypothesis → design (control, constants, ≥ 4 setups) → equipment list → safe execution → tables and flow charts → graphs (Excel, sensors or mm paper) → trends → explanation → conclusions → critical discussion (accuracy, limits, validity) → ≥ 3 sources → lab report. Oral external exam at the end of Y12 [S2, S3]
- Quantitative/math: K_c, ΔS and ΔG calculations; yield and conversion [bromine]; pKa/pI [biochemistry elective] [S2]
- Representations/models: Haworth and Fischer projections; polymer repeat units; MO diagrams; curly-arrow mechanisms; wedge formulas [S2]
- Inquiry/communication: accurate scientific language; source citation; optional **final thesis (עבודת גמר)** in chemistry [S1, S8]

## 3. Terminology and notation conventions

- **Language and direction:** teaching and exams are in Hebrew (Arabic in the Arab sector), both right-to-left. Formulas, equations, units, state symbols and variable names are **Latin script, left-to-right**, embedded in RTL text, e.g. `NaCl(s) → Na⁺(aq) + Cl⁻(aq)` inside a Hebrew sentence [S6]. Ministry PDFs show frequent bidi breakage (brackets flipped, numbers moved). Games need Unicode bidi isolation (`<bdi>`/`dir="ltr"` spans) around every formula. Hebrew word equations run RTL, with reactants on the right **(unverified; check with a teacher)**.
- **Element names:** many are Hebrew coinages: מימן H, חמצן O, פחמן C, חנקן N, נתרן Na, אשלגן K, סידן Ca, גופרית S, זרחן P, צורן Si, ברזל Fe, נחושת Cu, אבץ Zn, כסף Ag, זהב Au. Others are transliterated: כלור, ברום, יוד, הליום, מגנזיום [S5, S2].
- **Inorganic compound names** use a traditional adjectival pattern (metal/cation noun + anion adjective), not literal IUPAC: נתרן כלורי NaCl, מגנזיום חמצני MgO, נחושת כלורית CuCl₂, אבץ ברומי ZnBr₂, סידן ברומי CaBr₂, נתרן ברומטי NaBrO₃, אשלגן על-מנגני KMnO₄, מימן כלורי HCl, פחמן דו-חמצני CO₂, צורן חמצני SiO₂, חומצה גופרתית H₂SO₄ [S2, S5]. The official formula glossary (נספח 1, "מונחון לנוסחאות") defines the accepted representations [S16].
- **Organic names:** transliterated IUPAC-style stems (אתאנול, פנטאן, אלקאנים/אלקנים) plus trivial names (חומצה סטארית, חומצה לינולאית). The advanced-organic elective states that students **do not need systematic names** [S2, S16].
- **Representations with fixed names:** נוסחה מולקולרית (molecular formula); נוסחת ייצוג אלקטרונים (Lewis/electron-dot; a pair may be two dots or a line); נוסחת מבנה – ייצוג מלא / ייצוג מקוצר (full vs condensed structural formula); רישום מקוצר for fatty acids (e.g. C18:2 ω6 cis,cis) [S16]; "שפת הכימאים" (the chemists' language = symbols and formulas); **רמות הבנה**: מאקרוסקופית / מיקרוסקופית / סמל [S6].
- **Key terms:** ניסוח (writing an equation), איזון (balancing), מגיבים/תוצרים (reactants/products), סתירה (neutralisation), חמצון-חיזור (redox), דרגת חמצון (oxidation number), מחמצן/מחזר (oxidant/reductant), מסה מולרית, ריכוז מולרי (M), קשר קוולנטי/יוני/מתכתי, קשרי מימן, אינטראקציות ון-דר-ולס (abbreviated ו.ד.ו.), חומר אטומרי (network covalent solid), סריג (lattice), יונים ממוימים (hydrated ions), אנתלפיה, אנטרופיה, שיווי משקל, קצב תגובה, אנרגיית שפעול (activation energy), תצמיד משופעל (activated complex), זרז (catalyst) [S2].
- **Units and numbers:** decimal **point** (0.082); SI plus atm; V in L or mL; T in K and °C; energy in kJ/J (food labels in Israel use kcal; the Y9 curriculum notes kilocalorie vs kJ) [S2, S5]; concentration in M (mol/L); standard sign ΔH°. State symbols (s), (l), (g), (aq).
- **Abbreviations:** יח"ל (study units), ש"ש (weekly hours), מפמ"ר (national subject superintendent), בגרות (matriculation), מוט"ל (S&T for All), חט"ב/חט"ע (middle/upper secondary).
