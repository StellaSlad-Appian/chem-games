# Chemistry curriculum: Italy (Years 7–12)

**Purpose:** input for the cross-country concept/year map for chem-games.
**Research date:** 24 Sept 2026 (desk research by an agent).
**Review status:** not yet checked by a teacher who teaches in Italy.
**Key sources:** MIM (Ministero dell'istruzione e del merito): DM 254/2012 and DM 221/2025 (primo ciclo), DI 211/2010 *Indicazioni nazionali per i licei*, *Linee guida* for istituti tecnici (2010/2012), DM 29/2026 (tecnici reform), the draft *Nuove Indicazioni nazionali per i licei* (April 2026), and school curricula as evidence of what schools typically teach in each year. Full list in section 6.

## Summary for game design

- **No national chemistry syllabus year by year before upper secondary.** In *scuola secondaria di primo grado* (Years 6–8, "scuola media") chemistry sits inside **Scienze**, which shares one teacher and 6 h/week with maths ("Matematica e scienze"). National documents set targets only for the **end of Year 8**. Schools decide which year gets which topic. In practice the main chemistry block (atoms, molecules, elements, reactions, acids/bases with indicators) usually lands in **Year 7 (2ª media)** [S4, S7].
- **Two primo-ciclo curricula run side by side in 2026/27.** The *Nuove Indicazioni nazionali 2025* (DM 221/2025) apply only to **1ª media (Year 6)** this year. Years 7 and 8 still follow the 2012 *Indicazioni*. The 2012 text stops applying to scuola media from **2028/29** [S1, S2].
- **Upper secondary (Years 9–13) is split into tracks from age 14.** In the **liceo scientifico**, chemistry is part of "Scienze naturali" (biology + chemistry + Earth science) at 2-2-3-3-3 h/week, shared across the three sciences. It is **macroscopic and descriptive in Years 9–10**. The formal core (atomic models, periodic table, bonding, nomenclature, mole/stoichiometry, then equilibrium, acid–base, redox) lands in **Years 11–12**. **Organic chemistry and biochemistry come in Year 13** [S8, S11, S12].
- **The istituti tecnici move much faster.** The 2010 *Linee guida* put almost all of general chemistry (mole, gas laws, orbitals, bonding, IUPAC nomenclature, solutions, stoichiometry, equilibrium, Le Chatelier, pH and buffers, redox and electrochemistry, intro organic) into **Years 9–10** under "Scienze integrate (Chimica)", 3 h/week. In the specialist track **Chimica, materiali e biotecnologie (CMB)**, Years 11–13 then add analytical/instrumental, organic (mechanisms, R/S, E/Z) and industrial chemistry [S13, S14].
- **Several reforms are being phased in at once.** (a) The new tecnici (DM 29/2026) started with **first-year classes in 2026/27**. Chemistry in Years 9–10 becomes part of one integrated subject, "**Scienze sperimentali**", plus "Chimica applicata" in CMB. The new *Linee guida* with knowledge and skills **have not been published yet** [S15, S16]. (b) The new *Indicazioni nazionali per i licei* are a draft regulation: the CSPI gave its opinion in July 2026, and they are planned to start with first-year classes in **2027/28** [S17, S18]. (c) The new primo ciclo curriculum (above).
- **Exam leverage.** The *Esame di maturità* (renamed by L. 164/2025) takes place at the end of **Year 13**. Chemistry is a **written** paper only in the tecnico CMB track (second paper: e.g. "Chimica analitica e strumentale"). In the liceo scientifico the second paper is maths. INVALSI tests have **no science** [S19–S21].
- **Notation.** Decimal comma (6,022·10²³). Italian IUPAC names are used, but the **traditional names are still widely taught next to them** (*anidride carbonica*, *acido solforoso*, *ossido ferrico*, *solfato rameico*). Italian state-change words include *brinamento* (deposition) and *sublimazione*. Games must support Italian names such as *idrossido*, *ossiacido*, *idracido*, *miscuglio*.
- **Surprise:** the draft new liceo text (April 2026) is **thinner** than the 2010 one. Its chemistry objectives for Years 11–12 drop explicit mention of redox, electrochemistry and thermochemistry, and keep stoichiometry, nomenclature, the periodic table, bonding, equilibrium, pH and rates [S17]. If it is adopted unchanged, the depth of liceo chemistry after 2027/28 is uncertain.

**Track contrasts for chemistry**
- **Liceo scientifico** (the reference track here): Scienze naturali every year. The 2010 text for Years 11–12 adds "cenni di elettrochimica" and stresses quantitative calculations [S8].
- **Opzione scienze applicate**: more hours (3-4-5-5-5). It adds lab techniques in Years 9–10 (preparing a solution, filtering), a full treatment of organic functional groups and reactivity, and **electrochemistry** in Years 11–12. Year 13 covers polymers, composites and materials science (metals, ceramics, semiconductors, biomaterials) [S8].
- **Liceo classico, linguistico, scienze umane**: same core, 2 h/week, with no electrochemistry and no explicit quantitative emphasis [S8].
- **Liceo economico-sociale**: Scienze naturali only in Years 9–10. Chemistry up to Dalton, formulas, Mendeleev, atomic structure, bonds and basic organic compounds [S8].
- **Liceo artistico**: Scienze naturali in Years 9–10 (up to Dalton/formula/Mendeleev). In some specialisms, "**Chimica dei materiali**" in Years 11–12 covers pigments, ceramics, glass, metals, polymers and restoration [S8].
- **Istituti tecnici, settore tecnologico**: all specialisms had "Scienze integrate (Chimica)" in Years 9–10 (old system), with the full general-chemistry list [S13]. The economic sector had a lighter version (unverified: hours). **CMB** has three sub-tracks (*articolazioni*): Chimica e materiali, Biotecnologie ambientali and Biotecnologie sanitarie [S14, S15].
- **Istituti professionali** (D.Lgs 61/2017, in force since 2018/19): chemistry appears in the integrated sciences of Years 9–10 and in some specialisms (e.g. health services, food and wine). No 2026/27 reform was found (details unverified).

**Reforms in force or being phased in for 2026/27**
1. **Primo ciclo**: *Indicazioni nazionali per il curricolo della scuola dell'infanzia e del primo ciclo* (DM 9 Dec 2025 n. 221, GU n. 21 of 27 Jan 2026). In scuola media they apply from 2026/27 to first-year classes only; the 2012 text (DM 254/2012) applies to the remaining classes until it runs out, and ends for scuola media from 2028/29 [S1, S2, S3].
2. **Istituti tecnici**: DM 29 of 19 Feb 2026 (implementing arts. 26 and 26-bis DL 144/2022, PNRR reform). It applies to **first-year classes from 2026/27** (art. 9 c.1), with 32 h/week, a flexible specialism area and the integrated "Scienze sperimentali". *Linee guida* setting out knowledge and skills are to follow by ministerial directive (art. 9 c.3). A MIM document of 4 Sept 2026, "Primi orientamenti", says the learning objectives will come in those *Linee guida* [S15, S16]. Classes 2–5 continue under DPR 88/2010 and the 2010/2012 *Linee guida* (implied by the phase-in; not stated explicitly in the documents read).
3. **Licei**: draft *Nuove Indicazioni nazionali per i licei*, published for consultation 22 Apr 2026 (MIM note prot. 112374). The CSPI gave a favourable opinion on 14 Jul 2026 (22 for, 12 against). The planned start is **2027/28, first-year classes** [S17, S18]. **In 2026/27 all liceo classes still follow DI 211/2010.**
4. **Esame di Stato → "Esame di maturità"**: DL 127/2025, converted by L. 164/2025. The oral now covers four subjects set by the ministry each year. The second written paper is chosen each January by ministerial decree (DM 13/2026 for June 2026) [S19, S20].

**National exams that shape content**
- *Esame di Stato conclusivo del primo ciclo* (end of Year 8): written Italian, maths ("competenze logico-matematiche") and languages, plus an oral. There is no science paper, but science can come up in the oral (unverified detail).
- INVALSI (Years 8, 10, 13): Italian, maths and English only. **No science.**
- *Esame di maturità* (Year 13): liceo scientifico second paper = maths (2026). Tecnico CMB "Chimica e materiali" second paper = *Chimica analitica e strumentale*. In 2026 the oral subjects included *Chimica organica e biochimica* (from commercial exam guides; not checked in DM 13/2026 itself, so unverified) [S20, S21]. Reference frameworks for the second paper: DM 769/2018 (unverified; not fetched).

**Typical interval.** Big national revisions are rare and driven by reforms, not by a legal review cycle. Primo ciclo: 1979 → 2004 → 2007 → 2012 → 2025, i.e. about 3–25 years apart, with the last gap 13 years. Upper secondary: the Gelmini texts of 2010 are only now being replaced (tecnici 2026, licei planned 2027), a gap of about **16–17 years**. DPR 89/2010 art. 12 says the *Indicazioni* are "aggiornati periodicamente" (updated periodically) but sets **no fixed interval** [S8]. Revisions follow changes of government or minister (Moratti, Fioroni, Gelmini, Valditara) and, since 2021, PNRR commitments.

**Smaller updates.** Every year: the January decree setting the maturità second-paper subjects (and, since 2026, the four oral subjects). Every 1–3 years: cross-curricular guidelines (STEM 2023, civic education 2024, guidance/orientamento) and notes on how to apply them. These rarely change chemistry content.

**Announced or in progress (as of 24 Sept 2026)**
- Tecnici: *Linee guida* (knowledge and skills) for the new specialisms are to be issued by ministerial directive. **No date announced** (unverified: whether a draft is circulating). Year 10 of the new system starts in 2027/28.
- Licei: after the CSPI opinion, the regulation still has to finish its approval steps (Consiglio di Stato opinion and adoption were not confirmed in any source found). Planned start: **Year 9 in 2027/28**, then one year at a time. The whole liceo would be on the new text only in **2031/32** if the plan holds (inferred).
- Primo ciclo: Year 7 moves to the 2025 text in 2027/28 and Year 8 in 2028/29.

**Stability rating for game content: moderate churn (2026–2031).** The core chemistry concepts (particles → Dalton → atomic structure → periodic table → bonding → mole → reactions → equilibrium/acid–base/redox → organic) have been stable since 2010. But three phase-ins now overlap, and they shift where topics sit and how deep they go (tecnici integrate the sciences; the draft liceo text is thinner in Years 11–12). Treat year placement as ±1 year and re-check around 2027/28.

## 2. Year-by-year concepts and skills

Note on sources: for Years 7–8 the national texts set targets only for the end of Year 8. The year allocation below is **typical school practice** [S7] and should be read as indicative. For Years 9–12 the national texts set targets per **biennio** (Years 9–10, Years 11–12). The year split again follows school department plans [S11, S12].

### Year 7 (2ª classe, scuola secondaria di primo grado; "seconda media"; age 12)

In force in 2026/27: *Indicazioni* 2012 [S4]. From 2027/28 this year moves to the 2025 text [S2].

**Concepts**
- *Matter & particles*: particle model of matter used to explain states and changes of state (recap from Year 6). Atoms and molecules as simple models [S4, S7].
- *Atomic structure / Periodic table*: "La struttura della materia"; elements and their classification (a first look at the periodic table with symbols); subatomic particles often only named (typical) [S7].
- *Bonding*: "Legami e reazioni chimiche", qualitative only (typical) [S7].
- *Chemical reactions*: "concetti di trasformazione chimica": reactants and products; observing and describing reactions and their products. Standard examples: **candle combustion, baking soda + vinegar, solutions in water** [S4]. Conservation of mass (Lavoisier) often introduced (typical, unverified).
- *Acids, bases & salts*: acid/base behaviour of household substances; **pH measurement** with indicators (school plans list "misurazione del pH") [S7]. The 2025 text makes acid–base reactions with common substances explicit [S2].
- *Organic chemistry*: "I composti organici" (carbohydrates, lipids, proteins as nutrients), linked to the human body unit [S7].
- *Mixtures & separation / Solutions*: solubility as an operational idea; solution vs heterogeneous mixture (2025 text) [S2].

**Skills**
- Practical/lab: safe, **non-hazardous reactions with household products** [S4]; small-scale observation; separating mixtures.
- Quantitative/math: collecting data and finding simple quantitative relations; tables and graphs (e.g. temperature–time) [S4, S2].
- Representations/models: "simple models of the structure of matter" to interpret reactions; beginning to use symbols [S4].
- Inquiry/communication: observe, form hypotheses, check them; describe with drawings and diagrams ("verbalizzare, anche con schemi e disegni") [S7].

### Year 8 (3ª media; age 13)

In force in 2026/27: *Indicazioni* 2012 [S4] (2025 text from 2028/29). End-of-cycle targets (*traguardi*) apply here.

**Concepts**
- *Energetics*: energy as a quantity that is conserved; forms and sources of energy; unavoidable heat losses; renewable vs non-renewable sources [S4, S2].
- *Redox & electrochemistry* (context only): batteries and circuits in the physics block; the 2025 text mentions smartphone batteries [S2].
- *Industrial, environmental & applied*: the carbon cycle; greenhouse-effect experiment (2025 text); finite resources; pollution [S2, S4].
- *Bonding & structure*: crystal geometry with 3D models (2025 text, "strutture cristalline") [S2].
- *Inorganic/descriptive*: rocks and minerals (Earth science) [S4]. In **Tecnologia** (technology class): physical, chemical, mechanical and technological properties of materials; metals and alloys (the blast furnace, copper, aluminium) [S2].
- *Chemical reactions*: the Year 7 ideas are consolidated and interpreted with microscopic models ("Interpretare alcuni fenomeni osservati alla luce dei modelli microscopici della materia", 2025) [S2].

**Skills**
- Practical/lab: measurements with thermometers and digital sensors; data logging (2025) [S2].
- Quantitative/math: dimensional analysis and orders of magnitude (2025); quantitative relations between variables [S2, S4].
- Representations/models: simple diagrams and models of phenomena ("semplici schematizzazioni e modellizzazioni") [S4].
- Inquiry/communication: argue scientific claims with correct vocabulary; link science to the history of ideas [S4, S2].

### Year 9 (1º anno, secondaria di secondo grado, primo biennio; age 14)

In force in 2026/27: licei follow DI 211/2010 [S8]. Tecnici: first-year classes follow **DM 29/2026** (no content *Linee guida* yet) [S15]. The 2010 *Linee guida* remain the only detailed national list [S13].

**Concepts**
- [liceo scientifico, all licei] *Matter & particles*: states of matter and their changes (macroscopic properties); particle model [S8].
- [all licei] *Mixtures & separation*: classification of matter: homogeneous and heterogeneous mixtures, elements vs compounds (*sostanze semplici e composte*) with **operational definitions** [S8]. Separation techniques (filtration, distillation, chromatography, extraction), typically done in Year 9 [S11, S12].
- [all licei] *Chemical reactions*: observing, recognising and representing simple reactions from everyday life [S8].
- [liceo scientifico, typical] measurement, physical quantities, SI units, density, the scientific method. In Year 9, Earth science takes most of the time (a national rule: Earth science is done in the first year) [S8, S11].
- [tecnico, all tecnologico; old Linee guida] *Stoichiometry & the mole*, *Gases*, *Atomic structure*: in Years 9–10 the whole list below is expected (split by the school) [S13].
- [tecnico new 2026] "Scienze sperimentali" (integrated physics, chemistry, biology and Earth science, one subject with one mark) plus "**Chimica applicata**" (3 h) in CMB [S15].

**Skills**
- Practical/lab: basic lab safety; using glassware and balances; preparing a solution, filtering [scienze applicate] [S8]. [tecnico] Hazard pictograms on labels; small-scale experiments with non-harmful materials [S13].
- Quantitative/math: measurement uncertainty, significant figures, unit conversion (typical) [S11].
- Representations/models: phenomenological and descriptive approach ("fenomenologico e osservativo-descrittivo") [S8].
- Inquiry/communication: formulate hypotheses, collect data and draw conclusions; write a lab report (*relazione di laboratorio*) [S11].

### Year 10 (2º anno, primo biennio; age 15; last compulsory year)

In force in 2026/27: DI 211/2010 (licei); DPR 88/2010 and 2010 *Linee guida* (tecnici, old system) [S8, S13].

**Concepts**
- [all licei] *Formulas & nomenclature*: **the chemical formula and what it means** [S8].
- [all licei] *Chemical reactions & equations*: **laws of chemical combination** (*leggi ponderali*: Lavoisier, Proust, Dalton's multiple proportions) [S8].
- [all licei] *Atomic structure*: **Dalton's atomic model** [S8]; subatomic particles introduced (typical) [S11, S12].
- [all licei] *Periodic table*: "a first classification of the elements (Mendeleev's periodic system)" [S8].
- [liceo scientifico, often] *Stoichiometry & the mole*: "La quantità chimica: la mole" is often placed here [S11, S12] (nationally it belongs to Years 11–12 [S8]).
- [LES, artistico] end of chemistry for these tracks. LES adds atomic structure, bonds and basic organic compounds [S8].
- [tecnico, all tecnologico; old] full general chemistry (both years together) [S13]:
  - *Stoichiometry & the mole*: atomic and molecular mass, mole, Avogadro constant; balancing; stoichiometric calculations.
  - *Gases*: microscopic model of the ideal gas, gas laws, molar volume.
  - *Atomic structure*: atomic number, mass number, isotopes; evidence for electron shells (**flame test**); the orbital model.
  - *Periodic table*: metals, non-metals, metalloids.
  - *Bonding*: octet rule, bond types, intermolecular forces, valence, oxidation number, electronegativity, molecular shape.
  - *Formulas & nomenclature*: molecular and ionic systems, **IUPAC nomenclature rules**.
  - *Solutions*: % by mass, molarity, molality; colligative properties.
  - *Energetics & Kinetics*: energy in chemical change; catalysts and factors affecting rate.
  - *Equilibrium*: equilibrium constant, solubility equilibrium, **Le Chatelier**.
  - *Acids, bases & salts*: acid–base theories, pH, indicators (incl. plant-based), strong and weak acids, hydrolysis, **buffer solutions**, heat of neutralisation.
  - *Redox & electrochemistry*: balancing by the ion–electron method; cells, corrosion, Faraday's laws, electrolysis.
  - *Organic chemistry*: aliphatic and aromatic hydrocarbons, functional groups, nomenclature, biomolecules.

**Skills**
- Practical/lab: measuring mass, volume, temperature, density, melting and boiling points to identify substances; separations (filtration, distillation, crystallisation, centrifugation, chromatography, solvent extraction) [tecnico] [S13]; testing the *leggi ponderali* [liceo] [S12].
- Quantitative/math: the mole as the bridge between macro and micro; preparing solutions of a given concentration [tecnico] [S13]; simple mass-ratio problems [liceo] [S12].
- Representations/models: kinetic–molecular model; temperature–time graphs for changes of state [S13]; Dalton symbols and formulas [S8].
- Inquiry/communication: historical and epistemological reading of the classic experiments (Lavoisier, Proust, Dalton) [S8].

### Year 11 (3º anno, secondo biennio; age 16)

In force in 2026/27: DI 211/2010 (licei); 2012 *Linee guida* (tecnici) [S8, S14]. Nationally, licei content is given for Years 11–12 together. The split below follows school plans [S11, S12].

**Concepts**
- [liceo scientifico] *Atomic structure*: structure of the atom and **atomic models** (Thomson, Rutherford, Bohr, quantum-mechanical/orbitals, typical); electron configuration [S8, S11, S12].
- [liceo scientifico] *Periodic table*: the periodic system and **periodic properties** (radius, ionisation energy, electronegativity) [S8].
- [liceo scientifico] *Bonding & structure*: chemical bonds (ionic, covalent, metallic); **intermolecular forces**; molecular shape (VSEPR, typical); structure–property relationship [S8, S11].
- [liceo scientifico] *Formulas & nomenclature*: "the classification of the main inorganic compounds and their nomenclature" (oxides, hydroxides, acids, salts), placed in Year 11 or Year 12 depending on the school [S8, S11, S12].
- [liceo scientifico] *Stoichiometry*: types of reactions and stoichiometry; mole calculations [S8, S12].
- [LS-SA] adds organic chemistry "from the properties of the carbon atom up to the main functional groups and their reactivity" in Years 11–12 [S8].
- [tecnico CMB – Chimica analitica e strumentale, Y11–12] measurement theory, data handling and statistics; elemental composition and formula; stoichiometry; acids and bases, oxidants and reductants, **coordination compounds**; ion reactions and **qualitative analysis**; thermodynamics applied to equilibria; kinetics; aqueous equilibria; electrochemical potentials; atomic and molecular orbitals; **atomic and molecular spectroscopy**; electrochemical, optical and chromatographic methods [S14].

## 3. Terminology and notation conventions

- **Language of instruction:** Italian (German in South Tyrol's German-language schools, Slovene in some Friuli-Venezia Giulia schools). The regions with special statutes adapt the national texts [S15].
- **Nomenclature:** the national texts ask for "classificazione dei principali composti inorganici e la relativa nomenclatura" (licei) and "regole della nomenclatura IUPAC" (tecnici) [S8, S13]. In practice, Italian textbooks and university intro courses teach **three systems side by side** (unverified as a national rule; based on university material [S24] and Treccani [S23]):
  - *tradizionale*: -oso/-ico for lower/higher oxidation state (*ossido ferroso/ferrico*, *acido solforoso/solforico*, *cloruro rameoso/rameico*); **anidride** for non-metal oxides (*anidride carbonica* = CO₂, *anidride solforosa* = SO₂); -ito/-ato salts; *idracidi* (HCl = *acido cloridrico*), *ossiacidi*, *idrossidi*, *idruri*, *perossidi*;
  - *IUPAC/composizionale*: prefixes (*diossido di carbonio*, *triossido di zolfo*);
  - *Stock*: *ossido di ferro(III)*.
  Games should accept the traditional names, which are very common in everyday Italian ("anidride carbonica" is the everyday name for CO₂).
- **Element names differ** from English: *azoto* (N, not "nitrogeno"), *potassio* (K), *sodio* (Na), *zolfo* (S), *mercurio*, *stagno* (Sn), *piombo* (Pb), *ferro*, *rame* (Cu), *argento*, *oro*, *cloro*, *fosforo*, *ossigeno*, *idrogeno*, *carbonio*.
- **Numbers and units:** **decimal comma** (0,1 M); thousands grouped with a point or a space; multiplication dot in scientific notation (6,022·10²³ mol⁻¹). SI units; temperatures in °C and K; pressures often in atm and mmHg as well as Pa (typical). The older term *peso specifico* (specific weight) still appears next to *densità* (it is in the 2012 primo ciclo text) [S4].
- **Equations and ions:** arrow → for reactions and ⇄ for equilibria; state symbols (s), (l), (g), (aq); ion charges as superscripts (SO₄²⁻). Hazard pictograms follow CLP/GHS ("simboli di pericolosità") [S13].
- **Vocabulary that differs from English:**
  - *miscuglio* = mixture (*omogeneo/eterogeneo*); *sostanza semplice/composta* = element/compound; *stati di aggregazione* = states of matter; *passaggi di stato* = changes of state (*fusione, solidificazione, vaporizzazione/evaporazione/ebollizione, condensazione/liquefazione, sublimazione, brinamento* = deposition);
  - *leggi ponderali* = mass laws; *quantità chimica* = amount of substance; *massa molare*; *molarità*, *molalità*, *(normalità* still found in technical schools, unverified);
  - *ossidoriduzione/redox*; *pila* = galvanic cell; *legame dativo* = coordinate bond;
  - *trasformazione chimica/fisica* = chemical/physical change; *relazione di laboratorio* = lab report.
- **Organic:** Italian IUPAC names (*metano, etanolo, acido etanoico/acido acetico, benzene*); E/Z and R/S used in tecnici CMB [S14].

## 5. Open items / not verified

- **Placement within Years 7–8 and within each biennio is not national.** The Year 7/8 split and the Year 9/10 and 11/12 splits come from three school plans [S7, S11, S12]. A wider sample (or textbook tables of contents) would firm this up. Where nomenclature, the mole and gas laws sit varies by school, by up to a year.
- **Tecnici after DM 29/2026:** the *Linee guida* with knowledge and skills for "Scienze sperimentali" and "Chimica applicata" are not published. I could not locate the MIM "Primi orientamenti" document of 4 Sept 2026 itself (reported by Spaggiari [S16]). It was not confirmed that classes 2–5 formally continue with the 2010/2012 *Linee guida*.
- **Draft licei *Indicazioni*:** after the CSPI opinion of 14 Jul 2026 (secondary reports [S18]), I found no confirmation of a Consiglio di Stato opinion, final adoption or GU publication. The 2027/28 start comes from press and union sources, not a published regulation.
- **Hours from DPR 88/2010** (old tecnici: Scienze integrate Chimica 3+3; CMB Years 11–13 subject hours) and for liceo artistico "Chimica dei materiali" were not checked against the primary annexes (the GU PDF tables are images). The DPR 89/2010 scientifico and classico figures were checked via school-hosted copies of the annexes on unica.istruzione.gov.it [S9, S10].
- **Maturità 2026 subjects for CMB** come from commercial exam guides [S21]. The DM 13/2026 annexes were not opened. The subjects change every year.
- **Timeline items marked unverified** (1979 programmes, Moratti D.Lgs 226/2005, Fioroni 2007, DM 139/2007, *Nuovi scenari* 2018, D.Lgs 61/2017 details, DM 769/2018) come from background knowledge and were not fetched in this session.
- **Nomenclature practice** (traditional names taught alongside IUPAC/Stock) rests on university teaching material and Treccani, not a ministry document.
- **Sites:** gazzettaufficiale.it returned only the metadata page for DM 221/2025, not the text; the MIM copy was used instead [S2]. The miur.gov.it URL for DM 254/2012 failed; the mim.gov.it mirror worked [S4]. The *Nuovi scenari* (2018) text was not retrieved.
