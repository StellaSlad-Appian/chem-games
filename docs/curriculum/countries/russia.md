# Chemistry curriculum: Russia (Years 7–12)

**Purpose:** input for the cross-country concept/year map for chem-games.
**Research date:** 24 Sept 2026 (desk research by an agent).
**Review status:** not yet checked by a teacher who teaches in Russia.
**Key sources:** Федеральные рабочие программы (ФРП) по химии, 2025 editions (edsoo.ru) [S1–S4]; ФИПИ ОГЭ/ЕГЭ 2027 specifications and codifiers [S9–S10]; Минпросвещения methodological letter for 2025/26 [S6]; full list in section 6.

## Summary for game design

- **Chemistry starts in Year 8 (8 класс, age ~14) as a separate compulsory subject.** Year 7 has no chemistry lessons. The groundwork comes from Физика 7 класс (atoms and molecules, diffusion, states of matter) and some biology [S7, S8].
- **The content is set nationally and in detail.** Since 2023 every school must teach from the federal work programmes (ФРП). The editions in force for 2026/27 are the 2025 ones, in force since 1 Sept 2025 under приказ № 704 [S1–S4, S6, S13]. A game can target the topic lists almost line by line.
- **Year 8 is heavy on quantities.** It covers the mole, molar mass, molar volume (22,4 л/моль), mass fraction, reaction-equation calculations, valence, oxidation states, the Periodic Table, electron shells of elements 1–20, bonding types, and even thermochemical equations [S1].
- **Year 9 builds a "general chemistry" core and adds descriptive chemistry.** The core is electrolytic dissociation, ionic equations (full and net), electron-balance redox, rate and equilibrium (as ideas only), and a first look at hydrolysis. The descriptive part goes group by group: halogens, group VIA, group VA, group IVA, then metals. Qualitative tests for ions are a signature skill, and ОГЭ has a real hands-on experiment [S1, S10].
- **Upper secondary runs organic first, then general chemistry.** Year 10 (10 класс) is almost entirely organic chemistry. Year 11 (11 класс) is general and inorganic chemistry. Orbitals, s/p/d configurations, pH, Le Chatelier and electrolysis mostly land in Year 11, after organic [S3, S4].
- **There are two tracks: базовый (basic) and углублённый (advanced).** At basic level Years 10–11 get only **1 h/week**. At advanced level they get 3 h/week. Advanced adds hybridisation, reaction mechanisms, the equilibrium constant (idea only), quantum-number rules, complexes, electrolysis and Cr/Mn chemistry [S3, S4]. An optional advanced track also exists for Years 8–9 (3–4 h/week) [S2].
- **The 2025 editions trimmed the basic senior course.** Topics removed include ketones, toluene, sucrose, amines, pH, hydrolysis, electrolysis, corrosion and specific polymers. Games should not assume every basic-level Year 10–11 student has met these [S3 vs S5].
- **Notation games must respect Russian conventions.** These include the decimal comma, «Периодическая система Д.И. Менделеева» with A/Б subgroups, Roman-numeral valence (оксид железа(III)), thermochemical equations with "+ Q кДж" rather than ΔH, «=» in balanced equations, locants after the name (пропанол-2, бутадиен-1,3) and н.у. (normal conditions) [S9].
- **There is no Year 12.** School ends at 11 класс.
- **Reform is coming soon.** A new ФГОС СОО (приказ № 414 of 11 June 2026) takes effect on **1 Sept 2027**, with "more applied" chemistry in Years 10–11. ФГОС ООО (Years 5–9) is being revised in 2026–27. Practical tasks for ЕГЭ chemistry are being piloted from autumn 2026 [S14–S16].

**Reforms being phased in**
- **New ФГОС СОО.** приказ № 414 of 11.06.2026 was registered with the Ministry of Justice on 17.09.2026 (№ 88304). It takes effect on 1 Sept 2027 and does not change 2026/27 [S14]. Press reports say that mathematics, physics, chemistry and biology in 10–11 классы become "more applied", with a strengthened science and engineering component [S15, S17]. Chemistry-specific details: see Open items.
- **Revised ФГОС ООО and programmes for 5–9 классы.** Planned for 2026–2027 [S15]. The effective date is not yet published (unverified).
- **Practical part of the ЕГЭ in chemistry.** Experimental tasks are being piloted in regions in autumn 2026. No change to the 2027 exam [S16]. Full introduction around 2030 has been reported (unverified).
- **Unified state textbooks.** Announced for physics, for grades 7–11 [S15]. No chemistry unified textbook was found (unverified).

**National exams that shape content**
- **ОГЭ по химии (end of 9 класс, elective).** 23 tasks, 38 primary points, 180 min. Part 2 includes task 23, a **real chemical experiment**: four tests to identify substances in two numbered test tubes. Students may use the Periodic Table, the solubility table, the electrochemical series of metals and a calculator. The 2027 draft is unchanged from 2026 [S10, S12]. The codifier's content blocks are: первоначальные понятия; Periodic law and atomic structure; строение вещества; неметаллы/металлы and their compounds; химические реакции; химия и окружающая среда (including first ideas of organic compounds); расчёты [S10].
- **ЕГЭ по химии (end of 11 класс, elective, required for chemistry, medicine and similar university entry).** 34 tasks (28 short-answer, 6 extended), 56 primary points, 210 min. The same reference tables are provided and a calculator is allowed. The 2027 draft: "Изменения структуры и содержания КИМ отсутствуют" [S9]. The codifier covers both basic (БУ) and advanced (УУ) content. Several elements are **УУ-only**: thermochemistry, electrolysis, reaction mechanisms, and most organic reaction chemistry [S9]. In practice the ЕГЭ assumes the advanced course.
- **ВПР (national diagnostic tests, low stakes).** Chemistry is one of the randomly assigned subjects in 8 класс and one of the options in 10 класс (unverified, press sources only [S18]).

**Typical interval and whether revision is scheduled**
- **Standards (ФГОС).** Major generations came in 2004, 2010/2012, 2021/2022 and 2026/27: roughly every 10 years, now shortening to about 5.
- **No fixed legal review cycle was found.** Article 11 of 273-ФЗ, checked on a legal mirror, contains no revision interval [S19]. Revisions are ministry- and policy-driven, so ad hoc. Recent revisions have been framed as sovereignty and "единое образовательное пространство" reforms, and by the state plan for science and maths education to 2030 (распоряжение 19.11.2024 № 3333-р) [S6].
- **Federal programmes (ФОП/ФРП).** Amended almost every year since 2023: 2023 introduction, the 2024 order effective 2025, and new ФГОС-linked programmes expected for 2027.

**Smaller updates**
- ФИПИ publishes new ОГЭ and ЕГЭ specifications, codifiers and demo papers **every year**. Drafts appear in late August and are open for public discussion until about 30 September. Final versions follow later in the autumn (discussion deadline from secondary sources, unverified).
- Structural changes to the chemistry exams came in 2017, 2018 and 2022 for the ЕГЭ, and in 2020 and 2025 for the ОГЭ: about every 3–5 years. Minor wording or single-task changes happen in most other years [S11, S12].

**Now announced or in progress**
- New ФГОС СОО from 1 Sept 2027. Updated 10–11 programmes are to follow; the new chemistry ФРП text had not been found as of 24 Sept 2026.
- ФГОС ООО and programmes for 5–9 классы are to be revised in 2026–27 (effective date unverified).
- ЕГЭ practical tasks in chemistry: pilot in autumn 2026, then an assessment of feasibility. A target of around 2030 has been reported (unverified).

**Stability rating for game content:** **Moderate churn.** The core topic sequence has been stable for about 20 years: Year 8 substances, mole and inorganic classes; Year 9 ions, redox and the elements; Year 10 organic; Year 11 general and inorganic. What moves often: hours and levels, which topics are basic versus advanced, exam formats, and a new senior standard arriving in 2027.

## 2. Year-by-year concepts and skills

### Year 7 (7 класс)

No chemistry subject. The chemistry-relevant content sits in other subjects.

**Concepts**
- *Matter & particles* (in Физика 7 класс): atoms and molecules and their sizes; experiments showing that matter is discrete; particle motion and its link to temperature; Brownian motion and diffusion; attraction and repulsion between particles; states of matter (gas, liquid, crystalline solid) explained by particle structure; the special features of water's states [S7].
- *Measurement* (Физика 7): physical quantities, SI units, measurement error, the scientific method (question, hypothesis, experiment, explanation) [S7].
- *Biochemistry, first contact* (Биология, taught in 6 класс, not 7): inorganic (water, mineral salts) and organic substances (proteins, fats, carbohydrates) in plants; photosynthesis; mineral nutrition. Biology in 7 класс is plant systematics and has almost no chemistry [S8].

**Skills**
- *Practical:* measuring volume and temperature, estimating the size of an atom by the method of rows, observing diffusion and thermal expansion of gases [S7].
- *Quantitative:* reading a scale division, measurement error [S7].
- *Models:* particle models of the three states [S7].
- *Inquiry:* testing a hypothesis [S7].

Optional pre-chemistry ("propaedeutic") courses in 7 класс exist in some textbook series but are not part of the federal programme (unverified).

### Year 8 (8 класс)

**Concepts** [S1; углублённый additions from S2]
- *Matter & particles:* subject of chemistry; bodies (тела) and substances; physical properties; states of matter; atoms, molecules, chemical elements and symbols; simple and compound substances (простые/сложные вещества); atomic-molecular theory (атомно-молекулярное учение). [углублённый] metals versus non-metals, and molecular versus non-molecular substances; empirical and theoretical methods of science; Lomonosov.
- *Mixtures & separation:* pure substances and mixtures (homogeneous and heterogeneous); separation by magnet, filtration, evaporation, distillation and chromatography; purifying table salt.
- *Formulas & nomenclature:* chemical formula; valence (валентность); law of constant composition; naming oxides, bases, acids and salts. [углублённый] international and trivial names; acid, basic and double salts.
- *Stoichiometry & the mole:* relative atomic and molecular mass (Ar, Mr); mass fraction of an element; amount of substance, the mole and molar mass; the link between amount, mass and number of particles; molar volume of gases; calculations from equations. [углублённый] Avogadro's law, relative gas density, empirical formula from mass or mole fractions, mean molar mass of a mixture, volume ratios of gases.
- *Chemical reactions & equations:* physical versus chemical phenomena; signs of a reaction; conservation of mass; chemical equations; four reaction types (соединения, разложения, замещения, обмена).
- *Energetics:* heat effect of a reaction, thermochemical equations, exo- and endothermic reactions; fuels (coal, methane).
- *Inorganic/descriptive:* air as a mixture; oxygen (properties, preparation, combustion, the oxygen cycle, ozone as an allotrope); hydrogen (properties, preparation, uses); water (physical and chemical properties, water as a solvent, the water cycle, water pollution and treatment).
- *Solutions & concentration:* solutions; saturated and unsaturated; solubility; mass fraction of solute. [углублённый] factors affecting solubility, molar concentration.
- *Acids, bases & salts:* classifying inorganic compounds; oxides (basic, acidic, amphoteric, non-salt-forming); bases (alkalis versus insoluble bases); acids; salts; their properties and preparation; the Beketov activity series of metals (ряд активности металлов Н.Н. Бекетова); indicators; genetic links between classes (генетическая связь). [углублённый] amphoteric hydroxides (Zn, Al).
- *Periodic table:* early classifications; families (alkali and alkaline-earth metals, halogens, noble gases); the Periodic law; short- and long-period forms of the table; periods and groups (A and Б subgroups); physical meaning of the atomic, period and group numbers; trends in radius and metallic character; Mendeleev as "scientist and citizen".
- *Atomic structure:* nucleus (protons, neutrons), isotopes, electrons; electron shells of the first 20 elements; characterising an element from its position. [углублённый] radioactivity, s/p/d orbitals, electron configurations and orbital-box diagrams (электронно-графические формулы).
- *Bonding & structure:* electronegativity; covalent (polar and non-polar) and ionic bonds; ions, cations and anions. [углублённый] bond-formation mechanisms, electron and structural formulas, crystal-lattice types.
- *Redox:* oxidation state; redox reactions; oxidation and reduction; oxidising and reducing agents. [углублённый] balancing simple redox equations by electron balance.
- *Industrial, environmental & applied:* air pollution, enhanced greenhouse effect, ozone-layer depletion.

**Skills** [S1, S2]
- *Practical:* lab glassware and safety rules; observing physical and chemical changes; separating mixtures; preparing, collecting and identifying O₂ and H₂; preparing a solution of set mass fraction (углублённый: also of set molar concentration); using indicators (litmus, phenolphthalein, methyl orange); neutralisation; displacing a metal from a salt solution; the experimental problem «Важнейшие классы неорганических соединений».
- *Quantitative:* Mr and molar mass; mass fraction of an element; mass fraction in solution; calculations by equation (mass, amount, gas volume).
- *Models:* ball-and-stick models; writing formulas and equations; linking periodic-table data to atomic structure.
- *Inquiry and communication:* classifying substances and reactions; predicting properties; presenting results of experiments and projects.

### Year 9 (9 класс)

**Concepts** [S1; углублённый from S2]
- *Periodic table / Atomic structure:* trends for elements of periods 1–3 plus K and Ca; A/Б subgroups. [углублённый] ionisation energy, filling order of orbitals in long periods, the Cr/Cu "electron slip" (провал электрона).
- *Bonding & structure:* all bond types including metallic; crystal-lattice types (atomic, ionic, molecular, metallic) and how they relate to properties. [углублённый] donor-acceptor mechanism, hydrogen bonding and van der Waals forces; metal lattice types (bcc, fcc, hcp).
- *Chemical reactions & equations:* classifying reactions by several criteria (composition, heat effect, change in oxidation state, reversibility, catalyst).
- *Energetics:* thermochemical equations. [углублённый] Hess's law and its consequences.
- *Kinetics:* idea of reaction rate; homogeneous and heterogeneous reactions; catalysis; factors that change the rate. [углублённый] law of mass action, activation energy, enzymes, inhibitors.
- *Equilibrium:* reversible and irreversible reactions; the idea of chemical equilibrium and the factors that shift it. [углублённый] Le Chatelier's principle stated explicitly.
- *Redox:* balancing by electron balance (метод электронного баланса).
- *Acids, bases & salts / Solutions:* theory of electrolytic dissociation; electrolytes and non-electrolytes; degree of dissociation; strong and weak electrolytes; ion-exchange reactions and when they go; full and net ionic equations; properties of acids, bases and salts in ionic terms; the idea of salt hydrolysis. [углублённый] dissociation constant, ionic product of water, pH, crystal hydrates, ionic equations for hydrolysis.
- *Analytical:* qualitative reactions for ions: Cl⁻, Br⁻, I⁻, CO₃²⁻, PO₄³⁻, SiO₃²⁻, SO₄²⁻, OH⁻, NH₄⁺ and the metal ions studied. Flame colours of Na, K and Ca.
- *Inorganic/descriptive, non-metals:* halogens (Cl₂, HCl, hydrochloric acid, chlorides); group VIA (O and S allotropes, H₂S, sulfur oxides, sulfuric acid and its industrial production, sulfates); group VA (N₂, the nitrogen cycle, NH₃, ammonium salts, HNO₃, nitrates, P and its allotropes, P₂O₅, H₃PO₄, fertilisers); group IVA (carbon allotropes, adsorption, CO and CO₂, carbonic acid and carbonates, Si, SiO₂, silicates).
- *Inorganic/descriptive, metals:* metallic bond; the electrochemical series of metals (электрохимический ряд напряжений); general properties and extraction; corrosion and protection; alloys (steel, cast iron, duralumin, bronze); Na and K; Mg and Ca (water hardness); Al (amphoteric oxide and hydroxide); Fe and its Fe(II)/Fe(III) compounds. [углублённый] electrolysis of melts and solutions; Cu, Ag and Zn; Cr compounds; first ideas of complex compounds.
- *Organic (introduction):* methane, ethane, ethylene, acetylene, ethanol, glycerol and acetic acid as carbon compounds; natural sources of hydrocarbons (coal, gas, oil) and petrol; fats, proteins and carbohydrates.
- *Polymers & materials:* building materials (ceramics, glass, cement, concrete); samples of polymer materials.
- *Industrial, environmental & applied:* acid rain, pollution by S and N compounds, the CO₂ greenhouse hypothesis, maximum permissible concentration (ПДК), first aid for chemical burns and poisoning, safe use of household chemicals. [углублённый] principles of "green chemistry".

**Skills** [S1, S2, S10]
- *Practical:* preparing, collecting and identifying NH₃ and CO₂; electrical conductivity of solutions; ion-exchange reactions (precipitate, gas, water); identifying substances with qualitative tests; experimental problems on non-metals and on metals. **ОГЭ task 23: a real four-test identification experiment** with the results recorded in a table.
- *Quantitative:* the same calculation types as Year 8 (equation-based, mass fraction). [углублённый] Hess's-law and thermochemical calculations; solving systems of equations (4 h/week version).
- *Models:* crystal-lattice models (diamond, graphite, NaCl, fullerene); writing dissociation equations and full and net ionic equations; chains of transformations (генетические цепочки).
- *Inquiry:* investigating factors that affect rate; presenting experiments; critical use of information sources.

### Year 10 (10 класс)

Almost entirely organic chemistry at both levels [S3, S4].

**Concepts, базовый** [S3]
- *Organic chemistry:*
  - Butlerov's theory of chemical structure (теория строения А.М. Бутлерова); structural formulas; homology and isomerism; single and multiple bonds; classification; systematic (IUPAC) and trivial names.
  - Hydrocarbons: alkanes (methane and ethane: substitution, combustion); alkenes (ethylene and propylene: hydrogenation, halogenation, hydration, oxidation, polymerisation); alkadienes (buta-1,3-diene and isoprene; synthetic rubber); alkynes (acetylene); arenes (benzene: halogenation and nitration); genetic links between hydrocarbon classes.
  - Natural gas, oil (distillation, thermal and catalytic cracking, pyrolysis), coal.
  - Oxygen compounds: methanol and ethanol (hydrogen bonding, toxicity); ethylene glycol and glycerol (qualitative test); phenol; aldehydes (methanal and ethanal: oxidation, silver-mirror and Cu(OH)₂ tests); methanoic and ethanoic acids; esterification; stearic and oleic acids; soaps; esters and their hydrolysis; fats.
  - Carbohydrates: glucose and its reactions; fructose as an isomer.
- *Biochemistry:* glucose, photosynthesis, starch and cellulose (starch-iodine test); amino acids (glycine) as amphoteric compounds; peptides; proteins (primary, secondary and tertiary structure; hydrolysis; denaturation; colour tests).
- *Polymers & materials:* monomer, polymer, structural unit, degree of polymerisation; addition and condensation polymerisation (полимеризация / поликонденсация).
- *Removed in the 2025 basic edition* (still in the advanced course): ketones and acetone, toluene, sucrose, amines and aniline, named plastics, rubbers and fibres [S3 vs S5].

**Concepts, углублённый additions** [S4]
- Electronic structure of carbon (ground and excited state); sp³, sp² and sp hybridisation; σ- and π-bonds; homolytic and heterolytic bond breaking; radicals, nucleophiles and electrophiles; inductive and mesomeric effects; skeletal formulas; structural and spatial isomerism, including cis/trans.
- Cycloalkanes; radical substitution; electrophilic addition and Markovnikov's rule; 1,2- versus 1,4-addition in conjugated dienes; the acidity of terminal alkynes; aromaticity and directing effects of substituents; styrene; reforming; halogenoalkanes (substitution by OH, alcoholic versus aqueous alkali).
- Ethers; phenol-formaldehyde resin; ketones and nucleophilic addition; esters in acidic and alkaline hydrolysis; synthetic detergents; mono-, di- and polysaccharides (maltose, glycogen, esters of cellulose; artificial fibres).
- Amines (primary, secondary, tertiary; aniline and how it is made from nitrobenzene); α-amino acids (glycine, alanine); peptide synthesis and hydrolysis.
- Plastics (PE, PP, PVC, PS, PMMA, PC, PET) and plastic recycling; elastomers; natural, artificial and synthetic fibres (капрон, лавсан).
- *Removed in 2025 from the advanced course* (were italic "for acquaintance only" in 2023): reaction-mechanism statements, Haworth projections and anomers, D/L amino acids, nucleic acids, special-purpose polymers [S4 vs S5].

**Skills** [S3, S4]
- *Practical:* preparing ethylene and testing its properties; properties of acetic acid; qualitative tests (decolorising bromine water and KMnO₄, Cu(OH)₂ with glycerol, glucose and aldehydes, silver mirror, starch-iodine, protein colour tests); experimental problems «Распознавание органических соединений» and «Распознавание пластмасс и волокон» [углублённый].
- *Quantitative:* calculations by equation. [углублённый] molecular formula from mass fractions or combustion products; deducing structure from chemical properties; percentage yield.
- *Models:* ball-and-stick and space-filling models; full and condensed structural formulas; [углублённый] skeletal formulas.
- *Inquiry:* critical analysis of media information; ПДК and toxicity reasoning.

### Year 11 (11 класс)

General and inorganic chemistry, plus «Химия и жизнь» (chemistry and life) [S3, S4].

**Concepts, базовый** [S3]
- *Atomic structure:* energy levels and sublevels; s, p and d orbitals; electron configurations for periods 1–4.

## 3. Terminology and notation conventions

- **Periodic table:** «Периодическая система химических элементов Д.И. Менделеева». Both the short-period form (groups I–VIII with А/Б subgroups, «главная подгруппа (А-группа)» and «побочная подгруппа (Б-группа)») and the long-period form are taught. Group labels use Roman numerals plus a letter: VIA, VA, IVA, IA and IIA [S1, S4].
- **Nomenclature:** inorganic names use a Russian adaptation of IUPAC: оксид железа(III), гидроксид натрия, сульфат меди(II), гидросульфат. Trivial names are also expected and examined: угарный газ (CO), углекислый газ (CO₂), гашёная/негашёная известь, питьевая сода, пирит, медный купорос (unverified for the last one) [S3]. Organic chemistry uses «систематическая номенклатура (IUPAC)», but locants often follow the name: пропанол-2, бутадиен-1,3, метилбутадиен-1,3 [S3, S9]. Trivial names are required for about 15 organic compounds (этилен, ацетилен, глицерин, формальдегид, уксусная кислота, глицин and others) [S3].
- **Valence versus oxidation state:** both are taught. Валентность is given in Roman numerals and used to build binary formulas in Year 8. Степень окисления is written as a signed number. They are distinct concepts, and games should not merge them.
- **Quantities and symbols:** amount of substance n (older texts use ν); molar mass M (г/моль); molar volume Vm = 22,4 л/моль at н.у. (нормальные условия, 0 °C and 101,3 kPa); mass fraction ω (омега), often in %; molar concentration c (моль/л); relative atomic and molecular mass Ar and Mr; relative gas density D [S1, S9].
- **Decimal comma:** 22,4 л; 1,66 %; 0,42 моль/л [S9].
- **Equations:** balanced inorganic equations use «=». Reversible reactions use ⇄. Transformation chains use arrows. State labels are (г) gas, (ж) liquid and (тв) solid. **Thermochemical equations put the heat on the product side as "+ Q кДж"**, for example C₂H₅OH(ж) + 3O₂(г) = 2CO₂(г) + 3H₂O(ж) + 1410 кДж [S9]. ΔH is not the main school convention (unverified for the advanced textbooks).
- **Ionic equations:** «молекулярное, полное и сокращённое ионное уравнение» (molecular, full and net ionic). **Redox** is balanced by «метод электронного баланса» (electron balance). The half-reaction method is not named in the ФРП [S1, S4].
- **Key reference tables** allowed in exams: the Periodic Table, «таблица растворимости солей, кислот и оснований в воде» (solubility table) and «электрохимический ряд напряжений металлов» (electrochemical series). Year 8 teaches the «ряд активности металлов Н.Н. Бекетова» (Beketov activity series) [S1, S9, S10].
- **Words that differ from English usage:**
  - «щёлочь» (alkali, a soluble base) versus «основание» (base)
  - «атомная кристаллическая решётка» means a covalent network lattice
  - «генетическая связь» means the relationship between classes, shown by transformation chains
  - «качественная реакция» means a qualitative test
  - «тело» means a physical body or object
  - «простое вещество» means an element substance
  - «амфотерность» means amphoterism
  - «ПДК» means maximum permissible concentration
  - «кислые / основные / средние соли» means acid, basic and normal salts
- **Named scientists** matter in the programmes: Менделеев, Ломоносов, Бутлеров, Бекетов, Марковников and Зайцев (Zaitsev's rule is in the ЕГЭ codifier) [S1, S4, S9].
