## 1. KMK Bildungsstandards Chemie für die Allgemeine Hochschulreife (2020)

### 1.1 Status and framing (verified)
- Adopted by the KMK on 18.06.2020. It replaces the EPA Chemie. It is binding for all routes to the Abitur, including berufliche Gymnasien. From the 2024/25 school year the Abitur in every Land is supposed to be based on these standards.
- Two levels:
  - **grundlegendes Anforderungsniveau (gA)**: 2–3 lessons a week. This is the Grundkurs (GK) or Basisfach.
  - **erhöhtes Anforderungsniveau (eA)**: at least 4 lessons a week. This is the Leistungskurs (LK) or Leistungsfach.
  - The difference lies in "Umfang und Tiefe" (range and depth) and in how much students steer their own work. At eA, models are more complex and the maths goes further ("umfangreichere und tiefere Mathematisierung").
- The document says some of its content is "in manchen Ländern üblicherweise in der Sekundarstufe I oder in der Einführungsphase vermittelt". This matters for year tagging: a KMK item does not automatically belong to the Qualifikationsphase.
- There are four competence areas:
  - Sachkompetenz: S1–S17
  - Erkenntnisgewinnung: E1–E12
  - Kommunikation: K1–K13
  - Bewertung: B1–B14
- Standards relevant to games (verbatim):
  - S7 "beschreiben die Umkehrbarkeit chemischer Reaktionen, das dynamische Gleichgewicht und das Donator-Akzeptor-Prinzip und wenden diese an"
  - S8 "beschreiben Einflussfaktoren auf chemische Reaktionen und Möglichkeiten der Steuerung durch Variation von Reaktionsbedingungen sowie durch den Einsatz von Katalysatoren"
  - S9 "erklären unterschiedliche Reaktivitäten und Reaktionsverläufe"
  - S12 "deuten Stoff- und Energieumwandlungen hinsichtlich der Veränderung von Teilchen sowie des Umbaus chemischer Bindungen"
  - S14 "beschreiben ausgewählte Reaktionsmechanismen"
  - S15 "grenzen mithilfe von Modellen den statischen Zustand auf Stoffebene vom dynamischen Zustand auf Teilchenebene ab"
  - S16 "entwickeln Reaktionsgleichungen"
  - S17 "wenden bekannte mathematische Verfahren auf chemische Sachverhalte an"
  - K6 "unterscheiden zwischen Alltags- und Fachsprache"
  - K9 "verwenden Fachbegriffe und -sprache korrekt"

### 1.2 Basiskonzepte (verified)
The 2020 AHR standards define **three** Basiskonzepte, not four:
1. **Konzept vom Aufbau und von den Eigenschaften der Stoffe und ihrer Teilchen.** Aspects: Atom- und Molekülbau, chemische Bindung, Modifikationen, funktionelle Gruppen, Isomerie, inter-/intramolekulare Wechselwirkungen, Stoffeigenschaften, Stoffklassen, analytische Verfahren, Verwendungsmöglichkeiten.
2. **Konzept der chemischen Reaktion.** Aspects: Donator-Akzeptor, Umkehrbarkeit, Gleichgewicht, Reaktionstypen, Mechanismen, Steuerung.
3. **Energiekonzept.** Aspects: Energieformen/-umwandlung/-kreislauf, Aktivierungsenergie/Katalyse, Energie chemischer Bindungen/Wechselwirkungen, Reaktionskinetik, Enthalpie/Entropie. Kinetics sits inside the Energiekonzept.

The often-quoted four-concept set (Stoff-Teilchen, Struktur-Eigenschaft, chemische Reaktion, Energie) is the **Sek I / Mittlerer Schulabschluss (2004)** model **(unverified here, from background knowledge)**. BW's Sek I plan still uses headings like "Stoff – Teilchen – Struktur – Eigenschaften" (verified: BW section 3.2.1 title). NRW, Niedersachsen, Berlin/Brandenburg and Hessen SII all adopted the three KMK concepts (verified in each document).
→ **Game tag recommendation:** use the three AHR concepts for Sek II content: `bk:stoff-teilchen`, `bk:reaktion`, `bk:energie`.

Game-design notes:
- Operator verbs are a natural difficulty and verb tag (`op:nennen`, `op:erklaeren`, …).
- The **erklären vs erläutern** distinction confuses students and suits a meta-game:
  - erklären = trace a fact back to rules or laws
  - erläutern = make it clear with extra illustrative information
- **beurteilen vs bewerten** works the same way:
  - beurteilen = judgement on facts, by subject criteria
  - bewerten = value judgement against social norms

### 2.1 NRW: Kernlehrplan Chemie SII (2022). Verified from the PDF
**EF: two compulsory Inhaltsfelder**
1. *Organische Stoffklassen.* Content:
   - Funktionelle Gruppen: Hydroxy, Carbonyl, Carboxy, Ester, with their Nachweise
   - Löslichkeit, Schmelz- und Siedetemperatur
   - Einfach- und Mehrfachbindungen, EPA-Modell
   - **Konstitutionsisomerie**
   - intermolekulare WW
   - Oxidationsreihe der Alkanole with Oxidationszahlen
   - Estersynthese

   Expectation: "ordnen organische Verbindungen aufgrund ihrer funktionellen Gruppen in Stoffklassen ein und benennen diese nach systematischer Nomenklatur"; "stellen Isomere von Alkanolen dar und erklären die Konstitutionsisomerie".
2. *Reaktionsgeschwindigkeit und chemisches Gleichgewicht.* Content:
   - Reaktionskinetik
   - Le Chatelier
   - **MWG (Kc)**
   - Steuerung: Oberfläche, Konzentration, Temperatur, Druck
   - **Katalyse**

   Expectations:
   - "definieren die **Durchschnittsgeschwindigkeit** chemischer Reaktionen und ermitteln diese **grafisch aus experimentellen Daten**"
   - "stellen den zeitlichen Ablauf ... mithilfe der **Stoßtheorie** ... dar"
   - "erklären den Einfluss eines Katalysators auf die Reaktionsgeschwindigkeit auch anhand grafischer Darstellungen"
   - "bestimmen **rechnerisch Gleichgewichtslagen** ... mithilfe des MWG"
   - "simulieren den chemischen Gleichgewichtszustand als dynamisches Gleichgewicht"

**Q-Phase: GK and LK have the same five Inhaltsfelder; the LK has more content.** The KLP does not fix the order of Inhaltsfelder within Q1/Q2. The school-internal curriculum decides.
- *Säuren, Basen und analytische Verfahren.*
  - GK: 1. Hauptsatz, **Neutralisationsenthalpie, Kalorimetrie**. Expectations: "definieren den Begriff der Reaktionsenthalpie und grenzen diesen von der inneren Energie ab"; "bestimmen die Reaktionsenthalpie der Neutralisationsreaktion ... kalorimetrisch"; explain the different reaction rates of strong vs weak acids with base metals.
  - LK adds: Lösungsenthalpie and **Entropie**, "führen den spontanen Ablauf eines endothermen Lösungsvorgangs auf die Entropieänderung zurück".
- *Elektrochemische Prozesse und Energetik.*
  - GK: 1. Hauptsatz, **Standardreaktionsenthalpien, Satz von Hess**, heterogene Katalyse. Expectation: "ermitteln auch rechnerisch die Standardreaktionsenthalpien ... unter Anwendung des Satzes von Hess". The Basiskonzept note names "Standardbildungsenthalpie".
  - LK adds: **2. Hauptsatz, freie Enthalpie, Gibbs-Helmholtz-Gleichung**. Expectation: "berechnen die freie Enthalpie bei Redoxreaktionen".
- *Reaktionswege in der organischen Chemie.*
  - GK content:
    - functional groups (now including Amino)
    - Alkene, Alkine, Halogenalkane
    - **Konstitutions- und cis-trans-Isomerie**
    - Fette
    - **Mechanismen: radikalische Substitution, elektrophile Addition**
    - Estersynthese with homogeneous catalysis and Le Chatelier
  - LK adds:
    - aromatic system and Mesomerie
    - **Chiralität** (asymmetric C atom)
    - **SN1/SN2**, elektrophile Erstsubstitution, Kondensation
    - koordinative Bindung/Katalyse
    - Farbstoffe, Chromatografie
- *Moderne Werkstoffe.* Not core to the organic/energetics framework.
- **Not named in NRW:** RGT-Regel, rate laws/order, Maxwell–Boltzmann, instantaneous rate.

## 3. Learning objectives: safe core vs advanced

> These skill ids (K, T, G, O) are research labels. The curriculum map uses canonical concepts
> instead; [`ALIGNMENT.md`](./ALIGNMENT.md) §2 maps every id onto them.

### 3.1 Safe core: basic kinetics (in KMK gA and every Land checked)
K1. Define reaction rate as the change in concentration per unit time (v = Δc/Δt, mean rate). Take it from a c–t table or graph.
K2. Name and explain the factors: concentration, temperature, Zerteilungsgrad/surface area, catalyst (and pressure for gases).
K3. Explain these factors with collision theory (Stoßtheorie): collision frequency, a minimum energy, correct orientation, and the idea of a wirksamer Zusammenstoß (effective collision).
K4. Aktivierungsenergie: draw and read an energy profile (Edukte → Übergangszustand → Produkte). Tell Ea apart from ΔH.
K5. Catalyst:
   - offers an alternative path with lower Ea
   - is not consumed but does take part
   - speeds up forward and back reactions equally
   - makes equilibrium arrive faster but does **not** move its position
   - homogeneous vs heterogeneous catalysis
   - enzymes as biocatalysts
K6. Plan and carry out rate experiments with variable control.

Near-core (in most Länder):
- RGT-Regel: BW, BE, HE. Not named in NRW or Niedersachsen.
- Übergangszustand: Niedersachsen, BE.
- Instantaneous rate from a tangent: named only in Hessen (optional Q3.5). It is standard textbook practice elsewhere **(unverified)**.

### 3.2 Safe core: basic thermodynamics
T1. Exothermic vs endothermic on the material and the particle level. Sign convention: ΔH < 0 is exothermic.
T2. Systems: offen / geschlossen / isoliert. Named in BW LF, BE GK and HE; implicit elsewhere.
T3. 1. Hauptsatz: energy is conserved. Innere Energie vs Enthalpie, where ΔH is the heat exchanged at constant pressure.
T4. Calorimetry: Q = m·c·ΔT, then ΔrH = −Q/n (per mol of the limiting reactant). Includes error discussion (heat loss, heat capacity of the calorimeter).
T5. Satz von Hess: enthalpy cycles and summing partial equations.
T6. ΔrH° = Σν·ΔfH°(Produkte) − Σν·ΔfH°(Edukte). ΔfH° of elements in their standard state = 0.
T7. Enthalpy diagrams: draw and interpret them.
T8. Bond breaking needs energy; bond forming releases it. Explicitly: Niedersachsen, NRW (Gitter/Solvatation) and the BE LK. Implicit in KMK S12.

### 3.3 Safe core: equilibrium
G1. Reversible reaction and closed system as preconditions. Dynamic equilibrium: v(hin) = v(rück); concentrations constant, **not equal**.
G2. Write the MWG term Kc for a given equation. Read the position from the size of K.
G3. Le Chatelier for changes in c, p and T. Only T changes K.
G4. Catalyst: equilibrium arrives faster, position unchanged.
G5. Technical application: Haber-Bosch, trading off yield against rate.

Partial core: **calculating Kc from given equilibrium concentrations**. Present at gA in NRW, BW (implicit), BE (Δν = 0) and Hessen (Δν = 0), but **eA-only in Niedersachsen**. Tag it `level:gA` with a Niedersachsen exception flag.

### 3.4 Safe core: organic nomenclature and functional groups
O1. Homologe Reihe of the Alkane (C1–C10 stems: Meth-, Eth-, Prop-, But-, Pent-, Hex-, Hept-, Oct-, Non-, Dec-). General formula CnH2n+2. Trends in boiling point and solubility.
O2. IUPAC naming of branched Alkane:
   - longest chain = Hauptkette
   - Lokanten as low as possible (first point of difference)
   - Substituenten in alphabetical order, with multiplying prefixes di/tri/tetra
O3. Functional groups and classes:
   - C=C / C≡C → Alkene / Alkine
   - Hydroxy → Alkanole (-ol)
   - Carbonyl: Aldehyd (-al) / Keto (-on)
   - Carboxy → Carbonsäuren/Alkansäuren (-säure)
   - Ester (-ylester / -oat)
   - Amino → Amine (in Q/gA per KMK)
O4. Formula types: Summenformel, Struktur-/Lewis-Formel, Halbstrukturformel, Skelettformel.
O5. Konstitutions- (Struktur-) isomerie: generate isomers from a molecular formula. Explain property differences through intermolecular forces (London/Van-der-Waals, Dipol-Dipol, H-Brücken).
O6. Primary/secondary/tertiary alcohols and their oxidation products (with Oxidationszahlen).
O7. Mechanisms at gA: radikalische Substitution (Start/Kette/Abbruch, homolytic cleavage) and elektrophile Addition (Br2 + alkene, Bromwasser test).

Accept both German name conventions (verified usage in the documents):
- "Propan-2-ol" (Hessen style) and "2-Propanol"
- "Ethansäureethylester" (BW) and "Ethylethanoat", plus trivial names (Niedersachsen: "benennen Ester mit ihrem Trivialnamen")
- Konstitutionsisomerie (NRW/HE) = Strukturisomerie (Niedersachsen/BE)

### 3.5 Advanced-only extras (tag eA/LK)
- Entropy:
  - microstate definition (BW LF)
  - ΔrS° = ΣS°(P) − ΣS°(E) (BE LK, HE LK)
  - 2. Hauptsatz
- Gibbs energy:
  - ΔG = ΔH − T·ΔS
  - four-quadrant sign reasoning
  - Grenztemperatur T = ΔH/ΔS (BE LK)
  - ΔrG° from ΔfG° (BE LK)
  - exergonisch/endergonisch; metastabil (BW LF)
  - ΔG and cell voltage (NRW LK, "freie Enthalpie bei Redoxreaktionen")
- Rate law v = k·c and quantitative rate (BE LK). Autokatalyse (BE LK, HE LK). Surface catalysis steps (BE LK).
- Kc and equilibrium-concentration calculations including quadratics (HE LK, Niedersachsen eA, BW LF). Deriving the MWG from rates (BE LK). Löslichkeitsprodukt.
- SN1/SN2, SE, ester mechanism (except in Hessen, where it is gA), radikalische Polymerisation, Mesomerie/Aromaten, Chiralität (except in BW, where it is Basisfach).
- **In no curriculum checked:** reaction order and integrated rate laws, half-life, Arrhenius equation, Maxwell–Boltzmann distribution (textbook enrichment only; **unverified**).

## 4. Misconceptions for game distractors

Status key: **[V]** = seen in the source cited. **[L]** = from international literature that I verified exists (title, venue and year), with the finding as summarised in abstracts or search snippets. **[U]** = practitioner knowledge, no source located.

### Kinetics and catalysis
- **M1 [V]** "A catalyst shifts the equilibrium / raises the yield."
  Blume: "Ein Katalysator verschiebt keine Gleichgewichte, sondern stellt sie nur rascher ein" (https://www.chemieunterricht.de/dc2/katalyse/k-gg.htm). The Göttingen material says "Oft entsteht die Fehlvorstellung, dass Katalysatoren das Gleichgewicht verschieben" (V11-149). Literature: Hackling & Garnett 1985, *Eur. J. Sci. Educ.* 7(2), 205–214, https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/abs/10.1080/0140528850070211 [L].
- **M2 [L]** "A catalyst speeds up only the forward reaction." Hackling & Garnett 1985. A German summary gives about 17 % **[U: percentage source not found]**.
- **M3 [V/U]** "A catalyst does not take part in the reaction."
  The correct version is that it takes part via intermediates and is regenerated. Göttingen V11-149 states it correctly on p. 1 but also contains "…ohne selbst an der Reaktion teilzunehmen", so teaching material itself carries the slip. It also says more catalyst means "größer ist der Stoffumsatz" (more conversion), which mixes up rate and yield.
- **M4 [U]** "A catalyst supplies the activation energy" or "lowers ΔH".
- **M5 [V]** "Activation energy = the energy an endothermic reaction needs." BW Sek I explicitly makes students tell starting energy apart from endothermic energy demand.
- **M6 [L]** "Raising the temperature speeds up only endothermic reactions" or "slows down exothermic reactions".
  This is Le Chatelier transferred onto rate. Çakmakçı, Bunce & Sanger 2010, *J. Chem. Educ.* 87(4), 449–455, https://pubs.acs.org/jceda8/article-abstract/87/4/449/247528 (abstract: misconceptions persist into undergraduate study and are tied to gaps in thermodynamics and equilibrium). Specific misconception wording **[U]**.
- **M7 [U]** "Higher temperature → faster only because of more collisions." The correct reason is mainly that a larger fraction of collisions exceeds the minimum energy.
- **M8 [U]** "Every collision leads to reaction."
- **M9 [U]** "Rate is constant throughout the reaction" or "the rate constant equals the rate".
- **M10 [U]** "A more exothermic reaction (or a larger K, or a more negative ΔG) is faster." This confuses thermodynamics with kinetics.

### Equilibrium
- **M11 [V]** "At equilibrium c(Edukte) = c(Produkte)."
  GDCh 2008 describes this student error: "bei Gleichgewichten seien 'gleiche Stoffmengen' von Edukten und Produkten vorhanden". A related error there is "die Gesamtstoffmenge ... müsse ... erhalten bleiben". https://archiv.gdch.de/fileadmin/downloads/Netzwerk_und_Strukturen/Fachgruppen/Chemieunterricht/diag.pdf
- **M12 [U/L]** "At equilibrium the reactions stop" (static picture). KMK S15 targets exactly this. A German summary cites about 45 % **[percentage U]**.
- **M13 [U]** "The forward reaction must finish before the reverse one starts" (about 32 % **[U]**).
- **M14 [V]** "Changing pressure or concentration changes K." GDCh hint: K changes "mit einer einzigen Ausnahme" (temperature).
- **M15 [U]** "After adding reactant, equilibrium shifts back completely to the old concentrations." GDCh's correct answer says the constraint is never fully removed.

### Thermodynamics
- **M16 [L/V]** "Entropy = disorder."
  Lambert 2002, *J. Chem. Educ.* 79, 187, https://pubs.acs.org/doi/10.1021/ed079p187 [L]. Hessen KCGO uses "Maß für die Unordnung" [V], while BW uses microstates [V]. Use "disorder" only as a distractor for the *definition*, and be tolerant because Hessen teaches it.
- **M17 [V]** "Exothermic = spontaneous (freiwillig); endothermic reactions cannot run by themselves." This is the historical Thomsen-Berthelot principle (Blume, https://www.chemieunterricht.de/dc2/energie/triebkra.htm). NRW LK and Hessen LK explicitly target spontaneous endothermic processes.
- **M18 [L]** "Breaking bonds releases energy" / "energy is stored in bonds". Boo 1998, *J. Res. Sci. Teach.* 35(5), 569–581, https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ565757.
- **M19 [V]** "The product of an exothermic reaction contains more energy." This is a GDCh 2008 diagnostic item: "Magnesiumiodid enthält mehr innere Energie als das Gemisch" (false statement).
- **M20 [U]** "ΔG < 0 means the reaction runs fast (or immediately)." Metastable states are the counter-example (BW LF "metastabiler Zustand").
- **M21 [U]** Sign errors:
  - ΔH positive for exothermic
  - ΔfH of elements ≠ 0
  - forgetting stoichiometric coefficients in Σν·ΔfH
  - kJ vs J with T·ΔS (ΔS is in J/(mol·K)) — a high-yield calculation distractor
  - using °C instead of K in T·ΔS
- **M22 [U]** Calorimetry: using the mass of the solid instead of the solution; forgetting the sign; dividing by the wrong n.
- **M23 [U]** "Heat and temperature are the same"; "temperature is energy".

### Organic nomenclature and structure [all U: practitioner and common-error knowledge; no German empirical study located]
- **M24** Not finding the longest chain when it is drawn bent. Example: "2-Ethylbutan" instead of 3-Methylpentan; "1-Methylpropan" instead of Butan.
- **M25** Numbering from the wrong end, or minimising the **sum** of locants instead of using the first point of difference.
- **M26** Alphabetising with the multiplying prefix: "Dimethyl" sorted under d, placed before "Ethyl". Or ordering substituents by size or locant.
- **M27** Missing multiplying prefix or repeated locant ("2,2-Methylpropan", "2-Dimethylpropan").
- **M28** The main chain does not contain the functional group or C=C. The functional group or multiple bond does not get the lowest locant.
- **M29** Mixing up the -al and -on suffixes (Alkanal vs Alkanon). Placing the carbonyl of an aldehyde at C2.
- **M30** Leaving out or double-counting the carboxy C when numbering the chain of a carboxylic acid ("Propansäure" has 3 C including the COOH).
- **M31** Ester naming reversed: acid part vs alcohol part ("Ethylethanoat" vs "Methylpropanoat" confusions). German: "Ethansäureethylester".
- **M32** "Hydroxygruppe = Hydroxid-Ion, so alcohols are basic."
- **M33** Rotated or bent drawings of the same molecule counted as different isomers. cis/trans claimed for C=C with two identical substituents on one carbon.
- **M34** "Boiling breaks covalent bonds" instead of overcoming intermolecular forces (Barke 2006 covers particle and bonding misconceptions, https://link.springer.com/book/10.1007/3-540-29460-0 **[specific chapter U]**).
- **M35** Mechanism: electrophile vs nucleophile swapped; the Br⁺/Br⁻ attack order in AE reversed; homolytic vs heterolytic cleavage mixed up; radicals drawn with charges.

## 6. Tagging recommendations for the platform
1. **Level axis.** Use `gA` / `eA` (the KMK terms), with aliases GK/LK and Basisfach/Leistungsfach.
2. **Phase axis.** Use `sek1`, `ep`, `q` plus a Land-specific grade. A single "grade 11" tag is wrong for Germany:
   - Kinetics and equilibrium: NRW EF (Jg 11); BW Kursstufe (Kl. 11/12); BE Q2 (first Q year); HE Q3/Q4 (second Q year)
   - Organic naming: BW Sek I (Kl. ~10); NRW/NDS/HE EP (Jg 11)
3. **Per-Land exceptions to encode:**
   - Kc calculation is eA in Niedersachsen
   - ester mechanism is gA in Hessen
   - Chiralität is Basisfach in BW
   - SR is Sek I in BW and E-Phase in Hessen
   - v = k·c is LK only in BE
   - RGT is not named in NRW/NDS
   - entropy definition: disorder (HE) vs microstates (BW)
4. Don't build core content on reaction orders, Arrhenius or Maxwell–Boltzmann. Tag it `enrichment`.
5. Accept name variants: Propan-2-ol / 2-Propanol; Ethylethanoat / Ethansäureethylester / Essigsäureethylester; Konstitutions-/Strukturisomerie; Carboxy-/Carboxylgruppe.
