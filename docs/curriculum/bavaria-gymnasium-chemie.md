# Bavarian Gymnasium chemistry curriculum (LehrplanPLUS, G9): upper years, organic chemistry, kinetics, thermodynamics

**Purpose:** the reference curriculum for the organic chemistry and energetics games planned in
[`../ORGANIC_GAMES_FRAMEWORK.md`](../ORGANIC_GAMES_FRAMEWORK.md). Bavaria is the pilot German
curriculum; the other Länder are compared in [`germany-sek2-overview.md`](./germany-sek2-overview.md).
**Research date:** 24 Sept 2026 (desk research by an agent, from primary sources).
**Review status:** not yet checked by a teacher who teaches in Bavaria. > YOU DECIDE: who reviews it.

Primary sources: lehrplanplus.bayern.de (ISB), gesetze-bayern.de (GSO), ISB Kontaktbriefe Chemie 2024/2025/2026, the ISB explanatory PDF for the Abitur, IQB and KMK documents. Full URL list at the end. Anything not confirmed from a primary source is marked **(unverified)**.

## What this means for chem-games (summary)

- **"Year 10–12" is not one band in Bavaria.** Organic basics (nomenclature, isomers,
  functional groups, intermolecular forces) are taught in **Jgst. 9 NTG / Jgst. 10 for all
  other tracks**. Energetics with enthalpy, kinetics, equilibrium and the first mechanisms are
  taught in **Jgst. 12**. Jgst. 11 has chemistry for NTG only (food chemistry, pharmacy). A
  game therefore has to be tagged with a *Lernbereich*, not a year.
- **Two courses in the Oberstufe:** gA (3 h/week) and eA (Leistungsfach, about 5 h/week). Several
  topics are eA only: nucleophilic substitution, aromatics, quantitative Gibbs–Helmholtz,
  solubility product, Nernst. Games must be able to hide eA content.
- **Vocabulary depends on the year.** Jgst. 9–10 say *Reaktionsenergie*, never *Enthalpie* or
  ΔH; Jgst. 12 introduces *Reaktionsenthalpie*. Use E/Z, not cis/trans; *Kohlenstoffdioxid*;
  *K*S/p*K*S; and always keep *Stoffebene* and *Teilchenebene* apart ("Ethanol" vs
  "Ethanol-Molekül").
- **Nomenclature is deliberately shallow.** The ISB says it must not become an end in itself
  and should use simple molecules in context. That argues against a 6-level naming marathon and
  for naming embedded in other tasks.
- **Kinetics in 12 LB6 includes Maxwell–Boltzmann but no rate law.** Maxwell–Boltzmann is in
  scope in Bavaria but not in the other Länder checked. The rate law, reaction order and
  Arrhenius are out of scope everywhere except the Berlin LK.

## 0. Timeline: what applies in Sept 2026

A common assumption is that the first G9 cohort reached Jgst. 11 in 2024/25. **That is one year too late.**

- ISB Kontaktbrief Chemie 2024: the first G9 cohort entered the *Profil- und Leistungsstufe* (PuLSt, Jgst. 12) in school year **2024/25**. So it was in Jgst. 11 in **2023/24**, when the *Wissenschaftswoche* and the new P-Seminar ran "for the first time in their current form" (the letter says this about "the last school year").
- ISB Kontaktbrief 2025: LehrplanPLUS for Jgst. 13 was taught for the first time in **2025/26**.
- KM press release (4 Mar 2026) and ISB Kontaktbrief 2026: the **first G9 Abitur was sat in spring 2026**, by about 30,000 students ("erster Abiturjahrgang 2024–2026"). Bavaria had **no regular Abitur in 2025**. The last G8 Abitur was in 2024 (Wikipedia, secondary source).
- **Today (2026/27):** every year from 5 to 13 is on the G9 LehrplanPLUS. The current Jgst. 13 is the second G9 cohort, and the 2026 Abitur has already been sat under the new format. The ISB Kontaktbrief 2026 (Sept 2026) announces **no curriculum changes**, only new support materials. It also notes that LehrplanPLUS gets a website redesign from 2026/27 (navigation, not content).
- From 2026, Bavaria draws its Chemistry Abitur from the **joint federal-state task pool (ländergemeinsamer Aufgabenpool, IQB)**, choosing tasks that fit the Bavarian syllabus.

**Mapping "year 10–12":**
- Jgst. 10 is the end of Mittelstufe for all tracks.
- Jgst. 11 is the *Einführungsphase*. Only NTG students have Chemistry that year.
- Jgst. 12 and 13 are the *Qualifikationsphase* (called *Profil- und Leistungsstufe*). Chemistry runs at gA or eA level.

**Most organic-chemistry fundamentals sit in Jgst. 9 (NTG) or Jgst. 10 (other tracks).** Reaction mechanisms, energetics, kinetics and equilibrium are in Jgst. 12. Acid–base equilibria, biomolecules, polymers and dyes are in Jgst. 13.

## 1. Structure

### 1.1 Weekly hours, Jgst. 5–11 (GSO Anlage 1, version valid from 01.08.2026)

| Jgst. | NTG | HG / SG / MuG / WWG / SWG (all non-NTG) |
|---|---|---|
| 8 | 2 (+ Profilstunden) | none |
| 9 | 2 (+ Profilstunden) | 2 |
| 10 | 2 (+ Profilstunden) | 3 |
| 11 | 2 | none (Chemistry is not taught) |

- Track abbreviations: NTG = naturwissenschaftlich-technologisch; HG = humanistisch; SG = sprachlich; MuG = musisch; WWG / SWG = wirtschafts- / sozialwissenschaftlich (the WSG branches).
- **NTG Profilstunden:** 2 per week in Jgst. 8, 9 and 10, and 1 in Jgst. 11. They are used "zur Stärkung von Chemie und Physik" (GSO footnote). The ISB says classes should be split for them, with the student experiment at the centre.
- The Lehrplan hour totals show the practical split: NTG 8, 9 and 10 each total about **84 h** per year, of which about **28 h are "Profil"** content marked separately. That is roughly 2 regular hours plus 1 profile hour per week for Chemistry. The per-subject split of Profilstunden is not in the GSO text; it is inferred from the Lehrplan totals **(inference)**.
- Non-NTG hours: Jgst. 9 totals about 56 h (2 h/week); Jgst. 10 totals about 84 h (3 h/week). NTG Jgst. 11 totals about 54 h.
- **NTG students start Chemistry in Jgst. 8.** All other tracks start in Jgst. 9. The same content is therefore shifted by one year: NTG does it earlier and in more depth, and adds Profil experiments.

### 1.2 Oberstufe, Jgst. 12–13 (Qualifikationsphase / PuLSt)

- **gA level (grundlegendes Anforderungsniveau):** Chemistry is 3 h/week (GSO Anlage 3 lists it under MINT/Naturwissenschaften).
- **eA level (erhöhtes Anforderungsniveau):** only if Chemistry is chosen as the student's individual **Leistungsfach**. The subject then gets "zwei Wochenstunden mehr", i.e. **5 h/week**. It is a separate course with its own Lehrplan (gymnasiale-oberstufe.bayern.de).
- Deutsch and Mathematik are compulsory at eA for everyone. Students then choose one more Leistungsfach, which can be a natural science. Choosing a Leistungsfach commits the student to an Abitur exam in it.
- The Abitur has five subjects: three written, two oral (Wikipedia, secondary source).
- **LehrplanPLUS has separate Fachlehrpläne** for 12 gA, 12 eA, 13 gA and 13 eA. They share the same Lernbereich titles. eA has more hours per Lernbereich plus extra content (see section 2).
- The KMK 2020 standards require gA to have 2–3 h/week and eA at least 4 h/week.

### 1.3 The written Chemistry Abitur (ISB Erläuterungen, Sept 2023)

- **gA:** 255 min. Students pick 3 of 4 tasks. 30 BE per task, 90 BE in total.
- **eA:** 300 min. Students pick 3 of 4 tasks. 40 BE per task, 120 BE in total.
- Tasks are built on materials (M1, M2 …) inside one context, with 4–6 subtasks each.
- Each task covers the three *Basiskonzepte* and all four competence areas. **Bewertungskompetenz is weighted more heavily than in the old Abitur.**
- Each task focuses on one KMK *Inhaltsbereich*: *Stoffe, Strukturen, Eigenschaften*; *Chemische Reaktionen*; *Arbeitsweisen*; *Lebenswelt und Gesellschaft*.
- How gA and eA are differentiated (ISB examples):
  - gA only plugs given values into the MWG; eA must first calculate the equilibrium concentrations.
  - gA gets split operators ("beschreiben" + "ableiten") where eA gets "interpretieren".
  - gA is given the evaluation criteria; eA must choose its own.
  - eA materials are more complex.
- **Allowed aids:** only a StMUK-approved maths/science *Formelsammlung*. From 2025/26, **only the periodic table printed in that Formelsammlung** is allowed; fold-out periodic tables and the PDF version are not. The ISB 2026 letter reports an erratum in the Westermann Formelsammlung: Ni is printed as 55.693 u instead of 58.693 u.

## 2. Content by Jahrgangsstufe

Every year starts with **Lernbereich 1, "Wie Chemiker denken und arbeiten"** (in 12/13: "Wie Chemikerinnen und Chemiker denken und arbeiten"). It is compulsory, has no hours of its own and runs across the other Lernbereiche. It covers process skills: safety and *Gefahrstoffkennzeichnung*, the scientific method, models, formula language and representations, source criticism and evaluation.

Hours are approximate ("ca.") as stated in the Lehrplan.

### Jgst. 8: NTG only (first year of Chemistry)

1. **LB2** *Stoffe und ihre Eigenschaften – Von beobachtbaren Stoffeigenschaften zum Teilchenmodell*: about 23 h (8 Profil).
2. **LB3** *Chemische Reaktion – Vom Teilchenmodell zum Daltonschen Atommodell*: about 36 h (12 Profil). Includes energy change in reactions, the periodic table, mass calculations, and evaluating energy sources.
3. **LB4** *Chemische Verbindungen und ihre Eigenschaften – Vom Daltonschen Atommodell zum Kern-Hülle-Modell*: about 25 h (8 Profil).

### Jgst. 9, non-NTG (HG, SG, MuG, WWG, SWG): first year of Chemistry, about 56 h

1. **LB2** *Stoffe und ihre Eigenschaften – … zum Teilchenmodell* (about 10 h):
   - *Kenneigenschaften* (defining properties: melting point, boiling point, density)
   - separating mixtures (*Destillation*, *Extraktion*)
   - gas tests: *Glimmspanprobe*, *Kalkwasserprobe*, *Knallgasprobe*
2. **LB3** *Chemische Reaktion – … zum Daltonschen Atommodell* (about 20 h). **The first energetics and kinetics seeds are here:**
   - *Reaktionsenergie*: heat, work, radiation; *exotherm/endotherm*
   - **Aktivierung chemischer Reaktionen, Katalyse**, with energy diagrams, "auch unter Betrachtung katalysierter Reaktionen"
   - conservation of mass, Dalton's model, *Avogadro-Hypothese*
   - *Stoffmenge*, *molare Masse*, *molares Volumen*, *Größengleichungen*
   - **combustion of simple hydrocarbons, molecular formulas and names of the Alkane**
   - carbon cycle, CO₂ balance
3. **LB4** *Chemische Verbindungen … zum Kern-Hülle-Modell* (about 11 h): Rutherford, the *Elektronengasmodell* for metals, ionic bonding, *Verhältnisformeln* of binary salts, names such as *Blei(IV)-oxid*.
4. **LB5** *Atombau und gekürztes Periodensystem* (about 6 h): *Energiestufenmodell*, *Ionisierungsenergie*, *Edelgaskonfiguration*.
5. **LB6** *Donator-Akzeptor-Konzept – Elektronenübergänge* (about 9 h):
   - electrolysis as a forced redox reaction
   - salt formation as exothermic ("nur Reaktionsenergie", i.e. no enthalpy yet), with lattice energy as the driving force
   - oxidation = loss of electrons, reduction = gain of electrons; *Reduktionsmittel*/*Oxidationsmittel*
   - reversibility (e.g. *Zink-Iod-Batterie*)

### Jgst. 9 NTG, about 84 h (28 Profil). This is where organic chemistry starts for NTG

1. **LB2** *Atombau und gekürztes Periodensystem* (about 9 h, 3 Profil). Profil topics: flame colours, line spectra, radioactivity.
2. **LB3** *Donator-Akzeptor-Konzept – Elektronenübergänge* (about 18 h, 7 Profil). Profil topics: *Redoxreihe der Metallatome*, extracting metals by electrolysis.
3. **LB4** *Moleküle – Mit dem einfachen Orbitalmodell zum Elektronenpaarabstoßungsmodell* (about 21 h, 6 Profil):
   - *Valenzstrichschreibweise*, a simple orbital model, *Bindigkeit*, *Formalladung*
   - *Elektronenpaarabstoßungsmodell* (EPA model = VSEPR), *Mesomerie-Modell*
   - **branched and cyclic hydrocarbons, *Konstitutionsisomerie*, nomenclature**
   - **Alkene and Alkine molecules, *E/Z-Isomerie***
4. **LB5** *Wechselwirkungskonzept – Anziehung zwischen Teilchen* (about 36 h, 12 Profil):
   - electronegativity, *Partialladung*, bond and molecule polarity
   - **functional groups and IUPAC nomenclature for Alkohole, Aldehyde, Ketone, Carbonsäuren**
   - **intermolecular forces:** *Van-der-Waals-Wechselwirkungen* as the umbrella term for *London-Dispersionswechselwirkungen* and *Dipol-Dipol-Wechselwirkungen*; *Wasserstoffbrücken*
   - properties explained by these forces: *Viskosität*, *Siedetemperatur*, solubility
   - petroleum products (*Erdölprodukte*), the anomalies of water, *Hydratation*

### Jgst. 10, non-NTG: about 84 h. **The main organic-chemistry year for HG/SG/MuG/WSG**

1. **LB1** (cross-cutting). Content includes:
   - oxidation numbers as a model
   - the formula representations (quoted verbatim): "*Formeldarstellungen (Valenzstrichformel, Keilstrichformel, Strukturformel, Halbstrukturformel, Skelettformel, Projektionsformel) bei organischen Molekülen*"
   - preparing solutions of defined concentration
2. **LB2** *Moleküle – Mit dem einfachen Orbitalmodell zum Elektronenpaarabstoßungsmodell* (about 15 h):
   - EPA model, bond angles, *Mesomerie-Modell*
   - quoted: "verzweigte Kohlenwasserstoff-Moleküle: Konstitutionsisomerie, Nomenklatur"
   - quoted: "einfache Alken- und Alkin-Moleküle: Doppel- und Dreifachbindung, E/Z-Isomerie, Nomenklatur"
   - writing structural formulas from systematic names and converting between formula types
3. **LB3** *Wechselwirkungskonzept – Anziehung zwischen Teilchen* (about 20 h):
   - electronegativity and polarity
   - quoted: "funktionelle Gruppen und Nomenklatur bei Alkohol-, Aldehyd-, Keton-, Carbonsäure-Molekülen"
   - *Van-der-Waals*, *London-Dispersions-*, *Dipol-Dipol-* and *Ion-Dipol-Wechselwirkungen*, *Wasserstoffbrücken*
   - *Viskosität*, *Siedetemperatur*, *Löslichkeit*
   - petroleum products and their environmental impact; water
4. **LB4** *Donator-Akzeptor-Konzept und Reversibilität chemischer Reaktionen bei Protonenübergängen* (about 18 h):
   - indicators, the **pH scale**
   - Brønsted acid/base (spelled "*Brönsted*" in the Lehrplan), *Ampholyt*
   - acidity from bond polarity and **Mesomeriestabilisierung**; **comparing the acidity of alcohols and carboxylic acids**
   - reversibility (*Gleichgewichtspfeil*)
   - *Stoffmengenkonzentration* calculations, neutralisation, *Antazida*
   - simple titration and the *Äquivalenzpunkt*
5. **LB5** *Donator-Akzeptor-Konzept und Reversibilität … bei Redoxreaktionen in wässriger Lösung* (about 18 h):
   - **Oxidationszahlen in inorganic and organic particles**, rules for *Redoxteilgleichungen*
   - **the redox series of alcohols:** oxidability of primary, secondary and tertiary alcohols to Aldehyde, Ketone and Carbonsäuren, using permanganate
   - tests for aldehydes: *Fehling-Probe*, *Silberspiegel-Probe* (Tollens), *Schiffsche Probe*
   - ethanol: production, health risks, addiction
   - *Brennstoffzelle* (fuel cell)
6. **LB6** *Donator-Akzeptor-Konzept und Reversibilität bei Nukleophil-Elektrophil-Reaktionen* (about 13 h):
   - sugars: Glucose, Fructose, Saccharose; ring closure as **nucleophilic addition**; *Keto-Enol-Tautomerie*; Saccharose as a *Vollacetal*
   - **mechanism of acid-catalysed ester condensation (*säurekatalysierte Esterkondensation*) and base-induced ester hydrolysis (*baseninduzierte Esterhydrolyse*)**
   - the *Peptidbindung*; ester properties and uses; fats identified as esters

### Jgst. 10 NTG: about 84 h (28 Profil)

The NTG year has three content Lernbereiche, each about 28 h (9–10 of them Profil):

1. **LB2, proton transfer:** as non-NTG LB4. Profil adds conductivity, fountain experiments, and a **calorimetric determination of the heat of neutralisation**.
2. **LB3, redox (*Elektronenübergänge: Redoxreaktionen*):** metals with O₂, Cl₂, CO₂ and H₂O; oxidation numbers; **oxidation of alcohols with various oxidising agents**; aldehyde tests; ethanol and methanol as industrial chemicals; fermentation (Profil); fuel cells; solar hydrogen.
3. **LB4, nucleophile–electrophile reactions:** ester condensation and hydrolysis mechanism, peptide bond, esters as flavourings (Profil).
   - **Fats**, quoted: "Carbonsäureester aus Propan-1,2,3-triol und Fettsäuren", with the *Z-Konfiguration* of unsaturated fatty acids and melting range.
   - **Soaps and tensides** (*Seifen/Tenside*): amphiphilic character, detergents.
   - Sugars, as in non-NTG.

### Jgst. 11: NTG only, about 54 h (Einführungsphase; the non-NTG tracks have no Chemistry)

The page is titled "Chemie 11 (NTG)".

1. **LB2** *Lebensmittelchemie* (about 34 h):
   - macronutrients with their characteristic functional groups
   - analysis: colour reactions, quick tests, acid–base and redox titration
   - **thin-layer chromatography (DC), Rf value**
   - *optische Aktivität*: polarimeter, *spezifischer Drehwinkel*, *Biot-Gesetz*
   - **chirality:** *asymmetrisches Kohlenstoff-Atom*, *Chiralitätszentrum*, *Fischer-Projektion*, *D-/L-Nomenklatur*, *Enantiomere*, *Diastereomere*
   - **carbohydrates:** Glucose, Fructose and Galactose, the anomeric equilibrium, the *Haworth-Projektion*, the *glykosidische Bindung*, disaccharides, polysaccharides (starch, glycogen, cellulose)
   - **fats** (energy storage, essential fatty acids)
   - **proteins** built from "L-2-Aminocarbonsäure" units, the peptide bond
   - enzymes: *Schlüssel-Schloss-Prinzip*, enzymatic hydrolysis, lactose intolerance
   - hydrophilic, lipophilic and amphiphilic substances
2. **LB3** *Pharmazie* (about 20 h):
   - **3.1** *Arzneimittel im Verdauungssystem* (about 8 h): antacids, proton-pump inhibitors
   - **3.2** *Schmerzmittel* (about 12 h): ASS (acetylsalicylic acid), Ibuprofen, Paracetamol
     - **condensation mechanism for *Carbonsäureester* and *Carbonsäureamide***
     - simple **retrosynthesis** (steps only; a mechanism is required only for ester and amide formation)
     - extraction and synthesis of ASS; purity via co-chromatography and Rf
     - COX inhibition; computer modelling of the Ibuprofen enantiomers
3. Jgst. 11 also has the cross-subject *Wissenschaftswoche*.

### Jgst. 12: gA (about 84 h of content) and eA (about 140 h of content)

| LB | Title | gA h | eA h |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2 | *Atombau* (eA: *Atombau und koordinative Bindung*) | 7 | 17 |
| 3 | *Analytik* | 8 | 30 |
| 4 | *Chemische Bindung* | 13 | 13 |
| 5 | *Kohlenwasserstoffe – Energieträger und Reaktionspartner* | 17 | 29 |
| 6 | *Reaktionsgeschwindigkeit – Ermittlung und Deutung auf Teilchenebene* | 7 | 8 |
| 7 | *Chemisches Gleichgewicht – Reversible Reaktion und dynamisches Gleichgewicht* | 14 | 18 |
| 8 | *Redoxgleichgewichte – Energetik und technische Anwendung* | 18 | 25 |

- **LB2 Atombau:**
  - emission spectra, the *Natrium-Doppellinie*
  - the four quantum numbers
  - *Aufbauprinzip*, *Pauli-Prinzip*, *Hundsche Regel*, *Kästchenschreibweise*
  - s/p/d/f blocks
  - *eA only:* complexes (coordination number, *Chelateffekt*, 18-electron rule, haem, chlorophyll)
- **LB3 Analytik:**
  - ion tests
  - **tests for functional groups:** *Bromwasser-Probe* (multiple bonds), *Cer(IV)-ammoniumnitrat-Test*, *Dinitrophenylhydrazin-Test*, *Fehling-*, *Tollens-* and *Schiffsche Probe*, *Bromthymolblau-Test*, *Rojahn-Test*
  - blank tests; *Hinweis* vs *Nachweis* (indication vs proof)
  - *eA only:* chromatography (DC, HPLC, GC), redox titration (manganometry), EDTA complexometry, photometry (*Lambert-Beer*)
- **LB4 Chemische Bindung:**
  - wave–particle duality, *Heisenbergsche Unbestimmtheitsrelation*, ψ and |ψ|²
  - the LCAO model, σ/π molecular orbitals, HOMO/LUMO, molecular modelling
  - **sp³, sp² and sp hybridisation**
  - isolated, cumulated and conjugated π-bonds; *Mesomerie* (gA)
  - all intermolecular force types; comparing classes of substances
- **LB5 Kohlenwasserstoffe: the thermodynamics block plus the first mechanisms:**
  - fossil and renewable feedstocks, Power-to-Gas, greenhouse effect
  - **thermodynamic systems** (open, closed, isolated)
  - ***Reaktionsenergie* (constant volume) vs *Reaktionsenthalpie* (constant pressure)**
  - **calorimetry**, *Brennwert*
  - **standard reaction enthalpies and calculations with the *Satz von Hess***
  - reaction enthalpy explained from bonding
  - mechanism vocabulary: **homolysis/heterolysis, radical, nucleophile, electrophile**
  - **halogenation by radical substitution (*radikalische Substitution*) and electrophilic addition (*elektrophile Addition*)**
  - halogenated hydrocarbons: persistence, environment, health
  - *eA only:*
    - **benzene and aromaticity via the mesomerism model**, *Mesomerie-Energie*, resonance structures (*Grenzstrukturen*) and choosing the most probable one
    - **inductive and mesomeric effects** explaining the acidity of alcohols vs phenol vs carboxylic acids
    - **electrophilic aromatic substitution (*elektrophile aromatische Substitution*)**
    - **nucleophilic substitution (*nukleophile Substitution*) at halogenoalkanes**
    - importance of aromatic compounds
  - ISB explicitly confirms that nucleophilic substitution is **eA only**.
- **LB6 Reaktionsgeschwindigkeit (kinetics):**
  - rate as change of amount of substance per unit time
  - **average and instantaneous rate from graphs** (*mittlere/momentane Reaktionsgeschwindigkeit*)
  - dependence on **concentration, pressure, temperature (*RGT-Regel*), *Zerteilungsgrad* (particle size/surface area), catalyst**
  - **collision theory (*Stoßtheorie*):** orientation, speed, number of particles, the **Maxwell-Boltzmann distribution**, *Mindestenergie*, **activation energy (*Aktivierungsenergie*)**
  - **homogeneous and heterogeneous catalysis**; gA also has the *Autoabgaskatalysator*
  - Not listed in either level (as fetched): **rate laws (*Geschwindigkeitsgesetz*), reaction order, rate constant k, and the Arrhenius equation.**
- **LB7 Chemisches Gleichgewicht:**
  - reversible reaction, dynamic equilibrium in a closed system; "static at substance level vs dynamic at particle level"
  - **Le Chatelier** (temperature, pressure, concentration; a catalyst does not shift the equilibrium)
  - **MWG**: *Massenwirkungsquotient Q*, *Gleichgewichtskonstante Kc*
  - Haber-Bosch; oceans and CO₂
  - gA does only "simple calculations" (plugging values in); eA calculates the equilibrium concentrations
  - *eA only:* **solubility equilibria and the solubility product *K*L**, common-ion addition, phosphate precipitation
- **LB8 Redoxgleichgewichte: electrochemistry plus the second thermodynamics block:**
  - galvanic cells, *Halbzellen*, *Zellspannung*, *Standardwasserstoffhalbzelle*, *Standardpotential*, *elektrochemische Spannungsreihe*
  - primary cells (zinc-air, alkaline/Leclanché, Li), the fuel cell
  - **1st law of thermodynamics**
  - ***T·ΔS* as "gebundene Energie"**; **entropy as a measure of how energy and particles are distributed (2nd law)**; estimating ΔS
  - **Gibbs-Helmholtz: qualitative only in gA; qualitative and quantitative in eA**, including *freie Enthalpie G*, *exergon/endergon* and predicting whether a reaction runs spontaneously (*Freiwilligkeit*)
  - electrolysis as endergonic; energetic coupling
  - secondary cells (Li-ion, lead-acid); e-mobility
  - *eA only:* the **Nernst equation**, concentration cells, applying Le Chatelier to galvanic cells

- **LB4.1 Natural macromolecules: from *Aminocarbonsäuren* to protein**

  - tests: *Ninhydrin-Nachweis*, *Biuret-Probe*, *Xanthoprotein-Reaktion*
  - condensation to peptides, **mesomerism of the peptide bond**
  - primary, secondary, tertiary and quaternary structure; denaturation
  - enzymes (*Schlüssel-Schloss*, factors affecting enzyme activity)
  - *eA only:* **chirality**: asymmetric C, enantiomers, **Fischer projection, D/L**; fibrous vs globular proteins
  - **gA 13 does not include chirality**, so gA students meet it only in Jgst. 11, and only if they were NTG
- **LB4.2 Synthetic macromolecules (*Werkstoffe nach Maß*):**
  - recycling types; *Thermoplaste*, *Elastomere*, *Duroplaste*
  - monomer, polymer, *Copolymerisate*, *Vernetzung* (cross-linking)
  - **radical polymerisation *with mechanism* (start, chain propagation, chain termination)**
  - **polycondensation (polyester, polyamide; bi- and trifunctional monomers)**
  - fibres (Nylon, PET), silicones, conductive polymers
  - *eA only:* **polyaddition (polyurethane)**; OLEDs; conductive polymers in more depth
- **LB5 Nachhaltigkeit:**
  - photosynthesis and artificial photosynthesis, water splitting, hydrogen storage (Power-to-Gas/-Liquid)
  - **corrosion** (oxygen, acid and contact corrosion), corrosion protection, *Opferanode* (sacrificial anode)
  - *eA only:* decomposition voltage, *Überpotential* (overpotential), **Faraday's laws**, chlor-alkali electrolysis, redox-flow battery, Grätzel cell, LOHC, material cycles

## 4. German nomenclature and notation conventions

Sources: the ISB supplement "Nomenklatur von organischen Molekülen" (Nov 2023, assigned to 9 NTG / 10 SG), the Lehrplan wording, and ISB letters.

### 4.1 Verified from primary sources

- **Locants go before the suffix they refer to:** **Propan-2-ol** (ISB example), **Hex-2-en-1,4-diol**, **Propan-1,2,3-triol** (Lehrplan 10 NTG). The ISB examples never use "2-Propanol".
- **Name structure:** *Präfix* (substituents) + *Stamm* (number of C atoms) + *Suffix* (compound family).
- **Priority order** (highest first): Carbonsäure (*-säure*, prefix *Carboxy-*) > Aldehyd (*-al*, prefix *Formyl-*) > Keton (*-on*, prefix *Oxo-*) > Alkohol (*-ol*, prefix *Hydroxy-*) > Alken (*-en*) > Alkin (*-in*) > halogen compounds (prefixes *Fluor-, Chlor-, Brom-, Iod-*, ordered alphabetically like alkyl side chains) > Alkan (*-an*, prefix *Alkyl-*).
  - The ending *-carbonsäure* is used only when there are three or more carboxy groups.
  - ISB examples: "3-Oxopentanal (not 1-Formylbutan-2-on)" and "Hex-2-en-1,4-diol (not 1,4-Dihydroxyhex-2-en)".
- **Spelling:** German forms *Brom*, *Iod*, *Fluor* (not Bromo/Iodo), and *Propanon*, *Butanon*, *Ethanal*, *Butansäure*.
- **Trivial names are shown alongside systematic names** in the ISB table: *Isopropanol* (Propan-2-ol), *Acetaldehyd* (Ethanal), *Aceton* (Propanon), *Methylethylketon/MEK* (Butanon), *Buttersäure* (Butansäure). The Lehrplan itself uses trivial names for everyday or biological substances: *Essig-*, *Glucose*, *Saccharose*, *Acetylsalicylsäure*, *Ibuprofen*, *Paracetamol*, *Acetat-Puffer*, *Polyethylenterephthalat*.
- **Nomenclature depth is limited on purpose.** ISB says only basic rules are taught, on simple examples. Nomenclature tasks "dürfen … nicht zum Selbstzweck werden": they must be set in a context and used in moderation in assessments. Multi-functional monsters are explicitly "nicht einfach".
- **Formula types** (Lehrplan 10, LB1 Inhalte): *Valenzstrichformel* (Lewis), *Keilstrichformel* (wedge-dash), *Strukturformel*, *Halbstrukturformel* (condensed), *Skelettformel* (skeletal), *Projektionsformel* (*Fischer-* and *Haworth-Projektionsformel*, named in 11).
- **Isomer terms:** *Konstitutionsisomerie*, ***E/Z-Isomerie*** (E/Z, not cis/trans; the Lehrplan also uses *Z-Konfiguration* for unsaturated fatty acids), *Enantiomere*, *Diastereomere*, *asymmetrisches Kohlenstoff-Atom*, *Chiralitätszentrum*, *D-/L-*.
- **Substance level vs particle level (*Stoffebene* vs *Teilchenebene*) is kept strictly apart.** The Lehrplan writes *Alkohol-Moleküle*, *Carbonsäure-Moleküle*, *Wasser-Moleküle*, *Kohlenstoffdioxid-Moleküle*, *Oxonium-Ionen*, *Hydroxid-Ionen*, *Atom-Ionen*, *Kohlenstoff-Atom* (hyphenated compounds). A substance name ("Ethanol") and its particle ("Ethanol-Molekül") should not be mixed up. The KMK 2020 standards say the same: "Phänomene auf der Stoffebene und deren Deutung auf der Teilchenebene konsequent unterschieden".
- **Other preferred terms:**
  - ***Kohlenstoffdioxid*** (not Kohlendioxid)
  - ***Oxonium-Ion***
  - ***Elektronenpaarabstoßungsmodell*** (EPA model, not "VSEPR")
  - ***Elektronenpaarbindung***
  - ***Wasserstoffbrücken***
  - ***London-Dispersionswechselwirkungen***, with ***Van-der-Waals-Wechselwirkungen* as the umbrella term**
  - ***Aminocarbonsäure*** / *2-Aminocarbonsäure* / *L-2-Aminocarbonsäure* (not plain "Aminosäure")
  - *Silberspiegel-Probe*, *Fehling-Probe*, *Schiffsche Probe*
  - *Säure-Base-Titration*, *Äquivalenzpunkt*, *Halbtitrationspunkt*
  - *RGT-Regel*, *Zerteilungsgrad*
  - *Massenwirkungsgesetz (MWG)*, *Massenwirkungsquotient Q*, *Kc*, *K*L
  - *KS/pKS* (subscript S for *Säure*, not Ka)
  - *freie Enthalpie G*, *exergon/endergon*
  - *Reaktionsenthalpie* Δ*r*H°; the ISB Abitur example writes "Standardreaktionsenthalpie ΔrH°"
- **"Energie" vs "Enthalpie":** Mittelstufe uses ***Reaktionsenergie*** only (Jgst. 9: "nur Reaktionsenergie"). *Enthalpie* is introduced formally in 12 LB5, where it is explicitly distinguished from *Reaktionsenergie*. The ISB published a note on using the two terms (NTG 9.3–9.5 / non-NTG 10.2–10.3). **Games for Jgst. 9–10 should say "Reaktionsenergie", "exotherm/endotherm" and "Aktivierungsenergie", and not ΔH.**
- The Lehrplan writes **"Brönsted"** (with ö). It spells the mechanism terms inconsistently: *nucleophile Addition* in 10 LB6, but *Nukleophil*, *nukleophile Substitution* and *Nukleophil-Elektrophil-Reaktion* elsewhere.
- **Oxidation numbers** (*Oxidationszahlen*) are a model/tool ("Hilfsmittel") in Jgst. 10, applied to both inorganic and organic particles.

### 4.2 Conventions from general German IUPAC practice, not found stated in Bavarian sources **(unverified for Bavaria)**

- ***Ethen*, *Propen*, *Ethin*** are the systematic names. *Ethylen*/*Acetylen* are trivial names; they appear only inside polymer names such as *Polyethylen* or *PET*. The Lehrplan text I fetched does not name Ethen/Ethylen explicitly.
- *Ethansäure* (systematic) vs *Essigsäure* (trivial), and *Methanal* vs *Formaldehyd*: consistent with the ISB approach of giving both, but these specific pairs are not in the ISB table.
- Oxidation numbers are conventionally written as **Roman numerals above the element symbol** in German schoolbooks (e.g. +I, −II); this is not specified in the Lehrplan text.
- Carboxylate/ester naming (*Ethylethanoat* vs *Essigsäureethylester*): the Lehrplan says "Carbonsäureester" but I found no naming rule for esters. Both forms are common in German textbooks.

## 5. Kompetenzbereiche, Basiskonzepte, Operatoren

### 5.1 Kompetenzbereiche (Fachprofil Chemie; KMK 2020)

Bavaria (and KMK 2020) use **Sachkompetenz**, **Erkenntnisgewinnungskompetenz**, **Kommunikationskompetenz** and **Bewertungskompetenz**.

- The term "**Fachwissen**" (sometimes still used) is the old KMK 2004 Mittlerer-Schulabschluss label. Current Bavarian documents say ***Sachkompetenz***.
- In 12/13, LB1 is split into 1.1–1.4 by these four areas.
- **Basiskonzepte:**
  1. *Aufbau und Eigenschaften der Stoffe und ihrer Teilchen*
  2. *Chemische Reaktion* (donor–acceptor principles, reaction mechanisms, reversibility, equilibrium)
  3. *Energiekonzept* (thermodynamic and kinetic aspects: energy forms, activation energy/catalysis, reaction kinetics, enthalpy/entropy)
- The ISB lessons use **"Donator-Akzeptor-Konzept"** as the organising idea: proton transfer, electron transfer, and nucleophile–electrophile reactions all appear in Lernbereich titles.
- **Anforderungsbereiche** (KMK):
  - I: reproducing and describing in a learned context
  - II: independently selecting, arranging, explaining and transferring to comparable new contexts
  - III: complex problems, independent solutions, evaluation and reflection
  - The weighting is centred on AFB II, and AFB I should outweigh AFB III.

### 5.2 Operatoren (IQB "Grundstock von Operatoren", joint Abitur pool for Biologie/Chemie/Physik, Stand 31.03.2022)

This list is used for the Bavarian Abitur tasks, which come from the pool. The ISB says "folgende Operatorenliste" applies. Definitions below are paraphrased.

| Operator | Meaning (paraphrase) |
|---|---|
| *ableiten* | draw sound conclusions from findings or data |
| *abschätzen* | give magnitudes based on reasoned considerations |
| *analysieren* | extract the important parts, properties or relationships for a question; in Chemistry also: test a situation experimentally |
| *aufstellen, formulieren* | develop chemical formulas, equations (word or formula) or reaction mechanisms |
| *Hypothesen aufstellen* | state a scientifically justified conjecture |

| *berechnen* | show the calculation, starting from an approach |
| *beschreiben* | set out observations, structures or procedures in a structured way, in technical language |
| *beurteilen* | factual judgement (*Sachurteil*) justified by subject criteria |
| *bewerten* | value judgement (*Werturteil*) justified with reference to societal values and norms |
| *darstellen* | present in a structured way, also with drawings or tables |
| *diskutieren* | set arguments against each other and weigh them |

- Operators not on the list may be used when their plain-language meaning is clear (e.g. "durchführen").
